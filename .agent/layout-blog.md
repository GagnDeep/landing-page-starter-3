# Blog Page Layout Blueprint

1. **Global Header:** Standard navigation.
2. **Hero Section (The Aesthetic Journal):** Editorial-style header, very magazine-like layout.
3. **Featured Article:** Large, full-width or asymmetrical block highlighting the most recent or important post.
4. **Category Filter:** Horizontal list of topics (e.g., Bridal Prep, Skincare, Dental Health, Trends).
5. **Latest Posts Grid (Row 1):** 3-column grid of recent articles.
6. **Latest Posts Grid (Row 2):** Continuation of grid.
7. **Newsletter Insert:** In-line block breaking the grid, asking readers to subscribe for updates.
8. **Editor's Picks (Row 1):** 2-column layout for longer, in-depth articles.
9. **Editor's Picks (Row 2):** Continuation.
10. **Video Content Highlight:** A section specifically for vlog-style or treatment walkthrough videos.
11. **Author Spotlight (Dr. Arshpreet):** Small bio block linking to all articles written by the founder.
12. **Guest Contributors:** Mention of any collaborating dermatologists or makeup artists.
13. **Trending Topics:** A tag cloud or simple list of the most searched terms on the blog.
14. **Archive Dropdown/List:** Access to older posts by month/year.
15. **Pagination/Load More:** Elegant button or infinite scroll trigger.
16. **Social Sharing Prompt:** Encouragement to follow on Pinterest/Instagram for visual inspiration.
17. **Clinic News:** A dedicated small section for operational updates (new machines, awards).
18. **Consultation CTA:** Soft reminder to book a session based on the readings.
19. **Global Footer:** Standard footer.
20. **Sub-footer:** Copyright.
