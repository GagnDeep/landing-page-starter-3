# Layout Documentation: Blog/Editorial Page

## Structural Intent
The blog is positioned as an "Editorial" or "Journal", matching the magazine aesthetic. It relies on strong typography, ample white space, and high-quality imagery.

## Section Details

### 1. Featured Article (Hero)
- **Layout:** Large hero image with overlapping or adjacent large typography. Represents the latest or most important post.
- **Rhythm:** Dominant, magazine cover style.
- **Intent:** Hook the reader immediately with premium content.

### 2. Category Filter
- **Layout:** A horizontal, horizontally scrollable row of elegant pill buttons or text links.
- **Rhythm:** Subtle, navigational.
- **Intent:** Easy sorting (e.g., Trends, Maintenance, Academy).

### 3. Article Grid
- **Layout:** A responsive masonry or standard 2/3 column grid. Cards have minimal borders, relying on image and typography hierarchy.
- **Rhythm:** Consistent visual cadence, easy scanning.
- **Intent:** Browseable archive of expertise.

### 4. Newsletter Signup / Insider Access
- **Layout:** An inline banner interrupting the grid, or a prominent section at the bottom.
- **Rhythm:** Contrast break from the grid.
- **Intent:** Lead generation.
