# Blog Page Layout Structure

1. **Hero/Header**: "Dental Insights & Tips" title.
2. **Featured Post**: Large hero card for the most recent/important article.
3. **Categories Filter**: Pills to filter by category (Hygiene, News, Tips).
4. **Article Grid**: 3-column grid of article cards (Image, Date, Title, Excerpt).
5. **Pagination**: Simple numbered pagination.
6. **Newsletter Subscribe**: In-line CTA to get updates.
