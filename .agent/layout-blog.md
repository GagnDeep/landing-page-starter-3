# Layout: Blog Page
**Goal:** Establish authority, improve SEO, and provide valuable oral health education to the community.
**Rhythm:** Magazine-style layout with featured content followed by chronological or categorized grids.

1. **Global Header (Sticky)**: Standard navigation.
2. **Hero Section / Featured Post**: Large spotlight on the latest or most important article. Big image, title, excerpt, "Read More" button.
3. **Category Navigation**: Horizontal scrolling or pill-based menu to filter posts (e.g., "Oral Hygiene", "Cosmetic", "Kids").
4. **Recent Articles Grid**: 3-column grid of the 6 most recent posts. Each card has an image, date, category tag, title, and excerpt.
5. **Newsletter Signup Banner**: Inline banner encouraging users to subscribe for dental tips.
6. **Popular Posts Sidebar / Section**: List of the top 5 most-read articles.
7. **Author Spotlight**: Brief intro to the doctors writing the content, building trust.
8. **Article Grid Continuation**: More articles, with a "Load More" or pagination mechanism.
9. **Video Content Highlight**: A section dedicated to embedded YouTube or native video tips from the doctors.
10. **Infographics Gallery**: Thumbnail grid linking to downloadable or viewable oral health infographics.
11. **Patient Stories / Case Studies Category**: Highlight a specific category focused on real patient journeys.
12. **Ask the Dentist feature**: A callout encouraging users to submit questions that might be answered in a future blog.
13. **Search Bar**: Prominent search functionality to find specific topics.
14. **Tags Cloud**: Visual representation of popular topics.
15. **Social Sharing Prompt**: "Follow us for daily tips" with social media icons.
16. **Emergency Dental Tips**: A permanent sticky or highlighted section linking to "What to do in a dental emergency."
17. **Promotional Banner**: Subtle ad for a specific service (e.g., "Book your summer whitening session").
18. **CTA: Book an Appointment**: Standard booking push at the bottom.
19. **Global Footer (Top)**: Standard footer links.
20. **Global Footer (Bottom)**: Legal links.
