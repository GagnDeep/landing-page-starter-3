# Blog Page Layout Blueprint

**Goal:** Patient education and SEO authority.

1. **Hero Section:** "Dental Health Insights" - search bar integration.
2. **Featured Article:** Large highlight card for the most recent or most important post.
3. **Category Filter:** Pills to filter by (Oral Hygiene, Implants, Cosmetic, Kids).
4. **Article Grid:** Standard 3-column grid of blog cards (Image, Date, Title, Excerpt).
5. **Newsletter Signup:** "Get oral health tips in your inbox".
6. **Pagination/Load More:** Standard list navigation.
7. **Popular Topics Tag Cloud:** Quick links to common concerns.
8. **Final CTA:** "Have a specific concern?" -> Ask the Doctor Form.
