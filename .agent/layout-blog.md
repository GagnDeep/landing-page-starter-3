# Dentistree - Blog / Patient Education Page Layout Blueprint

**Goal:** Position the clinic as an authoritative yet approachable source of dental health knowledge, improving SEO and patient education.

## Sections (20+)

1. **Hero Section:** "The Dentistree Blog: Tips for a Healthier Smile".
2. **Featured Article Banner:** Large highlight of the most recent or important post (e.g., "How to Prepare Your Child for Their First Visit").
3. **Category Filter / Tabs:** Buttons to filter by "Pediatric", "Orthodontics", "Oral Hygiene", "Clinic News".
4. **Recent Posts Grid (Intro):** "Latest Articles".
5. **Blog Post Card 1:** Image, category badge, title, short excerpt, "Read More".
6. **Blog Post Card 2:** Image, category badge, title, short excerpt.
7. **Blog Post Card 3:** Image, category badge, title, short excerpt.
8. **Blog Post Card 4:** Image, category badge, title, short excerpt.
9. **Blog Post Card 5:** Image, category badge, title, short excerpt.
10. **Blog Post Card 6:** Image, category badge, title, short excerpt.
11. **Newsletter Signup Banner:** "Get monthly dental tips delivered to your inbox." Input field and subscribe button.
12. **Popular Topics Sidebar/Grid:** Quick links to highly searched topics (e.g., "Braces Pain Relief", "Teething Tips").
13. **Video Content Section:** "Watch & Learn". Embedded educational videos (e.g., proper brushing technique).
14. **Video Card 1:** Thumbnail and title.
15. **Video Card 2:** Thumbnail and title.
16. **Author Spotlight:** Brief bio of the doctors writing the content, reinforcing expertise.
17. **Patient Resources Links:** Links to downloadable forms or post-op care instructions.
18. **Pagination:** Controls to navigate older posts.
19. **Call to Action:** "Have a specific question about your dental health? Ask us during a consultation!"
20. **Global Footer:** Standard footer.
