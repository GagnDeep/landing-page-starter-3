# Layout Blueprint: Blog / Patient Education Page
**Status:** Defined (Phase 1)
**Vibe:** Educational, Helpful, Approachable

## Sections
1. **Hero:** "Dental Health Hub" - welcoming title.
2. **Featured Article:** Large card highlighting the most important or recent post.
3. **Categories Filter:** Sticky or top-bar filter (e.g., "Kids Dental", "Cosmetic", "Tips").
4. **Article Grid:** 3-column masonry or standard grid of article cards (Image, Title, Excerpt, Date).
5. **Pagination/Load More:** Standard controls.
6. **Newsletter Signup:** "Get monthly dental tips."
