# Layout Blueprint: Blog Page

1. **Header**: Global navigation.
2. **Hero Section**: Title: "Dental Health Hub." Subtitle: "Tips, news, and insights for a healthier smile."
3. **Featured Article**: A large hero-style card for the most recent or most important blog post.
4. **Category Filter**: Pills or tabs to filter by "Oral Hygiene", "Cosmetic", "Kids", "Clinic News".
5. **Recent Articles Grid**: Standard 3-column grid of blog post cards (Image, Title, Excerpt, Date, Read More link).
6. **Newsletter Signup**: Inline banner to subscribe for monthly dental tips.
7. **Pagination / Load More**: Standard pagination controls.
8. **Sidebar (Optional - on desktop)**: Search bar, Popular Posts, Categories.
9. **Footer**: Global footer.
