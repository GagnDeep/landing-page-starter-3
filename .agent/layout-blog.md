# Blog Page Layout Blueprint

1. **Global Notice Bar**: Top banner.
2. **Primary Navigation**: Sticky header.
3. **Hero Section**: "Dental Health Hub" or similar title with a fresh, clean image.
4. **Featured Article**: Large prominent card for the latest or most important post.
5. **Category Filters/Pills**: Quick sorting (e.g., "Kids", "Aligners", "Tips", "Clinic News").
6. **Recent Posts Grid**: 6-9 cards of the latest articles with thumbnails and excerpts.
7. **Newsletter Signup**: Inline form to subscribe for tips and updates.
8. **"Dr. Siddharth Recommends"**: Highlighted posts specifically chosen by the doctor (e.g., aligner care).
9. **"Dr. Isha's Pediatric Corner"**: Highlighted posts focusing on children's dental health.
10. **Popular Topics Tag Cloud**: Clickable tags for specific issues (e.g., "cavities", "whitening").
11. **Patient Stories/Case Studies**: Specific category highlighting successful treatments.
12. **Video Content Highlight**: Embedding or linking to educational videos created by the clinic.
13. **Search Bar**: Easy text search for specific dental questions.
14. **Pagination / "Load More"**: For browsing older posts.
15. **Social Sharing Prompt**: Encouraging readers to share helpful articles.
16. **Guest Posts / Expert Interviews**: Space for collaborative content if applicable.
17. **Community Events Coverage**: Posts about local events Dentistree has participated in.
18. **"Ask the Dentist" Submission**: A form to submit questions that might be answered in future posts.
19. **Ready to Book? (CTA)**: Persistent reminder to schedule an appointment.
20. **Global Footer**: Standard footer.
