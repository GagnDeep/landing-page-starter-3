# Dentocare Blog Page Layout

## 1. Hero Section
- **Layout:** Featured article highlight with large image and bold title.
- **Rhythm:** Engaging, magazine-style.

## 2. Category Filter
- **Layout:** Horizontal list of topics (e.g., Hygiene, Orthodontics, Clinic News).
- **Rhythm:** Functional.

## 3. Article Grid
- **Layout:** Responsive grid (2 or 3 columns) of article cards.
- **Rhythm:** Browsable and visually consistent.

## 4. Newsletter Signup
- **Layout:** Inline banner to subscribe to oral health tips.
- **Rhythm:** Unobtrusive.

## 5. Pagination
- **Layout:** Standard pagination controls.
- **Rhythm:** Utility.
