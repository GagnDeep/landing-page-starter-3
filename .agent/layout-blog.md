# Layout Blueprint: Blog / Editorial Page

This document outlines the structure for the Blog/Editorial section, designed to look like a premium digital magazine.

## 1. Hero: Editorial Hub
- **Intent:** Present the blog as a high-end publication.
- **Rhythm:** Large featured article spanning the top, overlay text on image.
- **Elements:** "The Journal" or "Editorial," latest featured post title, author, date.

## 2. Category Filter
- **Intent:** Easy navigation of topics.
- **Rhythm:** Horizontal scroll list of tags.
- **Elements:** "Hair Care," "Bridal Trends," "Academy News," "Ajay's Tips."

## 3. Latest Articles Grid
- **Intent:** Browse recent content.
- **Rhythm:** 3-column masonry or even grid.
- **Elements:** Article cards with high-quality thumbnail, serif title, short excerpt, "Read More" link.

## 4. Newsletter Subscribe (Inline)
- **Intent:** Capture leads interested in content.
- **Rhythm:** Full-width structural break, soft background color.
- **Elements:** "Get weekly beauty insights," email input, subscribe button.

## 5. Popular / Trending Posts
- **Intent:** Highlight evergreen or highly-read content.
- **Rhythm:** Sidebar on desktop, or a distinct 2-column section below the grid.
- **Elements:** Numbered list or smaller card format.

## 6. Video Content Section
- **Intent:** Differentiate media types.
- **Rhythm:** Darker background section, large video thumbnails.
- **Elements:** Tutorials or behind-the-scenes videos.

## 7. Spotlight on Academy
- **Intent:** Cross-promote educational content.
- **Rhythm:** Visually distinct card or banner within the feed.
- **Elements:** "Student Spotlight" or "Masterclass Highlights."

## 8. Pagination / Load More
- **Intent:** Access older content without overwhelming the initial load.
- **Rhythm:** Centered button or simple numbered pagination at the bottom.
- **Elements:** "Load More Articles."

## 9. Author Bios (Bottom of Feed)
- **Intent:** Build authority for the writers (often Ajay sir or senior staff).
- **Rhythm:** Small circular avatars with brief text.
- **Elements:** "Meet the Experts."

## 10. Footer CTA
- **Intent:** Convert readers into clients.
- **Rhythm:** Standard pre-footer block.
- **Elements:** "Inspired? Book a consultation today."
