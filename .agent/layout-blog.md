# Layout: Blog & Insights Page

## Structural Intent
To position "The Dental Masters" as thought leaders in dentistry. The blog serves as an educational resource for patients, demonstrating expertise and improving SEO.

## Visual Rhythm
An editorial, magazine-like layout. Focus on beautiful typography, ample white space, and high-quality, non-stock imagery (or highly curated, abstract stock).

## Sections

1.  **Hero Section:** Title: "Insights & Education." A featured article highlighted prominently with a large image and compelling typography.
2.  **Category Navigation:** A clean, horizontal list of topics (e.g., Implantology, Smile Design, Oral Health, Clinic News) to filter content.
3.  **Featured Articles Grid:** A 2-column or asymmetrical grid highlighting 2-3 important, high-level articles (e.g., "The Truth About Dental Tourism").
4.  **Latest Articles Feed:** A standard 3-column grid or list view of the most recent blog posts. Includes thumbnail, title, excerpt, and read time.
5.  **Newsletter Subscription:** A prominent block encouraging users to subscribe for monthly insights. Clean form design.
6.  **Patient Education Series:** A highlighted section for multi-part series or comprehensive guides (e.g., "The Complete Guide to Dental Implants").
7.  **Case Study Highlight:** A specific type of post focusing on a clinical case, showing before/after and the thought process behind the treatment.
8.  **Video Content / Webinars (If applicable):** A section linking to video content, interviews with the founders, or educational animations.
9.  **Topic Deep Dive: Implants:** A section pulling specific articles related to the clinic's core specialty.
10. **Topic Deep Dive: Aesthetics:** A section pulling articles related to cosmetic procedures.
11. **Author Profiles:** A small sidebar or footer section highlighting the doctors who author the posts, adding credibility.
12. **Search Functionality:** A clean, unobtrusive search bar to find specific topics.
13. **Popular Articles:** A sidebar or small grid showing the most read pieces.
14. **Social Sharing Prompts:** Subtle icons on individual article cards or within the layout to encourage sharing.
15. **FAQ Integration:** A block linking to the main FAQ page for common questions that might not need a full article.
16. **Load More / Pagination:** Clean, minimalist controls to load older articles.
17. **Clinical Terminology Glossary (Teaser):** A link to a page explaining complex dental terms in simple language.
18. **Behind the Scenes:** Articles focusing on the clinic culture, technology updates, or team training.
19. **Final CTA:** A persistent banner or block at the bottom of the feed encouraging readers to book a consultation if they have concerns.
20. **Footer:** Standard global footer.
