# Blog Layout Blueprint

## Section 1: Blog Hub Hero
- **Intent:** "Smile Journal." Insights on oral health, clinic news, and aligner tips.
- **Rhythm:** Magazine-style header, large feature text.

## Section 2: Featured Article (Latest/Most Important)
- **Intent:** Highlight a key piece of content (e.g., "The Ultimate Guide to Invisible Aligners").
- **Rhythm:** Full-width card with large image, bold title, and reading time.

## Section 3: Category Navigation
- **Intent:** Filter by Aligners, General Health, Cosmetic, Clinic News.
- **Rhythm:** Sticky horizontal scroll or pill-shaped tags.

## Section 4: Recent Posts Grid
- **Intent:** Chronological feed of the latest articles.
- **Rhythm:** 3-column masonry or standard grid.

## Section 5: "Ask the Dentist" Series Highlight
- **Intent:** Specific Q&A posts answered by Dr. Sumeet.
- **Rhythm:** Distinct visual styling, perhaps a different background color.

## Section 6: Newsletter Subscribe Banner
- **Intent:** Capture emails for regular updates.
- **Rhythm:** Interruption banner halfway down the page.

## Section 7: Popular Topics (Tags)
- **Intent:** Quick links to highly searched terms.
- **Rhythm:** Cloud of text links.

## Section 8: Patient Success Stories (Case Studies disguised as blogs)
- **Intent:** Narrative-driven results.
- **Rhythm:** Image-heavy layout with before/after thumbnails.

## Section 9: Video Content Highlight
- **Intent:** Embeds of YouTube/Instagram educational reels.
- **Rhythm:** Carousel of video players.

## Section 10: Seasonal Oral Care Tips
- **Intent:** Timely content (e.g., "Protecting teeth during holidays").
- **Rhythm:** Standard blog card.

## Section 11: Behind the Scenes at the Clinic
- **Intent:** Culture and staff highlights.
- **Rhythm:** Photo-essay style preview.

## Section 12: Demystifying Dental Tech
- **Intent:** Explaining the 3D scanners and tools.
- **Rhythm:** Diagram-focused thumbnails.

## Section 13: Children's Dental Health Corner
- **Intent:** Content specifically for parents.
- **Rhythm:** Softer, warmer visual cues.

## Section 14: Load More / Pagination
- **Intent:** Access older content.
- **Rhythm:** Simple centered button or numbered list.

## Section 15: Single Post Layout: Header
- **Intent:** Title, Author, Date, Share buttons.
- **Rhythm:** Centered, narrow width.

## Section 16: Single Post Layout: Content Body
- **Intent:** The actual article.
- **Rhythm:** Max-width `prose`, highly readable, generous line height.

## Section 17: Single Post Layout: In-line CTA
- **Intent:** "Ready to start your aligner journey?" midway through the post.
- **Rhythm:** Subtle, non-intrusive box.

## Section 18: Single Post Layout: Author Bio
- **Intent:** Credibility (Usually Dr. Sumeet).
- **Rhythm:** Small avatar, brief text.

## Section 19: Single Post Layout: Related Articles
- **Intent:** Keep users reading.
- **Rhythm:** 3-column grid at the bottom.

## Section 20: Standard Footer
- **Intent:** Consistent navigation ending.
- **Rhythm:** Inherits global footer.
