# Layout Blueprint: Blog / Patient Education

**Purpose**: Establish Dr. Anshum Gupta as a thought leader and educate the Patiala community on oral health. Good for SEO.

## Sections

1.  **Global Navigation Bar**: Standard sticky header.
2.  **Page Header (Hero)**: H1 ("Dental Health Hub"). Search bar prominent in the center.
3.  **Featured Article**: Large banner. Highlights the most important or recent article (e.g., "The Complete Guide to Dental Implants").
4.  **Category Filter**: Pill-shaped horizontal scrolling list (All, Implants, Braces, Oral Hygiene, Kids).
5.  **Latest Posts Grid**: 3-column masonry or standard grid of recent articles. Includes image, title, read time, and short excerpt.
6.  **Sidebar (Optional/Desktop)**: Search, Categories, Most Popular Posts, Newsletter Signup.
7.  **Pagination/Load More**: Clean button to load older posts.
8.  **Video Content Section**: A row dedicated to educational YouTube videos embedded directly.
9.  **Infographics Download**: Section offering downloadable PDFs (e.g., "Braces Care Guide", "Post-Extraction Instructions").
10. **Myth vs. Fact Section**: Interactive or stylized cards debunking common dental myths.
11. **Author Bio Block (Dr. Anshum)**: Small section reinforcing who writes/reviews the content.
12. **Subscribe for Updates**: Prominent newsletter signup block to capture emails.
13. **Patient Stories / Case Studies**: Specific tag or section dedicated to deep-dive case studies with clinical photos.
14. **Community Events Coverage**: Posts related to free camps or seminars held in Patiala.
15. **Ask the Dentist**: A small form where users can submit questions that might be answered in future posts.
16. **Trending Topics**: Tag cloud or simple list of highly searched terms.
17. **Disclaimer**: Medical disclaimer noting that the blog is for education, not diagnosis.
18. **Related Services Links**: Quick links back to the main services pages from the blog index.
19. **Global Pre-Footer**: Booking CTA.
20. **Global Footer**: Standard footer.
