# Blog Page Layout Architecture

## 1. Hero Section (`blog-hero`)
- **Intent:** Introduce the journal/blog.
- **Rhythm:** A clean header reading "The Journal" or "Recent Stories" with a brief subtitle.

## 2. Featured Story (`featured-post`)
- **Intent:** Highlight a particularly stunning recent wedding.
- **Rhythm:** A large, prominent "card" taking up the top section, with a large image and a descriptive excerpt.

## 3. Categories/Tags (`blog-categories`)
- **Intent:** Allow users to filter content.
- **Rhythm:** A subtle horizontal list of tags (e.g., "Weddings", "Engagements", "Editorial") below the hero or featured post.

## 4. Post Grid (`post-grid`)
- **Intent:** Display the archive of stories.
- **Rhythm:** A balanced, 2 or 3-column grid of post cards. Each card needs an image, title, and date.

## 5. Pagination/Load More (`pagination`)
- **Intent:** Navigation for older posts.
- **Rhythm:** A simple "Load More" button or classic pagination numbers at the bottom of the grid.

## 6. Newsletter/Subscribe (Optional) (`newsletter-signup`)
- **Intent:** Capture emails for updates.
- **Rhythm:** A subtle, elegant inline form.

## 7. Final CTA (`blog-cta`)
- **Intent:** General inquiry prompt.
- **Rhythm:** Standard high-contrast CTA block.
