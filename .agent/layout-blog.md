# Spacedent Patient Education (Blog) Layout

Educational content to establish authority and help with local SEO.

## Sections
1. **Hero Header:** "Patient Education & Dental Tips"
2. **Featured Article:** Large highlight of the most recent/important post.
3. **Category Filter:** E.g., "Pediatric", "Oral Hygiene", "Treatments".
4. **Article Grid:** Clean, visually appealing list of recent posts.
5. **Newsletter Signup:** "Get monthly dental tips".
6. **Specific Article Layout (Sub-layout):** Clean typography, reading time, author bio (Dr. Vikramjit or Dr. Arshiya), related articles.
