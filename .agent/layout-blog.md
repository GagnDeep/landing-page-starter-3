# Layout Blueprint: Blog & Insights Page

## Structural Slots

1.  **Global Top Bar & Header** (Inherited)
2.  **Page Hero**
    *   *Intent:* "Dental Insights & News". Minimalist header.
3.  **Featured / Latest Article (Hero layout)**
    *   *Intent:* Large image, bold title, excerpt. The most important recent piece of content.
4.  **Category Navigation**
    *   *Intent:* Pill-shaped links (All, Oral Hygiene, Cosmetic, Implants, Clinic News).
5.  **Article Grid (Main Feed)**
    *   *Intent:* 3-column grid of article cards. Image, date, category tag, title, read more link.
6.  **Pagination or "Load More"**
    *   *Intent:* Standard list expansion.
7.  **Newsletter Subscribe Block**
    *   *Intent:* Inline CTA to get articles delivered to email.
8.  **Popular / Trending Articles Sidebar (Optional depending on layout width)**
    *   *Intent:* Quick links to high-traffic posts.
9.  **Global Footer** (Inherited)
