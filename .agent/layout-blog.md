# Layout: Blog & Insights Page Blueprint

An editorial, magazine-style layout to establish authority and educate patients.

1. **Hero/Featured Article:** Large image, bold headline, highlighting the most recent or important post.
2. **Categories/Tags:** Clean pill-shaped filters (e.g., "Oral Hygiene", "Cosmetic", "Implants").
3. **Article Grid:** Masonry or clean bento layout for recent articles. Card design includes thumbnail, reading time, date, and title.
4. **Newsletter Signup:** In-line break in the grid to capture emails.
5. **Popular Articles Sidebar (Desktop) / List (Mobile):** Quick links to highly trafficked posts.
6. **Pagination/Load More:** Elegant, non-intrusive loading mechanism.
