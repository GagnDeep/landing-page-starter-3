# Blog Page Layout Architecture

## 1. Page Header
- **Visuals:** Minimalist header.
- **Content:** "Dental Health Insights & News".

## 2. Featured Article
- **Visuals:** Large card taking up top of grid.
- **Content:** Most recent or most important article (e.g., "Complete Guide to Implants").

## 3. Article Grid
- **Visuals:** 3-column grid of standard cards. Pagination at bottom.
- **Content:** Blog posts with thumbnail, title, excerpt, date, and category.

## 4. Newsletter Signup / CTA
- **Visuals:** Inline banner.
- **Content:** Subscribe for tips, or standard "Book an appointment" CTA.
