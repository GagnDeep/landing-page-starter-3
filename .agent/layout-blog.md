# Blog (The Journal) Layout Blueprint

## Goal
Establish thought leadership, improve SEO, and educate patients on treatments, aftercare, and industry trends. The tone should be editorial, like a high-end beauty magazine.

## Sections
1. **Hero Section:** "The Preet Aesthetics Journal."
2. **Featured Article:** A large, prominent block highlighting the most recent or most important post.
3. **Category Filters:** Tags to filter by "Dentistry", "Skincare", "Bridal", "Clinic News".
4. **Article Grid:** A responsive grid of article cards (Image, Category, Title, Date, Read More link).
5. **Pagination / Load More:** Elegant button to load older articles.
6. **Newsletter Signup:** Inline banner to subscribe for updates.
7. **Popular Topics Sidebar (Optional on desktop):** Quick links to most-read articles.
