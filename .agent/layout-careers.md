# Layout Blueprint: Careers

**Purpose**: Attract top dental talent, hygienists, and administrative staff by showcasing the clinic as a premier, modern workplace in Patiala.

## Sections

1.  **Global Navigation Bar**: Standard sticky header.
2.  **Page Header (Hero)**: H1 ("Join Our Team"). Image of the staff looking professional and happy.
3.  **Our Culture**: Short narrative on the work environment, focus on continuous learning, and patient-first philosophy.
4.  **Why Work With Us**: Grid highlighting benefits (Advanced Technology, Mentorship from Dr. Anshum, Competitive Pay, Work-Life Balance).
5.  **Current Openings Filter**: Simple toggle or list to filter by Clinical vs. Administrative roles.
6.  **Job Posting 1 (e.g., Associate Dentist)**: Expandable card. Title, Experience required, Apply button.
7.  **Job Posting 2 (e.g., Dental Hygienist)**: Expandable card.
8.  **Job Posting 3 (e.g., Front Desk Executive)**: Expandable card.
9.  **Application Form**: Integrated form for submitting a resume and cover letter directly.
10. **"Don't see your role?" Section**: Encouragement to submit an open application for future consideration.
11. **Employee Testimonials**: Quotes from current staff about working at the clinic.
12. **The Facility (Workspace Tour)**: Images focusing on the staff break room, modern operatories, and clean environment.
13. **Training & Development**: Information on how the clinic supports continued education (CE) for its staff.
14. **Hiring Process Timeline**: Step-by-step (Application -> Phone Screen -> Clinical Interview/Trial -> Offer).
15. **Diversity & Inclusion Statement**: Standard HR statement.
16. **Student Internships/Shadowing**: Information for dental students looking to observe.
17. **Contact HR**: Direct email for career-related inquiries.
18. **Location Reminder**: Emphasizing the Patiala location.
19. **Global Pre-Footer**: Link back to About Us to learn more about the clinic's history.
20. **Global Footer**: Standard footer.
