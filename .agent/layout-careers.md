# Layout Blueprint: Careers Page

## Structural Slots

1.  **Global Top Bar & Header** (Inherited)
2.  **Page Hero**
    *   *Intent:* "Join Our Team". Inspiring image of clinic staff collaborating.
3.  **Why Work With Us?**
    *   *Intent:* Value proposition for employees. Focus on modern tech, continuous learning, premium environment.
4.  **Culture & Benefits Grid**
    *   *Intent:* Icons + text highlighting perks (Mentorship, Health benefits, Work-life balance).
5.  **Open Positions (List/Accordion)**
    *   *Intent:* Categorized by Dentists, Hygienists, Admin. Expandable to see job descriptions.
6.  **Application Form / Process**
    *   *Intent:* Upload resume, cover letter, and basic details.
7.  **Employee Testimonials**
    *   *Intent:* Quotes from current staff about the work environment.
8.  **Global Footer** (Inherited)
