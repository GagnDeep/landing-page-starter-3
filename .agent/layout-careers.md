# Dentistree - Careers Page Layout Blueprint

**Goal:** Attract top dental talent by showcasing a positive, modern, and supportive work culture.

## Sections (20+)

1. **Hero Section:** "Join the Dentistree Family". Image of the team looking happy and professional.
2. **Culture Intro:** "Why work with us?" Emphasize work-life balance, modern tech, and a supportive team.
3. **Benefit 1:** Competitive Compensation & Bonuses.
4. **Benefit 2:** Continuing Education Support.
5. **Benefit 3:** Health Benefits & Dental Care.
6. **Benefit 4:** Modern, State-of-the-Art Facility.
7. **A Day in the Life:** Short descriptive paragraph or video about the daily clinic atmosphere.
8. **Testimonial from Staff:** Quote from a current employee about the work environment.
9. **Open Positions (Header):** List of current vacancies.
10. **Job Card 1:** E.g., "Registered Dental Hygienist" - Location, Type (Full-time).
11. **Job Card 2:** E.g., "Dental Assistant" - Location, Type.
12. **Job Card 3:** E.g., "Front Desk Coordinator" - Location, Type.
13. **Job Details (Expanded View/Modal structure):** Responsibilities list.
14. **Job Details:** Requirements/Qualifications list.
15. **Application Process (Steps):** 1. Apply online, 2. Phone interview, 3. In-person visit.
16. **General Application Form (Intro):** "Don't see your role? Send your resume anyway."
17. **Form Field:** Name & Email.
18. **Form Field:** Resume Upload capability.
19. **Equal Opportunity Statement:** Brief legal/cultural statement on diversity.
20. **Global Footer:** Standard footer.
