# Layout Blueprint: Careers Page

This document outlines the structure for the Careers page, aimed at attracting top talent to the salon and academy.

## 1. Hero: Join Our Elite Team
- **Intent:** Inspiring call to action for professionals.
- **Rhythm:** Dynamic hero with background video of stylists working.
- **Elements:** "Elevate Your Craft with Us," "View Open Positions" CTA.

## 2. Our Culture & Values
- **Intent:** Explain why it's a great place to work.
- **Rhythm:** 3-column grid.
- **Elements:** "Continuous Learning," "Premium Environment," "Creative Freedom."

## 3. Benefits & Perks
- **Intent:** Highlight the tangible advantages of employment.
- **Rhythm:** List or icon grid.
- **Elements:** Health benefits, continuous training at the Academy, product discounts.

## 4. Open Positions (Listings)
- **Intent:** Clear display of current needs.
- **Rhythm:** Accordion or list of detailed cards.
- **Elements:** Job Title, Location (Studio/Home), Experience Required, "Apply Now" button.

## 5. Day in the Life (Video/Gallery)
- **Intent:** Give a realistic preview of the work environment.
- **Rhythm:** Full-width media slider.
- **Elements:** Images or short clips of team members working, training, and interacting.

## 6. Testimonials from Staff
- **Intent:** Internal social proof.
- **Rhythm:** Carousel of quotes.
- **Elements:** Quotes from current stylists/educators about their growth.

## 7. The Academy Advantage for Staff
- **Intent:** Highlight internal upskilling.
- **Rhythm:** Distinct block, perhaps sharing visual language with the Academy pages.
- **Elements:** Information on how staff get priority access to masterclasses.

## 8. Application Process
- **Intent:** Set expectations for the hiring timeline.
- **Rhythm:** Horizontal timeline or 4-step list.
- **Elements:** "Apply -> Review -> Audition -> Join."

## 9. General Application Form
- **Intent:** Capture talent even if a specific role isn't listed.
- **Rhythm:** Clean form layout.
- **Elements:** Resume upload, portfolio link, area of interest.

## 10. Footer
- **Intent:** Standard ending.
- **Rhythm:** Global component.
- **Elements:** Links back to main site areas.
