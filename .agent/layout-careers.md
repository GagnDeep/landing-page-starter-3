# Layout: Careers Page

## Structural Intent
To attract top-tier dental professionals, specialists, and support staff. The page must communicate that The Dental Masters is a center of excellence, a place for career growth, and an ethical environment.

## Visual Rhythm
Professional, structured, and inspiring. Uses photography of the team in action, clean lists for job postings, and strong typographic hierarchy for culture statements.

## Sections

1.  **Hero Section:** Title: "Join The Masters." Subtitle: "Build your career at Chandigarh's premier dental center of excellence."
2.  **Our Culture:** A strong narrative section detailing the work environment—ethical, high-tech, and collaborative.
3.  **Why Work With Us?:** A grid of benefits (e.g., Access to advanced technology, Mentorship from master clinicians, Continuous education, Premium environment).
4.  **The Standard of Excellence:** A statement emphasizing that only those dedicated to the highest international standards should apply.
5.  **Open Positions (Filterable):** A clean, interactive list or accordion of current openings, categorized by role (Doctors, Hygienists, Admin).
6.  **Role Detail: Specialists & Associates:** Description of what is expected from clinical staff.
7.  **Role Detail: Support & Admin:** Description of expectations for the patient coordination team.
8.  **Mentorship Program:** Details on any programs designed to train junior doctors in advanced techniques.
9.  **Technology & Facilities:** A section highlighting the equipment staff get to work with (CBCT, scanners, premium materials), as this attracts top talent.
10. **Employee Testimonials:** Short quotes from current staff about the work environment.
11. **The Application Process:** A clear timeline (e.g., Apply -> Interview -> Clinical Assessment -> Offer).
12. **General Application Form:** A form for speculative applications even if a specific role isn't listed. Includes CV/Resume upload.
13. **Diversity & Inclusion Statement:** A standard statement ensuring equal opportunity.
14. **Continuing Education:** Details on how the clinic supports staff attending conferences or taking courses.
15. **Gallery: The Team in Action:** Candid, professional shots of the team working together.
16. **Clinic Location Highlights:** Selling the location (Sector 21C, Chandigarh) as a great place to work.
17. **FAQ for Applicants:** Short accordion answering common questions about working there.
18. **Contact HR:** Direct email for specific recruitment queries.
19. **Final CTA:** "Take the next step in your professional journey."
20. **Footer:** Standard global footer.
