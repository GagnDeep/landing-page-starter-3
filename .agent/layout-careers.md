# Careers Page Layout Blueprint

1. **Global Notice Bar**: Top banner.
2. **Primary Navigation**: Sticky header.
3. **Hero Section**: "Join the Dentistree Family" - showing team camaraderie.
4. **Our Culture & Values**: Why it's great to work here (supportive, growth-oriented, patient-first).
5. **Message from the Founders**: Dr. Siddharth and Dr. Isha on team importance.
6. **Benefits & Perks Grid**: e.g., continuing education, health benefits, uniform allowance, paid time off.
7. **Day in the Life**: Short narrative or video of working at the clinic.
8. **Current Openings (Section Header)**: Lead-in to job listings.
9. **Job Listing: Dental Assistant (if open)**: Accordion or card with details.
10. **Job Listing: Hygienist (if open)**: Accordion or card with details.
11. **Job Listing: Front Desk / Coordinator (if open)**: Accordion or card with details.
12. **Job Listing: Associate Dentist (if open)**: Accordion or card with details.
13. **"Don't see your role?"**: Open application prompt for future considerations.
14. **Application Form**: Simple upload form for resume and cover letter.
15. **The Interview Process**: Clear steps of what applicants can expect.
16. **Team Testimonials**: Quotes from current staff about working there.
17. **Continuing Education Commitment**: Highlighting how the clinic invests in staff training.
18. **Clinic Facilities for Staff**: Mention of break room, amenities.
19. **Equal Opportunity Statement**: Standard diversity and inclusion text.
20. **Global Footer**: Standard footer.
