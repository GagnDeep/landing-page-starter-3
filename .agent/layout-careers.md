# Careers Page Layout Blueprint

1. **Hero Header:** Page title "Join Our Team".
2. **Culture & Values:** A section describing the work environment, "Clinic-as-Studio" philosophy, and commitment to excellence.
3. **Benefits & Perks:** Why work at Dental Lifeline Clinic (e.g., continuous education, modern equipment).
4. **Open Positions (List):** Dynamic or static list of current job openings (e.g., Dental Assistant, Receptionist).
5. **Application Process:** Brief explanation of what to expect after applying.
6. **General Application Form:** A way to submit a resume even if no specific role is listed.
