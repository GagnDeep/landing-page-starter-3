# Careers Page Layout Intent

**Page Intent:** Attract top clinical and administrative talent in Chandigarh.

1. **`CareersHero`**: Join our family-trusted practice.
2. **`WhyWorkWithUs`**: Benefits, culture, and growth opportunities.
3. **`OpenPositionsList`**: Accordion or list of current openings.
