# Careers Layout Blueprint

## Goal
Attract top-tier talent to the clinic by showcasing a premium, supportive, and advanced work environment.

## Sections
1. **Hero Section:** "Join Our Team of Experts."
2. **Culture & Values:** A text block describing the clinic's commitment to excellence, continuous learning, and staff wellbeing.
3. **Why Work With Us?:** A grid of benefits (e.g., Advanced Training, Premium Environment, Competitive Compensation).
4. **Open Positions:** A list or accordion of current job openings (e.g., Associate Dentist, Aesthetic Nurse, Patient Coordinator).
5. **Application Form:** A simple form to submit a resume/CV directly if no specific role fits.
6. **Day in the Life Gallery:** 3-4 images showing staff interacting happily in the clinical environment.
