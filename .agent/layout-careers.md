# Careers Page Layout Blueprint

1. **Global Header:** Standard navigation.
2. **Hero Section (Join The Studio):** Inspiring image of the team working, strong headline about pursuing excellence in aesthetics.
3. **Culture Intro:** Text block detailing the work environment—high standards, continuous learning, luxury service.
4. **Core Values (Workplace):** 3-column grid (e.g., Empathy, Precision, Artistry).
5. **Benefits & Perks:** List of employee benefits (training stipends, wellness programs, aesthetic treatments).
6. **Career Growth:** Explanation of mentorship under Dr. Arshpreet and advancement opportunities.
7. **Day in the Life:** Short video or photo essay showing the daily rhythm of the clinic.
8. **Current Openings Header:** "Open Roles".
9. **Department Filter:** Buttons to filter by Clinical, Aesthetic, Admin.
10. **Role 1 (e.g., Senior Aesthetician):** Accordion or card with brief details and "Apply" button.
11. **Role 2 (e.g., Dental Assistant):** Accordion/card.
12. **Role 3 (e.g., Patient Concierge):** Accordion/card.
13. **Application Process:** Timeline of how hiring works (Review, Initial Chat, Working Interview, Offer).
14. **Diversity & Inclusion:** Statement on building a welcoming team.
15. **Spontaneous Application:** "Don't see a role? Send your resume anyway" block.
16. **Clinic Gallery:** Photos specifically highlighting the breakroom, tech, and staff areas.
17. **Testimonial (Employee):** Quote from a current staff member about working there.
18. **Application Form Integration:** Form specifically for resume uploads and cover letters.
19. **Contact HR:** Direct email for career inquiries.
20. **Global Footer:** Standard footer.
21. **Sub-footer:** Copyright.
