# Careers Page Layout Blueprint

## Visual Rhythm & Layout Intent
Professional, highlighting the clinic culture and standards.

## Sections
1. **Hero:** "Join Our Team".
2. **Culture & Environment:** Why work at The Dental Masters? (Premium setting, learning opportunities, ethical standards).
3. **Open Positions:** List of current vacancies (e.g., Associate Dentist, Hygienist, Front Desk).
4. **Application Process:** How to apply, what to expect.
5. **General Application Form:** Form for speculative applications.
