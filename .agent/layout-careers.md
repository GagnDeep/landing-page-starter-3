# Careers Layout Blueprint

## Section 1: Careers Hero
- **Intent:** "Build the future of dentistry with us."
- **Rhythm:** Dynamic image of the team, bold inviting headline.

## Section 2: Why Work With Us?
- **Intent:** Sell the clinic to potential employees. State-of-the-art tech, great culture.
- **Rhythm:** 3-column feature block with icons.

## Section 3: Core Values
- **Intent:** Alignment check. What kind of people succeed here?
- **Rhythm:** 4-card grid.

## Section 4: A Day in the Life (Video/Gallery)
- **Intent:** Show, don't just tell, the work environment.
- **Rhythm:** Large media container.

## Section 5: Benefits & Perks
- **Intent:** Health insurance, continuing education, team retreats.
- **Rhythm:** Bulleted list with custom checkmarks in a muted box.

## Section 6: Open Positions Header
- **Intent:** Transition to the job board.
- **Rhythm:** Centered H2, search bar.

## Section 7: Department Filters
- **Intent:** Filter by Clinical (Dentists, Hygienists), Admin, Tech.
- **Rhythm:** Pill-shaped toggle buttons.

## Section 8: Job Listings Grid
- **Intent:** Display open roles.
- **Rhythm:** Cards with Job Title, Type (Full time), Location, and "Apply" button.

## Section 9: "Don't see a fit?" (General Application)
- **Intent:** Capture talent even if there's no open role.
- **Rhythm:** Callout box with email link.

## Section 10: The Interview Process
- **Intent:** Set expectations (Apply, Phone Screen, In-person, Offer).
- **Rhythm:** 4-step horizontal process bar.

## Section 11: Employee Testimonials
- **Intent:** Social proof from current staff.
- **Rhythm:** Blockquote slider.

## Section 12: Continuing Education
- **Intent:** Highlight commitment to staff growth.
- **Rhythm:** Split layout, image of training session.

## Section 13: Mentorship Program
- **Intent:** Attract junior staff or recent grads.
- **Rhythm:** Dedicated callout card.

## Section 14: Diversity & Inclusion Statement
- **Intent:** Welcoming environment for all.
- **Rhythm:** Simple, sincere text block.

## Section 15: Clinic Location/Commute
- **Intent:** Practical info for locals.
- **Rhythm:** Small map with transit options.

## Section 16: FAQ for Applicants
- **Intent:** Answer common hiring questions.
- **Rhythm:** Small accordion.

## Section 17: Tech Stack (For Clinical Staff)
- **Intent:** Highlight the specific brands/machines used (e.g., iTero scanners).
- **Rhythm:** Logo strip.

## Section 18: HR Contact
- **Intent:** Direct line for hiring questions.
- **Rhythm:** Simple text link/email.

## Section 19: Bottom CTA
- **Intent:** Final push to view jobs.
- **Rhythm:** Centered button.

## Section 20: Standard Footer
- **Intent:** Consistent navigation ending.
- **Rhythm:** Inherits global footer.
