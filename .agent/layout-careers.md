# Layout Blueprint: Careers Page

1. **Header**: Global navigation.
2. **Hero Section**: Title: "Join Our Dental Family." Subtitle: "Build a rewarding career providing gentle, compassionate care in Patiala."
3. **Why Work With Us?**: 3-4 columns detailing benefits (Culture, Work-Life Balance, Modern Tech, Competitive Pay).
4. **Clinic Culture / Video**: A short text or embedded video showing the team environment behind the scenes.
5. **Current Openings List**: Dynamic or static list of roles (e.g., Dental Hygienist, Front Desk Coordinator). Each links to a detailed description.
6. **Application Process**: Step-by-step graphic (Apply -> Interview -> Trial Day -> Offer).
7. **Spontaneous Application / General Form**: "Don't see a role? Send us your resume anyway." Simple upload form.
8. **Footer**: Global footer.
