# Layout: Careers Page
**Goal:** Attract top dental and administrative talent by showcasing a supportive, state-of-the-art work environment.
**Rhythm:** Inspiring, culture-focused, followed by practical job listings.

1. **Global Header (Sticky)**: Standard navigation.
2. **Hero Section**: Title "Join Our Team". Image of staff smiling together.
3. **Culture & Values**: Section explaining the clinic's commitment to patient care and employee growth.
4. **Why Work With Us? (Grid)**: Benefits like continuous learning, modern tech, supportive environment.
5. **A Word from the Founder**: Quote or video from Dr. Kochar about the team culture.
6. **Current Openings Section Header**: "Open Positions".
7. **Job Category Filter**: Pills to filter by Clinical, Administrative, Support.
8. **Job Listing Cards**: Grid or list of available roles (e.g., Associate Dentist, Receptionist).
9. **Job Card Details**: Title, Type (Full-time/Part-time), Location, "Apply Now" button.
10. **Day in the Life**: Short section or video highlighting what it's like to work there.
11. **Growth Opportunities**: Information on training, mentorship, and career progression.
12. **Diversity & Inclusion Statement**: Commitment to a diverse workplace.
13. **Application Process**: Step-by-step graphic (Apply -> Interview -> Trial -> Onboarding).
14. **General Application Form**: Form for candidates to submit their resume even if a specific role isn't listed.
15. **Employee Testimonials**: Quotes from current staff members.
16. **Clinic Gallery (Staff Focus)**: Behind-the-scenes photos of the team (birthdays, training).
17. **Contact HR**: Direct email link for recruitment inquiries.
18. **Newsletter Signup**: Standard footer signup.
19. **Global Footer (Top)**: Standard footer links.
20. **Global Footer (Bottom)**: Legal links.
