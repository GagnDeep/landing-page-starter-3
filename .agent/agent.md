- Established design direction: A sophisticated, "bridal-aesthetic" luxury clinic feel for Preet Dental & Facial Aesthetics Clinic.
- The design purposely avoids generic "medical" tropes, favoring soft, bridal-inspired palettes (ivory, champagne, muted gold) with deep clinical tones and high contrast for accessibility.
- Typography Strategy: Cormorant Garamond conveys heritage, luxury, and premium care (serif). DM Sans ensures clinical clarity and readability across devices (sans-serif).
- Color Strategy: Light mode uses soft ivory and champagne. Dark mode ("evening mode") uses deep espresso/charcoal while retaining the warmth and luxury of the primary gold accents. OKLCH is used for accurate perceptual color manipulation.
- Spacing and motion principles prioritize a relaxed, elegant, and accessible user experience.
