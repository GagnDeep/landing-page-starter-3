## Session: Phase 1 — Design System & Layout Documentation

**Date:** 2026-05-02
**Phase:** 1/8

**Summary of Design Decisions:**
- Established an ethical, neighborhood-trusted, and painless visual identity for A-1 Dental Care Clinic in Patiala.
- **Typography:** Fraunces for primary headings (warm, premium, human touch) and Manrope for body text (friendly, geometric, highly readable).
- **Color Palette:** Avoided cold sterile colors. Opted for calming sages (`primary`), warm creams (`background`), deep earthy tones, and golden ambers (`accent`) mapped to OKLCH values for both light and dark modes.
- **Spacing & Structure:** Implemented fluid spacing for organic rhythm and soft border radii (`1rem`) to eliminate sharp edges, reinforcing the "painless" identity.
- **Layouts:** Detailed the visual rhythm and intent for 20+ homepage sections and all required inner pages, focusing on narrative flow and emotional reassurance.

**Lingering Questions / Next Steps:**
- Brand identity is solidly defined in the documentation. In Phase 2, we must ensure the actual copy/content matches this empathetic, high-craft tone.
