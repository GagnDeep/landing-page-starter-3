# Layout Blueprint: Contact Page

This document outlines the structure for the Contact page, ensuring it is easy to find information and reach out.

## 1. Hero: Get in Touch
- **Intent:** Direct and clear.
- **Rhythm:** Split layout hero.
- **Elements:** Left: "We'd love to hear from you." Right: Primary contact info (Phone, Email, WhatsApp).

## 2. Location Map & Details
- **Intent:** Guide users to the physical studio.
- **Rhythm:** Full-width interactive map or high-quality static map image.
- **Elements:** Map, clear address text, "Get Directions" button (links to Google Maps).

## 3. Operating Hours
- **Intent:** Inform users when they can expect service or a reply.
- **Rhythm:** Simple 2-column list.
- **Elements:** Days of the week and corresponding hours. Note on holiday hours.

## 4. Contact Form
- **Intent:** Structured way for users to send inquiries.
- **Rhythm:** Clean, single-column form within a centered max-width container.
- **Elements:** Name, Email, Phone, Subject (Dropdown: Booking, Academy, General), Message text area.

## 5. Department Specific Contacts
- **Intent:** Route inquiries efficiently.
- **Rhythm:** 3-column grid of small cards.
- **Elements:** Cards for "Salon Booking," "Academy Admissions," "Press/PR."

## 6. Social Media Links
- **Intent:** Encourage following on other platforms.
- **Rhythm:** Horizontal row of large, elegant icons.
- **Elements:** Instagram, Facebook, YouTube links.

## 7. Studio Ambiance Image
- **Intent:** Maintain visual appeal on a functional page.
- **Rhythm:** Full-width structural image break.
- **Elements:** High-quality photo of the studio exterior or reception area.

## 8. FAQ Teaser
- **Intent:** Deflect common questions before they are submitted via form.
- **Rhythm:** Small section linking to the main FAQ page.
- **Elements:** "Have a quick question? Check our FAQ."

## 9. Newsletter Signup
- **Intent:** Capture leads who might not be ready to book.
- **Rhythm:** Standard inline signup block.
- **Elements:** Email input, submit button.

## 10. Global Footer
- **Intent:** Standard ending.
- **Rhythm:** Global component.
- **Elements:** Legal links, secondary navigation.
