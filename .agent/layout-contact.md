# Contact Page Layout Blueprint

1. **Hero Header:** Page title "Get in Touch" or "Visit Us".
2. **Contact Information Grid:** Cards for Phone, Email, Address, and Operating Hours.
3. **Interactive Map:** A large, embedded map showing the Sector 18A, Chandigarh location.
4. **Contact / Booking Form:** A clean, accessible form for general inquiries or appointment requests.
5. **Emergency Contact Info:** Prominent section detailing what to do in a dental emergency out of hours.
6. **Parking & Accessibility:** Information on where to park and clinic accessibility features.
