# Layout: Contact Page

## Structural Intent
To make getting in touch as frictionless, professional, and reassuring as possible. It must convey the premium nature of the clinic even in a functional step.

## Visual Rhythm
Functional, split layout. One side focuses on clean data (address, numbers, hours), the other side provides a refined, easy-to-use form. Ample space to prevent a cluttered, stressful feel.

## Sections

1.  **Hero Section:** Title: "Get in Touch." Subtitle: "Schedule a consultation or speak with our care coordinators." Minimalist typographic header.
2.  **The Split Layout (Main Interaction):**
    *   **Left/Top:** Contact details (Phone, Email, WhatsApp). High-contrast typography.
    *   **Right/Bottom:** The Contact Form. Clean inputs, clear labels, subtle focus states (`--color-primary-base`).
3.  **Location Details:** Full address: Sector 21C, Chandigarh. Includes a styled, custom map (desaturated or matching brand colors, avoiding generic Google Maps styling).
4.  **Operating Hours:** A clean table or list showing clinic hours, highlighting any evening or weekend availability.
5.  **Direct Department Contacts:** Specific numbers or emails for different needs (e.g., New Patients, International Coordination, Billing).
6.  **WhatsApp Integration:** A prominent, styled button for immediate WhatsApp communication (crucial for NRI/International clients).
7.  **International Patient Support:** A dedicated block explaining time zone considerations and how to arrange a virtual consultation.
8.  **Parking & Accessibility:** Clear instructions on where to park and details on clinic accessibility for disabled patients.
9.  **Emergency Protocol:** A high-contrast block detailing the process for dental emergencies out of hours.
10. **What Happens Next?:** A brief 3-step timeline explaining what happens after the form is submitted (e.g., "1. We review, 2. Coordinator calls, 3. Appointment set").
11. **Virtual Consultation Option:** Information and a secondary form/link for booking a video call prior to traveling.
12. **Clinic Interior Teaser:** 1-2 high-quality images of the reception/waiting area to reassure the patient of the premium environment they are contacting.
13. **Social Media Links:** Refined icons linking to Instagram, LinkedIn, etc.
14. **Review Us:** A small block linking to Google Reviews or similar platforms.
15. **Careers Link:** A subtle link for professionals looking to contact the clinic for employment.
16. **Privacy Assurance:** A small note near the form assuring users that their medical and personal data is secure.
17. **Referral Program (Optional):** Information for other doctors referring complex cases to The Dental Masters.
18. **Media Inquiries:** Contact details for press or media.
19. **Final Reassurance Quote:** A calming quote about patient care.
20. **Footer:** Standard global footer.
