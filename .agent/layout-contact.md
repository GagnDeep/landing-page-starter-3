# Layout: Contact & Location Page Blueprint

Designed for maximum conversion and easy wayfinding.

1. **Hero Section:** "Get in Touch" or "Visit Us".
2. **Two-Column Layout (Desktop):**
   - **Left:** Contact details (Phone, Email, WhatsApp link), Operating Hours, and Physical Address.
   - **Right:** A clean, minimal contact form (Name, Phone, Service Interest, Message).
3. **Interactive Map:** A stylized embedded map (Google Maps or Mapbox) matching the brand palette.
4. **Parking & Accessibility Info:** Clear instructions on where to park and clinic accessibility.
5. **Emergency Contact Block:** Highlighted section for out-of-hours dental emergencies.
