# Contact Page Layout Intent

**Page Intent:** Make reaching out as easy and accessible as possible.

## Sections
1. **`ContactHero`**: Welcoming header.
2. **`ContactSplitLayout`**:
   - Left: Form to request an appointment or ask a question.
   - Right: Direct contact details (Phone, Email, WhatsApp).
3. **`InteractiveMap`**: Large map showing the exact Sector 35C location with parking details.
4. **`HoursAndEmergencies`**: Clear display of operating hours and emergency contact protocol.
