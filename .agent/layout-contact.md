# Contact Page Layout Blueprint

## Visual Rhythm & Layout Intent
Highly functional, clear, and welcoming.

## Sections
1. **Hero:** "Contact Us" / "Get in Touch".
2. **Contact Information Cards:** Phone numbers, email address, physical address (Sector 21C, Chandigarh).
3. **Working Hours:** Clear table or list of opening times.
4. **Interactive Map:** Embed or high-quality static map with a link to directions.
5. **Contact/Booking Form:** Clean, accessible form for inquiries or appointment requests.
6. **Emergency Dental Care:** Brief section on what to do in an emergency.
