# Contact Page Layout Blueprint

**Goal:** Make it incredibly easy to find the clinic and reach out.

1. **Hero Section:** "We're Here for You" with direct phone numbers prominent.
2. **Contact Form:** Clean, comprehensive form (Name, Email, Phone, Preferred Date, Reason for Visit).
3. **Location & Map:** Large interactive Google Map centered on Leela Bhawan, Patiala.
4. **Clinic Details Card:** Address, Email, Phone, WhatsApp link.
5. **Working Hours:** Clear table of Monday-Sunday timings, noting any closed days.
6. **Emergency Instructions:** What to do outside of working hours.
7. **Parking & Accessibility Info:** Detail wheelchair access and parking spots.
8. **Social Media Links:** Quick connects to IG/FB.
