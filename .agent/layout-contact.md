# Contact Page Layout Blueprint

1. **Global Notice Bar**: Top banner.
2. **Primary Navigation**: Sticky header.
3. **Hero Section**: "Get in Touch" / "We'd Love to Hear from You".
4. **Primary Contact Info Cards**: Three distinct cards for Phone, Email, and Address.
5. **Working Hours / Schedule**: Clear display of opening hours, including weekend availability if any.
6. **Emergency Dental Care Info**: Prominent box detailing what to do in an after-hours emergency.
7. **Contact Form**: Clean, easy-to-use form (Name, Email, Phone, Interest/Message).
8. **Interactive Map**: Large map centered on Sector 22B, Chandigarh.
9. **Parking & Directions Guide**: Specific text on where to park and how to find the clinic entrance.
10. **Public Transit Info**: Closest bus stops or transit options.
11. **Accessibility Information**: Notes on wheelchair access, elevator availability.
12. **"What to Expect" Pre-visit**: Brief note on the check-in process.
13. **New Patient Forms Link**: Prominent link to download or fill out forms beforehand.
14. **Social Media Links Grid**: Follow us on Instagram, Facebook, etc.
15. **WhatsApp Contact Button**: Direct link for WhatsApp messaging, very common in India.
16. **Photo of the Clinic Exterior**: To help patients recognize the building.
17. **Photo of the Reception Area**: To set a welcoming expectation.
18. **Feedback/Complaints Direction**: A polite note on where to send specific feedback.
19. **Book Appointment Direct Link (CTA)**: A distinct button for immediate booking vs general contact.
20. **Global Footer**: Standard footer.
