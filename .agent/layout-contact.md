# Layout Blueprint: Contact Us

**Purpose**: The primary conversion page. Must make it incredibly easy to find the clinic, book an appointment, and get in touch during emergencies.

## Sections

1.  **Global Navigation Bar**: Standard sticky header.
2.  **Page Header (Hero)**: H1 ("Get in Touch"). Friendly, welcoming tone.
3.  **Emergency Contact Banner**: High visibility banner at the top of the content area for immediate assistance.
4.  **Main Layout Container**: 2-column split (Desktop) / Stacked (Mobile). Left: Form. Right: Info.
5.  **Appointment Booking Form**: Comprehensive but clean form. Fields: Name, Phone, Email, Preferred Date, Service Interest, Message.
6.  **Direct Contact Details**: Phone numbers (Reception & Emergency), Email address. Large, clickable typography.
7.  **Location Details**: Full address (Naresh Dental Clinic, Patiala). Directions or landmarks.
8.  **Working Hours Table**: Clear tabular layout showing Monday-Sunday timings.
9.  **Interactive Google Map**: Large embedded map showing the exact location.
10. **"Get Directions" Button**: Direct link to open Google Maps app for navigation.
11. **Parking Information**: Brief note on where patients can park safely.
12. **Accessibility Information**: Note on wheelchair access or ground-floor facilities.
13. **Social Media Links**: Icons linking to Facebook, Instagram, YouTube.
14. **WhatsApp Chat Integration**: Floating or embedded button to start a WhatsApp conversation.
15. **Virtual Tour Teaser**: Small thumbnail linking to a Google 360 view or video of the clinic.
16. **What to Bring**: Checklist for first-time visitors (ID, previous X-rays, medication list).
17. **Response Time Expectation**: Note stating "We aim to reply within X hours".
18. **Secondary Locations (If applicable)**: Info on any branches.
19. **Global Pre-Footer**: Simplified CTA or FAQ link.
20. **Global Footer**: Standard footer.
