# Contact Layout Blueprint

## Section 1: Contact Hero
- **Intent:** "We're here for you." Welcoming, non-intimidating entry point.
- **Rhythm:** Split layout: Text left, welcoming clinic image right.

## Section 2: Emergency Contact Banner
- **Intent:** Immediate visibility for urgent dental issues.
- **Rhythm:** High-contrast strip right below the hero.

## Section 3: The Primary Contact Form
- **Intent:** Easy booking or inquiry. Fields for name, email, phone, and reason for visit.
- **Rhythm:** Clean, floating card on the left side of a grid.

## Section 4: Direct Contact Info (Right side of grid)
- **Intent:** Phone number, email address, WhatsApp link.
- **Rhythm:** Large typography, clickable links.

## Section 5: Interactive Map
- **Intent:** Show exact location in Chandigarh.
- **Rhythm:** Full width or large block container.

## Section 6: Address & Directions
- **Intent:** Text address, parking instructions, landmarks.
- **Rhythm:** Alongside or below the map.

## Section 7: Operating Hours
- **Intent:** Clear display of opening times, noting 24/7 availability for emergencies.
- **Rhythm:** Tabular layout, highlighting current day.

## Section 8: "What happens next?"
- **Intent:** Set expectations after submitting the form (e.g., "We will call you within 2 hours").
- **Rhythm:** 3-step timeline.

## Section 9: Virtual Consultation Option
- **Intent:** Offer a low-friction first step (send a photo of your teeth).
- **Rhythm:** Distinct card with an upload icon.

## Section 10: Social Media Links
- **Intent:** Connect on Instagram, Facebook for more casual interaction.
- **Rhythm:** Row of large icons.

## Section 11: Department Specific Contacts
- **Intent:** Separate numbers/emails for billing vs. appointments.
- **Rhythm:** Simple list.

## Section 12: Accessibility Information
- **Intent:** Detail wheelchair access, elevator availability.
- **Rhythm:** Small icon + text block.

## Section 13: FAQ Teaser
- **Intent:** "Have a quick question? Check our FAQ."
- **Rhythm:** Text link block.

## Section 14: Career Inquiries Note
- **Intent:** Direct job seekers away from the patient booking form.
- **Rhythm:** Small text link to Careers page.

## Section 15: Vendor/Partner Inquiries
- **Intent:** Separate contact flow for B2B.
- **Rhythm:** Small text link or accordion.

## Section 16: Patient Portal Login (If applicable)
- **Intent:** Direct existing patients to their dashboard.
- **Rhythm:** Secondary button.

## Section 17: Clinic Gallery (Small)
- **Intent:** Remind them of the premium environment they are contacting.
- **Rhythm:** 3-image row.

## Section 18: Newsletter Signup
- **Intent:** Secondary capture.
- **Rhythm:** Minimalist inline form.

## Section 19: Privacy Assurance
- **Intent:** Note that medical data sent via form is secure.
- **Rhythm:** Padlock icon, tiny text.

## Section 20: Standard Footer
- **Intent:** Consistent navigation ending.
- **Rhythm:** Inherits global footer.
