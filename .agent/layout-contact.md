# Layout Documentation: Contact Page

## Structural Intent
A mix of utility and atmosphere. It provides necessary contact details and a form, grounded by imagery of the salon location to reinforce the physical reality of the brand.

## Section Details

### 1. Split Layout Hero
- **Layout:** 50/50 split. Left side contains large typography and contact info (address, phone, email, hours). Right side is a high-quality image of the salon facade or interior.
- **Rhythm:** Balanced, informational vs. visual.
- **Intent:** Immediate access to details + visual grounding.

### 2. Contact Form
- **Layout:** Clean, minimalist form fields (using custom inputs without heavy borders).
- **Rhythm:** Structured, airy padding between inputs.
- **Intent:** Low-friction communication.

### 3. Map & Directions
- **Layout:** Full-width or large embedded map focused on the Leela Bhawan, Patiala location.
- **Rhythm:** Utilitarian block.
- **Intent:** Practical navigation aid.
