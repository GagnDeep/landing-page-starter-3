# Layout Blueprint: Contact Page

1. **Header**: Global navigation.
2. **Hero Section**: Title: "Get in Touch." Subtitle: "We're here to help you smile."
3. **Contact Information Grid**: 3 columns: Address (with link to directions), Phone Numbers (General & Emergency), Email Address.
4. **Operating Hours**: Clear schedule, highlighting any special hours.
5. **Interactive Map**: Embedded Google Map showing the Leela Bhawan clinic location.
6. **Contact / Appointment Request Form**: Fields: Name, Phone, Email, Preferred Date/Time, Reason for Visit, Message. Submit button.
7. **Emergency Protocol Reminder**: A small callout below the form advising immediate phone contact for severe emergencies.
8. **Parking & Transit Info**: Text detailing where to park or how to reach via local transit.
9. **Footer**: Global footer.
