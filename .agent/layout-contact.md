# Contact Layout Blueprint

## Goal
Provide a seamless, elegant way for users to reach out, find the clinic, and book appointments.

## Sections
1. **Hero Section:** "Get in Touch."
2. **Contact Information Grid:** Three elegant cards for Phone, Email, and Location/Address.
3. **Interactive Map / Location Details:** High-quality map integration showing the Patiala location, parking info, and landmarks.
4. **Hours of Operation:** Clear, structured list of opening hours.
5. **Contact / Booking Form:** A clean, multi-step or well-organized form collecting Name, Contact Details, Interest (Dental/Aesthetics), and Message. Ensure high contrast and easy tapping on mobile.
6. **Social Links:** Direct links to Instagram, Facebook, etc.
7. **Emergency Protocol:** A small, distinct section outlining what to do in case of a dental emergency.
