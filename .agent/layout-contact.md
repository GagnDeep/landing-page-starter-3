# Layout Blueprint: Contact Us Page

## Structural Slots

1.  **Global Top Bar & Header** (Inherited)
2.  **Page Hero**
    *   *Intent:* "Get in Touch". Welcoming, supportive tone.
3.  **Two-Column Layout: Main Section**
    *   *Column A (Left): Contact Information*
        *   Clinic Address (Chandigarh/Patiala details)
        *   Phone Numbers (Emergency vs. Reception)
        *   Email Address
        *   Working Hours
    *   *Column B (Right): Contact/Booking Form*
        *   Fields: Name, Phone, Service Interest, Preferred Time, Message.
4.  **Interactive Map Embed**
    *   *Intent:* Full-width Google Maps embed showing clinic location and directions link.
5.  **Parking & Accessibility Info**
    *   *Intent:* Practical details for arrival (e.g., "Valet available", "Wheelchair accessible").
6.  **Emergency Protocol Block**
    *   *Intent:* Clear instructions on what to do in a dental emergency after hours.
7.  **Global Footer** (Inherited)
