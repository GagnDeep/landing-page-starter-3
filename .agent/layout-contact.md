# Layout: Contact Page
**Goal:** Make it as easy as possible for users to reach the clinic, especially in emergencies.
**Rhythm:** Direct, actionable, and location-focused.

1. **Global Header (Sticky)**: Standard navigation.
2. **Hero Section**: Title "Contact Us". Subtitle "We're here for you 24/7."
3. **Emergency Callout**: Massive, highly visible block with the 24/7 emergency phone number.
4. **Contact Information Grid**: 3 Cards for Phone, Email, and Address.
5. **Operating Hours**: Clear table showing 24/7 availability for emergencies, and regular consulting hours.
6. **Interactive Map**: Google Maps embed of Sector 21B, Chandigarh location.
7. **Directions & Parking**: Text instructions on how to reach and where to park.
8. **Contact Form**: Fields for Name, Phone, Email, Service Needed, and Message.
9. **Form Submit Button**: Prominent "Send Message" or "Request Callback".
10. **WhatsApp Integration**: Button to start a WhatsApp chat with the front desk.
11. **Social Media Links**: Icons linking to Instagram, Facebook, etc.
12. **FAQ Quick Links**: Links to top 3 questions from the FAQ page.
13. **Patient Testimonial**: A short quote about the helpfulness of the staff.
14. **Accessibility Info**: Details on wheelchair access or other accommodations.
15. **Careers Teaser**: "Looking to join our team?" link.
16. **Secondary Location (if any)**: Information for any other branches.
17. **Teleconsultation Info**: Details on how to book a video consultation.
18. **Newsletter Signup**: Standard footer signup.
19. **Global Footer (Top)**: Standard footer links.
20. **Global Footer (Bottom)**: Legal links.
