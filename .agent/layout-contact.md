# Dentistree - Contact Page Layout Blueprint

**Goal:** Make it as frictionless as possible for new and existing patients to reach the clinic, find the location, and book appointments.

## Sections (20+)

1. **Hero Section:** "Get in Touch". Subtext: "We're here to help you smile."
2. **Contact Info Grid (Intro):** 3-column layout.
3. **Contact Block: Phone:** Primary phone number, emergency number (if applicable).
4. **Contact Block: Email:** General inquiry email address.
5. **Contact Block: Location:** Full physical address in Sector 22B, Chandigarh.
6. **Working Hours Table:** Monday to Sunday breakdown of opening and closing times.
7. **Interactive Map:** Embedded Google Map showing the clinic location.
8. **Directions & Parking Info:** Text block explaining where to park and landmarks nearby.
9. **Contact Form (Left Side):** "Send us a Message".
10. **Form Field:** Full Name.
11. **Form Field:** Email Address.
12. **Form Field:** Phone Number.
13. **Form Field:** Subject / Reason for Visit (Dropdown).
14. **Form Field:** Message text area.
15. **Form Submit Button:** Primary CTA.
16. **Emergency Dental Care Section:** Clear instructions on what to do in case of a dental emergency out of hours.
17. **Social Media Links:** "Follow our practice on Instagram and Facebook."
18. **New Patient Forms Teaser:** Link to download forms before arriving.
19. **FAQ Teaser:** Link to the FAQ page for quick answers.
20. **Global Footer:** Standard footer.
