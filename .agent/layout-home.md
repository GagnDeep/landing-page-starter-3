# Home Page Layout Blueprint: 99 Institute

This document defines exactly 20 distinct sections for the Home Page, establishing the layout intent and visual rhythm.

## 1. Top Announcement Bar
- **Layout Intent:** Slim, full-width banner at the very top.
- **Visual Rhythm:** Simple, single line of text (e.g., "Enrollments open for Fall Mastery Class") with a subtle background color (`Muted / Surface`) to not overpower the navigation.

## 2. Global Navigation Shell
- **Layout Intent:** Sticky header. Logo centered or left-aligned, navigation links spread elegantly.
- **Visual Rhythm:** High contrast on scroll, transparent at the top. Incorporates a distinct CTA button for "Book Salon" or "Join Academy".

## 3. Cinematic Hero Section
- **Layout Intent:** Full viewport height (or min 80vh). High-impact background imagery or subtle video loop (luxury salon/academy vibe).
- **Visual Rhythm:** Large, serif display heading overlaid. Text is centered or strongly left-aligned with a dark/light gradient overlay to ensure text readability. Two primary actions: "Explore Services" and "View Courses".

## 4. Brand Trust & Recognition Bar
- **Layout Intent:** Horizontal strip just below the hero.
- **Visual Rhythm:** Logos of partner brands (e.g., MAC, Huda Beauty) or features in magazines. Monochromatic and slightly faded to convey authority without distraction.

## 5. The 99 Institute Philosophy (Intro)
- **Layout Intent:** Wide, airy section with significant padding (`spacing-xl` or `spacing-2xl`).
- **Visual Rhythm:** A large, evocative quote in the primary serif font. Introduces the dual nature of the brand: Salon & Academy.

## 6. Signature Salon Services Overview
- **Layout Intent:** Grid layout (e.g., 3 columns).
- **Visual Rhythm:** Image-heavy cards with sharp or slightly softened corners. Clean sans-serif titles and a subtle "View Service" text link. Hover effects should be a slow zoom (Ken Burns).

## 7. Featured Before/After Slider
- **Layout Intent:** Split screen or an interactive slider component.
- **Visual Rhythm:** Demonstrates transformation. Clean lines, minimal UI around the slider to keep focus on the artistry.

## 8. The Academy: Master Your Craft
- **Layout Intent:** Asymmetric split layout (e.g., 40% text, 60% imagery).
- **Visual Rhythm:** Large serif heading. Imagery should show students in action, conveying a "student-friendly" but professional environment.

## 9. Course Curriculum Highlights
- **Layout Intent:** Accordion or list-based layout.
- **Visual Rhythm:** Clean, structured. Minimalist design. When an item is expanded, it reveals a small image and brief description.

## 10. Meet the Mentors / Founders
- **Layout Intent:** 2 or 3 column grid for profiles.
- **Visual Rhythm:** Editorial portrait photography. Deep, rich background (`Primary` or `Muted Dark`) to make the section feel like a glossy magazine feature.

## 11. Student Success Stories (Testimonials)
- **Layout Intent:** Carousel or masonry grid of cards.
- **Visual Rhythm:** Emphasize the text (serif for the quote, sans-serif for the author). Include small, circular avatars.

## 12. Video Feature / Masterclass Teaser
- **Layout Intent:** Full-width video player or a large 16:9 thumbnail with a play button overlay.
- **Visual Rhythm:** Dark overlay to make the play button pop. Evokes a high-production value, masterclass feel.

## 13. The Salon Experience (Atmosphere)
- **Layout Intent:** Collage or masonry gallery of the physical salon space.
- **Visual Rhythm:** Focus on interior design, lighting, and luxury. Captures the "premium" aspect of the brand.

## 14. Pricing Tiers / Packages (Academy or Salon)
- **Layout Intent:** 3-column pricing table layout.
- **Visual Rhythm:** The middle tier is slightly elevated (using the `Elevated` shadow/border). Clear, geometric sans-serif for pricing numbers.

## 15. Upcoming Masterclasses & Events
- **Layout Intent:** List format with distinct date blocks.
- **Visual Rhythm:** Date on the left (bold sans-serif), event details in the middle, and a "Register" button on the right.

## 16. The Beauty Blog / Articles
- **Layout Intent:** 3-column card grid for recent posts.
- **Visual Rhythm:** Image on top, subtle date/category overline, serif title. Minimal text preview.

## 17. FAQ Section
- **Layout Intent:** Centered, narrow column with an accordion UI.
- **Visual Rhythm:** Structured, highly readable. Focus on "student-friendly" clarity.

## 18. Instagram / Social Integration
- **Layout Intent:** Full-width grid of square images (e.g., 4 or 6 columns).
- **Visual Rhythm:** Seamless, no gaps between images to create a rich tapestry of artistry. A central overlay button: "Follow @99Institute".

## 19. Pre-Footer / Final CTA
- **Layout Intent:** Large, bold block of color (using `Primary Brand` color).
- **Visual Rhythm:** High contrast. A final push for enrollment or booking. "Ready to transform your career?"

## 20. Global Footer
- **Layout Intent:** 4-column multi-link layout.
- **Visual Rhythm:** Dark background (even in light mode) for a strong grounding effect. Clean sans-serif links, newsletter signup input with a sleek, minimal button.
