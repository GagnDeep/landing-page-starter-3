# Spacedent Home Page Layout

The Home Page acts as the front door for Spacedent, designed to build trust immediately. It must feel welcoming, modern, and highlight both their pediatric expertise and tech-forward treatments.

## Structural Blueprint (20+ Sections)

1. **Top Announcement Bar:** Subtle background. Content: Emergency contact info or special clinic hours.
2. **Global Navigation:** Clean header with logo (left), primary links (center), and "Book Appointment" CTA (right). Sticky on scroll.
3. **Hero Section:** Asymmetric layout. Left: Compelling, trustworthy headline with fluid typography. Right: High-quality, warm image of a pediatric or general patient interaction. Soft teal accents.
4. **Trust Indicators (Social Proof):** Horizontal scrolling or simple row of metrics (e.g., "5.0★ Google Rating", "10,000+ Smiles Transformed", "Expert Team").
5. **Introduction / Philosophy:** Short, readable text block introducing Dr. Vikramjit & Dr. Arshiya's philosophy of gentle, modern care.
6. **Core Services Teaser Grid:** An irregular grid (breaking the standard card layout) highlighting: Pediatric Dentistry, Single-Sitting Root Canal, Implants, Orthodontics.
7. **Featured Service Spotlight (Pediatric):** A dedicated, warm section detailing their gentle approach for kids. Image-heavy.
8. **Featured Service Spotlight (Tech/Advanced):** Highlighting tech-forward treatments like Single-Sitting Root Canals. Focus on "pain-free" and "efficient".
9. **Patient Testimonial Carousel:** Minimalist, card-based carousel featuring real reviews. Focus on readability.
10. **The Clinic Experience (Gallery/Video Teaser):** A section that "shows, doesn't tell" the relaxing, modern clinic environment.
11. **Meet the Doctors Teaser:** Photos and brief bios of Dr. Vikramjit and Dr. Arshiya. Link to full About page.
12. **Why Choose Us (Differentiators):** Icon-text pairings (e.g., "Pain-Free Approach", "Advanced Technology", "Family Friendly", "Transparent Pricing").
13. **Technology Highlight:** A section demonstrating the modern equipment used (e.g., Digital X-Rays, Intraoral Cameras) to build authority.
14. **Interactive Treatment Journey:** A step-by-step visual of what a new patient can expect from their first visit.
15. **Insurance & Financing Info:** Clear, reassuring block about accepted insurances and payment plans to reduce friction.
16. **FAQ Accordion:** Top 4-5 common questions (e.g., "Do root canals hurt?", "When should my child first visit the dentist?").
17. **Location & Contact Block:** Map integration, clinic address, phone number, and operating hours. Left-aligned text, asymmetric map visual.
18. **Final CTA Section:** High-contrast block. "Ready for a Brighter Smile?" -> Large primary button for booking.
19. **Newsletter / Updates Signup:** Optional soft section for staying in touch (dental tips).
20. **Global Footer:** Comprehensive footer with secondary links, social icons, legal links, and address.
