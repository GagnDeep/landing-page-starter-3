# Home Page Layout Architecture

## Overview
The Home page serves as the digital front door for Boho Salon Chandigarh. The visual rhythm is designed to slowly draw the user in, alternating between high-impact imagery and breathable, elegant typography, capturing both the boho aesthetic and a premium salon experience.

## Section 1: Top Navigation Bar (`Global Nav`)
- **Intent:** Unobtrusive, sticky access to pages and booking.
- **Rhythm:** Transparent on hero, solid background on scroll. Centered logo, subtle links.

## Section 2: Hero Section (`Hero`)
- **Intent:** Establish the mood instantly.
- **Rhythm:** Full-bleed image or video background featuring elegant styling (e.g., curly hair flowing). Large, centered editorial typography. Prominent primary Call-to-Action (CTA) for booking.

## Section 3: Brand Welcome (`Introduction`)
- **Intent:** Introduce the salon's ethos and its Chandigarh/NRI focus.
- **Rhythm:** Generous whitespace. Centered text with a high-contrast serif heading. Brief, poetic paragraph.

## Section 4: Signature Services Teaser (`Services Overview`)
- **Intent:** Quick glance at what the salon is known for.
- **Rhythm:** 2x2 or 3-column grid of elegant cards, featuring minimal icons or high-quality close-up photos (e.g., chrome gel nails).

## Section 5: The Boho Experience (`Philosophy`)
- **Intent:** Explain the boho-modern, relaxing vibe.
- **Rhythm:** Asymmetric layout. Text on the left, overlapping images of the salon interior on the right.

## Section 6: Featured Service: Nail Art (`Spotlight: Nails`)
- **Intent:** Highlight the chrome gel nails expertise.
- **Rhythm:** Full-width section with a muted background color. Large image carousel or masonry grid of nail designs.

## Section 7: Featured Service: Hair (`Spotlight: Hair`)
- **Intent:** Highlight the advanced curly haircuts.
- **Rhythm:** Alternating from the previous section. Image on the left, detailed description and booking link on the right.

## Section 8: Testimonials (`Social Proof`)
- **Intent:** Build trust through client reviews.
- **Rhythm:** Horizontal scrolling carousel. Elegant quotation marks, clean typography for the review and client name.

## Section 9: Before & After (`Transformation`)
- **Intent:** Visual proof of expertise.
- **Rhythm:** Interactive slider or split-screen showing a stark transformation, focusing on hair or nails.

## Section 10: Meet the Experts (`Team Teaser`)
- **Intent:** Personalize the brand.
- **Rhythm:** 3-column grid featuring styled, warm portraits of the lead stylists/artists.

## Section 11: Promotional Banner (`Current Offer`)
- **Intent:** Drive immediate conversions for seasonal or first-time offers.
- **Rhythm:** Edge-to-edge block of solid primary color. Large, bold text with a contrasting button.

## Section 12: Salon Ambiance (`Gallery`)
- **Intent:** Showcase the physical location in Sector 17.
- **Rhythm:** Masonry layout of 4-6 high-quality interior shots, emphasizing earthy tones and natural light.

## Section 13: Premium Products (`Retail Teaser`)
- **Intent:** Upsell curated products used in-house.
- **Rhythm:** Carousel of product bottles beautifully lit against warm backgrounds.

## Section 14: Value Proposition (`Why Choose Us`)
- **Intent:** Reinforce quality and hygiene.
- **Rhythm:** 3-column text-centric layout with minimalist icons (e.g., Premium Products, Strict Hygiene, Expert Stylists).

## Section 15: Membership / Loyalty (`VIP Program`)
- **Intent:** Encourage recurring visits.
- **Rhythm:** Elegant card layout centered on the screen, detailing perks of the VIP program.

## Section 16: As Seen In / Press (`Authority`)
- **Intent:** Establish prestige.
- **Rhythm:** Simple, centered row of muted logos from local or national publications.

## Section 17: Instagram Feed (`Social Teaser`)
- **Intent:** Keep the site feeling fresh and connect users to social media.
- **Rhythm:** 4x1 grid of square images pulling from the IG feed.

## Section 18: Newsletter Signup (`Lead Capture`)
- **Intent:** Capture emails for marketing.
- **Rhythm:** Clean, centered input field on a muted background block.

## Section 19: Location & Hours (`Visit Us`)
- **Intent:** Practical information for visiting.
- **Rhythm:** Split layout: stylized map on one side, address (Sector 17, Chandigarh) and opening hours on the other.

## Section 20: Global Footer (`Footer`)
- **Intent:** Secondary navigation, legal, and final contacts.
- **Rhythm:** Dark or muted background. Multi-column links, social icons, and copyright details.
