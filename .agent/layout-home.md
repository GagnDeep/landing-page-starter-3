# Layout Documentation: Home Page

## Structural Intent
The home page is designed as a digital front door that feels like stepping into a high-end salon. The rhythm alternates between immersive, high-impact visual sections and structured, informative grids. Asymmetry and generous whitespace are used to create an editorial layout.

## Section Details

### 1. Navigation / Header
- **Layout:** Sticky or fixed header, minimalist. Left-aligned logo, right-aligned navigation links and CTA.
- **Rhythm:** Tight padding, sharp contrast against the hero image.
- **Intent:** Unobtrusive but always accessible.

### 2. Hero Section
- **Layout:** Full viewport height (`100vh`), split-screen or large overarching image with an overlapping content card. High-impact typography (Cormorant Garamond).
- **Rhythm:** Expansive. The focus is entirely on a single powerful image and an elegant headline.
- **Intent:** Establish the "affordable luxury" and "premium salon" vibe immediately.

### 3. Trust Bar / Accolades
- **Layout:** A subtle, horizontally scrolling or evenly spaced row of badges, awards, or "featured in" logos.
- **Rhythm:** Compact, muted contrast.
- **Intent:** Immediate social proof before any claims are made.

### 4. Value Proposition
- **Layout:** Centered, narrow max-width text block with a large, statement serif headline.
- **Rhythm:** Generous top and bottom padding (`py-24` or `py-32`).
- **Intent:** A quiet, confident moment explaining the "affordable luxury" promise.

### 5. Services Teaser (Horizontal Scroll/Grid)
- **Layout:** A staggered grid or horizontal scrolling section featuring 3-4 key services (Botox, Keratin, Color).
- **Rhythm:** Asymmetrical. Large images paired with succinct descriptions.
- **Intent:** Showcase expertise visually without overwhelming the user with a full menu.

### 6. Feature Spotlight (The Transformative Treatment)
- **Layout:** 50/50 split layout. Image on one side (perhaps a subtle before/after or just a stunning result), typography and details on the other.
- **Rhythm:** Balanced, structured.
- **Intent:** Deep dive into a signature service.

### 7. Feature Highlights (Expertise & Science)
- **Layout:** A 3-column grid of small, elegant cards or simple icon-and-text lockups.
- **Rhythm:** Uniform, orderly.
- **Intent:** Highlight the rigorous, expert-led nature of the salon (products used, techniques, hygiene).

### 8. Ajay Sir / Expert Profile
- **Layout:** Editorial layout. A high-quality portrait overlapping a solid color block, with a bio and credentials alongside.
- **Rhythm:** Broken grid, overlapping elements to create depth.
- **Intent:** Personalize the brand, building trust in the leadership and academy aspect.

### 9. Social Proof / Testimonials
- **Layout:** A masonry grid of high-contrast typography quotes, or a large, single-quote carousel.
- **Rhythm:** Spacious, text-heavy but clean.
- **Intent:** Authentic voices reinforcing the premium experience.

### 10. Video / Immersive Experience (The Vibe)
- **Layout:** Full-width container with a silent, auto-playing video loop (or static image if video isn't available) capturing the salon atmosphere.
- **Rhythm:** Expansive, visually dominant.
- **Intent:** Sell the feeling of being in the space.

### 11. "How It Works" / Consultation Process
- **Layout:** A numbered list or stepped vertical timeline.
- **Rhythm:** Linear, predictable, reassuring.
- **Intent:** Demystify the process and lower the barrier to booking.

### 12. Academy / Education Teaser
- **Layout:** Dark mode inverted section (if the rest is light) or a distinct background color. 2-column layout highlighting courses.
- **Rhythm:** High contrast, structured.
- **Intent:** Introduce the educational branch of the business.

### 13. Pricing Teaser (Transparent Luxury)
- **Layout:** Minimalist table or list of 3-4 starting prices for key services.
- **Rhythm:** Clean, tabular, easy to scan.
- **Intent:** Anchor expectations and prove the "affordable" part of "affordable luxury."

### 14. Partner Logos / Brands Used
- **Layout:** A clean, muted grid of brand logos (e.g., L'Oréal, Olaplex).
- **Rhythm:** Compact, uniform.
- **Intent:** Borrowed authority through premium product associations.

### 15. Before & After Interactive Element
- **Layout:** A slider component comparing a hair transformation.
- **Rhythm:** Contained, interactive.
- **Intent:** Visual proof of transformative treatments.

### 16. Location & Atmosphere
- **Layout:** Map integration alongside interior shots of the Leela Bhawan, Patiala salon.
- **Rhythm:** Practical, grid-based.
- **Intent:** Ground the digital experience in a physical, luxurious location.

### 17. Blog / Editorial Preview
- **Layout:** A 2 or 3-column grid of article cards (tips, trends, maintenance advice).
- **Rhythm:** Editorial, magazine-like flow.
- **Intent:** Position the brand as a thought leader in hair care.

### 18. FAQ Accordion
- **Layout:** A narrow, centered accordion list.
- **Rhythm:** Dense text, clean interactive toggles.
- **Intent:** Address objections and common queries efficiently.

### 19. Newsletter / Insider List
- **Layout:** A simple, elegant input field centered on an accent background.
- **Rhythm:** Contained, focused.
- **Intent:** Capture leads for ongoing marketing.

### 20. Final CTA (Book Now)
- **Layout:** Massive typography, full-width section with a striking background image or deep brand color. Large primary button.
- **Rhythm:** Bold, unmissable.
- **Intent:** The ultimate conversion point.

### 21. Footer
- **Layout:** Multi-column (links, contact info, socials, legal).
- **Rhythm:** Dense but organized, muted typography.
- **Intent:** Structural wrap-up.
