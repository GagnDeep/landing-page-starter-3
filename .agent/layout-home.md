# Layout Documentation: Home Page

## 1. Top Navigation Bar (Sticky/Transparent)
- **Intent**: Elegant entry point. Seamlessly integrates with the hero section when at the top, becoming a solid, softly blurred background on scroll.
- **Rhythm**: Logo left (serif, elegant), Navigation links center (sans-serif, spaced), "Book Appointment" CTA right (primary button).

## 2. Hero Section (Immersive Full Bleed)
- **Intent**: Establish the "at-home luxury" vibe immediately. Evoke a feeling of stepping into a calm, premium sanctuary.
- **Rhythm**: Large, elegant serif headline (e.g., "The Salon Experience, Refined for Your Home"), secondary sans-serif subhead, primary CTA ("Book Your Session"), and a soft, high-quality ambient background image or slow-moving ambient video loop.

## 3. Brand Proposition / Introduction
- **Intent**: Explain the core concept smoothly—luxury service without leaving home.
- **Rhythm**: Centered serif heading, short descriptive text, generous padding (`2xl` spacing). Subtle entry animation on scroll.

## 4. Service Categories Preview (Grid)
- **Intent**: Showcase the main pillars of service (e.g., Hair, Skin, Nails, Spa).
- **Rhythm**: 2x2 or 4-column grid depending on viewport. Each card features a high-quality detail image, a serif title, and a subtle hover effect (slight lift, soft shadow).

## 5. The Experience / "How It Works"
- **Intent**: Clarify the seamless process of booking and receiving at-home services.
- **Rhythm**: 3-step horizontal flow (or vertical on mobile). Icons or numbers with short, clear text. "Book" -> "We Arrive" -> "You Relax".

## 6. Featured Stylists / Professionals
- **Intent**: Build trust by showcasing the expertise and premium nature of the staff.
- **Rhythm**: Carousel or row of stylized portraits. Names in serif, specialties in sans-serif.

## 7. Signature Packages (Highlight)
- **Intent**: Drive conversions to high-value, curated experiences (e.g., "Bridal Prep", "Sunday Unwind").
- **Rhythm**: Distinct background color (e.g., `muted` or subtle `secondary` tint). Large cards highlighting package benefits.

## 8. Client Testimonials (Social Proof)
- **Intent**: Establish local credibility in Chandigarh.
- **Rhythm**: Elegant, minimalist quotes centered. Soft, fading transitions between reviews.

## 9. Before & After / Portfolio Snippet
- **Intent**: Visual proof of quality.
- **Rhythm**: Interactive slider or a clean 2-column image layout showing transformations.

## 10. The Products We Use
- **Intent**: Reinforce the "premium" aspect by listing high-end partner brands.
- **Rhythm**: A subtle, greyscale logo marquee or a clean grid of product imagery.

## 11. Membership / Loyalty Program
- **Intent**: Encourage recurring bookings.
- **Rhythm**: Split layout (text left, image right). CTA to "Join the Club".

## 12. Gift Cards / Gifting
- **Intent**: Offer a secondary conversion path.
- **Rhythm**: Visually distinct section, perhaps dark mode inverted, focusing on the joy of giving an experience.

## 13. Service Area Map / Availability
- **Intent**: Confirm they serve the user's location (Chandigarh specific).
- **Rhythm**: Minimalist map or a clean text list of neighborhoods served.

## 14. Safety & Hygiene Standards
- **Intent**: Overcome a key objection to at-home services.
- **Rhythm**: Icon-driven list (e.g., sanitized kits, masked professionals, disposable tools).

## 15. Latest from the Blog / Beauty Tips
- **Intent**: SEO value and establish authority in the beauty space.
- **Rhythm**: 3-column card layout with elegant thumbnail images and short read times.

## 16. FAQs (Quick Answers)
- **Intent**: Remove friction before booking.
- **Rhythm**: Clean accordion layout. Only top 3-4 questions, with a link to the full FAQ page.

## 17. Newsletter Signup
- **Intent**: Capture leads who aren't ready to book yet.
- **Rhythm**: Simple input field and button, centered layout. "Get exclusive tips and offers."

## 18. Final CTA (Full Width)
- **Intent**: The ultimate push to book after consuming the page content.
- **Rhythm**: Large, immersive background block. Large serif text, prominent primary button.

## 19. Instagram / Social Feed
- **Intent**: Show active, modern brand presence.
- **Rhythm**: Edge-to-edge grid of latest Instagram posts, "Follow Us @YourHomeSalon".

## 20. Footer (Comprehensive)
- **Intent**: Secondary navigation, legal, and contact information.
- **Rhythm**: Multi-column layout. Logo, quick links, contact details, social icons, and copyright text at the bottom.
