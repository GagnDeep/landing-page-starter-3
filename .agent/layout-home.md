# Layout: Home Page
**Goal:** Establish trust immediately, highlight 24-hour availability, and guide users to booking or learning more.
**Rhythm:** Alternating calm expansive spaces with focused interactive zones.

1. **Global Header (Sticky)**: Logo left, nav center, "Book Appointment" CTA right.
2. **Hero Section**: Large welcoming image of the clinic/doctor, bold headline (Fraunces), primary CTA ("Book Now"), secondary CTA ("Our Services").
3. **Emergency Banner**: High-contrast bar below hero highlighting "24/7 Emergency Dental Care" with a phone number.
4. **Trust Indicators (Logos)**: Row of awards, affiliations, or "As seen in" logos.
5. **Introduction / Welcome**: Two-column layout. Left: Warm message from Dr. Kochar. Right: High-quality image of Dr. Kochar with a patient.
6. **Core Services Overview (Grid)**: 3-4 cards highlighting key services (General, Cosmetic, Orthodontics, Emergency) with icons and "Learn More" links.
7. **The "Why Choose Us" Section**: Soft background color (`--color-surface-cream`). Bulleted points focusing on technology, compassion, and experience.
8. **Patient Testimonials (Carousel)**: Cards with quotes, patient names, and star ratings. Focus on different demographics (elderly, families).
9. **Before & After Teaser**: Slider or side-by-side images showing successful treatments.
10. **Clinic Tour Video/Image Gallery**: Large visual area showing the clean, welcoming interior to reduce anxiety.
11. **Meet the Team Teaser**: Quick intro to the staff, linking to the full About page.
12. **Technology Highlight**: Tech-focused section (e.g., "Painless Dentistry with Laser Tech").
13. **Insurance & Financing Options**: Clear icons and brief text explaining accepted insurances and payment plans.
14. **Location & Hours**: Map integration, address, and clear operating hours (emphasizing 24/7).
15. **Recent Blog Posts**: 3 up-to-date articles on dental health.
16. **FAQ Accordion**: 3-4 common questions (e.g., "Do you accept walk-ins?").
17. **Call to Action (Final Push)**: Large, inviting section. "Ready for a Brighter Smile?" with a prominent booking button.
18. **Newsletter Signup**: Simple input field for health tips.
19. **Global Footer (Top Section)**: Links to all pages, contact info, social icons.
20. **Global Footer (Bottom Section)**: Copyright, Privacy Policy, Terms of Service links.
