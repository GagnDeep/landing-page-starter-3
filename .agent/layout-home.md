# Layout: Home Page Blueprint

This document defines the structural rhythm and visual pacing for the Home Page, establishing a premium, high-end "Clinic-as-Studio" feel. It consists of 20 distinct sections.

1. **Global Header (Navigation):** Transparent on load, blurring/solidifying on scroll. Minimal, elegant typography. Clean CTAs ("Book Appointment", "Call Now").
2. **Hero Section (The First Impression):** Large atmospheric imagery or subtle video loop (hygienic, bright, modern clinic space). Bold serif headline, clean sans-serif subheadline. Primary booking CTA.
3. **Trust & Authority Bar:** Subtle banner featuring "5.0-Star Rated", "20+ Years Experience", "Pain-Free Technology". Minimal icons.
4. **Practice Philosophy:** A clean, wide-open text section. Focus on the core mission—expert, painless care in a serene environment. High-contrast typography.
5. **Wait Time Status (Live Feel):** A small, elegant UI component simulating live clinic status (e.g., "Current Wait Time: < 5 mins"). Adds modern, premium operational transparency.
6. **Core Services Grid (The Expertise):** A meticulously aligned bento-box or classic 3-column grid showcasing primary services (Implants, Aesthetics, General). Soft hover states.
7. **Signature Service Highlight (e.g., Painless Implants):** Full-width section with deep navy/charcoal background (Dark mode feel even in light mode). Highlighting a premium service with striking photography.
8. **Doctor Profiles (The Experts):** Elegant portrait photography, clean typography for credentials. Highlighting authority and warmth.
9. **Patient Reviews (The Proof):** A refined carousel or masonry layout of testimonials. Use serif for quotes to feel like editorial pull-quotes.
10. **Before & After Gallery Preview:** A clean, subtle slider or side-by-side view. Must not feel gross or overly clinical; needs a lifestyle/studio presentation.
11. **Technology & Hygiene (The Standard):** Focus on sterile, advanced tech. Minimalist icons, ample white space, mint/sage accents to signify cleanliness and calm.
12. **Virtual Tour Teaser:** A panoramic image or clean video thumbnail inviting users to "See the Clinic".
13. **Insurance & Financing:** Clean, structured list or logos of accepted partners. Focus on accessibility without losing the premium feel.
14. **Location & Atmosphere:** High-quality exterior/interior shot. Address, embedded stylized map (desaturated or matching brand colors).
15. **FAQ (The Assurances):** Clean accordion layout. Focus on common anxieties (pain, cost, time).
16. **Blog/Articles Teaser (The Knowledge):** 3-column layout of recent insights. Editorial style—looks like a high-end magazine.
17. **Call to Action (The Final Push):** Large, inviting section. Warm background color. "Ready for your new smile?"
18. **Newsletter/Updates:** Minimalist input field. "Join our community for dental health tips."
19. **Global Footer:** Comprehensive but uncluttered. Links, contact info, legal, social icons.
20. **Floating Action Button (Mobile Only):** Persistent "Book Now" or WhatsApp icon for immediate conversion.
