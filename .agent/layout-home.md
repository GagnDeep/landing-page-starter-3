# Layout Blueprint: Home Page
**Status:** Defined (Phase 1)
**Vibe:** Welcome, Trust, Expertise, Neighborhood Comfort

## Sections (Top to Bottom)

1.  **Global Navigation (Header):**
    - Intent: Sticky header for easy access to booking and key pages.
    - Components: Logo (left), Links (center), "Book Appointment" CTA (right), Mobile Menu trigger.
2.  **Hero Section:**
    - Intent: Immediate warm welcome, clear value proposition, and primary action.
    - Layout: Split or overlaid. Soft imagery of the clinic/smile, large welcoming headline, dual CTAs ("Book Now", "View Services").
3.  **Trust Bar (Social Proof):**
    - Intent: Establish immediate credibility.
    - Layout: Horizontal strip displaying "5.0 Stars", "Google Reviews", years in practice, or key affiliations.
4.  **Clinic Philosophy / Intro:**
    - Intent: Brief introduction to the "neighbourhood clinic" vibe.
    - Layout: Centered text block, perhaps with a soft signature or doctor's photo.
5.  **Service Spotlight - General Dentistry:**
    - Intent: Highlight core bread-and-butter services (checkups, cleanings).
    - Layout: Image + Text block (alternating sides for rhythm).
6.  **Service Spotlight - Cosmetic Dentistry:**
    - Intent: Highlight aesthetic services (whitening, veneers).
    - Layout: Text + Image block.
7.  **Service Spotlight - Restorative Dentistry:**
    - Intent: Highlight complex services (implants, crowns).
    - Layout: Image + Text block.
8.  **Why Choose Us (Features Grid):**
    - Intent: Differentiate based on comfort and technology.
    - Layout: 3 or 4 column grid of cards (e.g., "Gentle Care", "Modern Tech", "Flexible Scheduling"). Icons + short text.
9.  **Doctor Profile Snippet (Dr. Bansal):**
    - Intent: Personalize the practice.
    - Layout: Portrait photo next to a short bio snippet and a link to the full About page.
10. **Patient Testimonials Carousel:**
    - Intent: Build emotional trust through patient stories.
    - Layout: Horizontal scrolling or fading carousel of quote cards.
11. **The Clinic Experience (Visual Tour):**
    - Intent: Show the calm, clean environment.
    - Layout: Masonry or grid gallery of 3-4 high-quality interior photos.
12. **Insurance & Payment Options:**
    - Intent: Remove financial anxiety.
    - Layout: Simple banner or grid listing accepted insurances or financing options.
13. **Technology Highlight:**
    - Intent: Reassure patients about modern, painless procedures.
    - Layout: Split section; photo of equipment/screen + explanation of benefits.
14. **Emergency Dental Banner:**
    - Intent: Provide immediate help for urgent cases.
    - Layout: Distinctive (maybe slightly accented background) banner with a clear "Call Now" CTA.
15. **Recent Blog/Advice Snippets:**
    - Intent: Show ongoing expertise and patient education.
    - Layout: 3-column card grid of recent articles (e.g., "Tips for brushing").
16. **FAQ Snippet:**
    - Intent: Address immediate common questions.
    - Layout: Accordion list of top 3-4 questions. Link to full FAQ.
17. **Location & Hours:**
    - Intent: Practical information for finding the clinic.
    - Layout: Split view; Map/Address on one side, Opening Hours on the other.
18. **Final CTA (Book Appointment):**
    - Intent: Capture users who have scrolled to the bottom.
    - Layout: Large, centered block with a strong heading and prominent CTA button.
19. **Newsletter / Updates Sign-up:**
    - Intent: Capture emails for soft leads.
    - Layout: Simple input field + submit button.
20. **Global Footer:**
    - Intent: Secondary navigation, legal links, full contact details.
    - Layout: Multi-column layout (Contact, Quick Links, Services, Legal) + Copyright bar.
