# Home Page Layout Blueprint

**Goal:** Establish immediate trust and convey high-end medical authority alongside a welcoming patient experience.
**Sections:** (20 total to ensure a comprehensive, rhythmic flow)

1. **Hero Header:**
   - **Intent:** Immediate visual impact.
   - **Elements:** Large headline (Lora), subheadline (Manrope), primary CTA ("Book Appointment"), secondary CTA ("Our Services"), background ambient clinical visual or high-quality clinic image.

2. **Trust Bar / As Seen In:**
   - **Intent:** Social proof and authority right after the fold.
   - **Elements:** Logos of certifications, affiliations (e.g., ADA, ISO), or local recognition.

3. **Value Proposition (The Naresh Difference):**
   - **Intent:** Clear statement of why choose this clinic.
   - **Elements:** 3-column layout highlighting Expertise, Hygiene, and Advanced Technology.

4. **Meet Dr. Anshum Gupta (Founder/Lead):**
   - **Intent:** Personal connection and professional authority.
   - **Elements:** Professional headshot, brief bio highlighting qualifications, signature, link to full About page.

5. **Services Overview - Highlight 1 (Dental Implants):**
   - **Intent:** Focus on a high-value, specialized service.
   - **Elements:** Alternating text/image layout. Deep dive into implant expertise.

6. **Services Overview - Highlight 2 (Cosmetic Dentistry):**
   - **Intent:** Appeal to aesthetic desires.
   - **Elements:** Before/After slider or high-quality imagery of smile makeovers.

7. **Services Grid:**
   - **Intent:** Show breadth of services.
   - **Elements:** Grid of cards (General, Orthodontics, Pediatric, Root Canal, etc.) with icons and brief descriptions.

8. **Patient Testimonials (Video/Quote Carousel):**
   - **Intent:** Emotional connection and proof of satisfaction.
   - **Elements:** Carousel of patient stories, focusing on transformation and comfort.

9. **Technology & Hygiene Standards:**
   - **Intent:** Reassure patients about safety and modern care.
   - **Elements:** Icons/illustrations of sterilization protocols, 3D imaging, laser dentistry.

10. **Virtual Tour Teaser:**
    - **Intent:** Demystify the clinic environment.
    - **Elements:** Video thumbnail or auto-playing muted video of the clean, modern clinic interior.

11. **Statistics / Milestones:**
    - **Intent:** Quantifiable success.
    - **Elements:** Animated counters (e.g., "10,000+ Smiles Restored", "15+ Years Experience").

12. **Insurance & Financing Options:**
    - **Intent:** Overcome cost objections.
    - **Elements:** Logos of accepted insurances, mention of EMI or financing partners.

13. **Step-by-Step Patient Journey:**
    - **Intent:** Set expectations for new patients.
    - **Elements:** Horizontal timeline or step cards (Consultation -> Plan -> Treatment -> Aftercare).

14. **Emergency Dental Care Banner:**
    - **Intent:** Urgent call to action for immediate needs.
    - **Elements:** Distinct background color (destructive or accent), bold text, immediate contact number.

15. **Interactive Smile Assessment Quiz Teaser:**
    - **Intent:** Lead generation.
    - **Elements:** Brief prompt asking users about their smile goals, leading to a form.

16. **Latest from the Blog (Patient Education):**
    - **Intent:** Establish thought leadership.
    - **Elements:** 3 recent blog post cards with titles and read times.

17. **FAQ Highlights:**
    - **Intent:** Address common hesitations immediately.
    - **Elements:** Accordion with 4-5 top questions (e.g., "Does it hurt?", "How long do implants last?").

18. **Location & Contact Info:**
    - **Intent:** Practical logistics.
    - **Elements:** Map integration, address details (Patiala), clinic hours, contact details.

19. **Final Global CTA:**
    - **Intent:** Capture users who have scrolled to the bottom.
    - **Elements:** Strong headline, "Schedule Your Visit" button, visually distinct container.

20. **Footer (Global Component Teaser):**
    - **Intent:** Navigation and legal links.
    - **Elements:** Sitemaps, social links, legal notices.
