# Layout Blueprint: Home Page

1. **Global Header**: Logo (left), Navigation Links (center - Home, About, Services, Patient Info), Primary CTA (right - "Book Appointment"). Transparent on load, solid on scroll.
2. **Hero Section**: Full-height or large format. Soft, warm lifestyle image of a healthy smile or clinic interior. Large `Lora` heading: "Calm, Comprehensive Dental Care in Patiala." Subheading in `Plus Jakarta Sans`. Primary CTA ("Schedule a Visit") and Secondary CTA ("Meet Dr. Bansal").
3. **Trust Indicators (Social Proof / Badges)**: A horizontal band directly below the hero. "Serving Patiala for X Years", "5-Star Rated", Affiliations/Certifications.
4. **Welcome / Philosophy Intro**: A split-screen layout. Left: Soft image of Dr. Bansal or the clinic. Right: A warm welcoming message emphasizing gentle care and community focus.
5. **Core Services Grid (High Level)**: A 3 or 4-column grid highlighting main service pillars (e.g., General Dentistry, Cosmetic Smiles, Orthodontics, Pediatric Care). Minimalist icons or soft photography.
6. **The "Calm Clinic" Difference (Feature List)**: Why choose us? Bullet points or small cards emphasizing anxiety-free environment, modern technology, transparent pricing, and gentle techniques.
7. **Patient Testimonials (Carousel or Masonry)**: Real stories from patients. Focus on feelings of comfort and excellent results.
8. **Dr. Bansal Profile Snippet**: A dedicated spotlight on the lead dentist. Brief bio, credentials, and a quote about patient care. Link to full About page.
9. **Tour the Clinic (Visual Gallery)**: A horizontal scroll or grid showing the clean, welcoming reception, state-of-the-art treatment rooms, and sterilization standards.
10. **Technology & Comforts**: Highlighting specific tech (e.g., Digital X-rays, painless injection wands) and comforts (e.g., noise-canceling headphones, soothing music).
11. **New Patient Special / Offer**: A prominent callout box with a gentle colored background (using the secondary teal). E.g., "Comprehensive New Patient Exam & X-Rays."
12. **Insurance & Financing Preview**: Brief mention of accepted insurances and flexible payment plans to reassure patients about affordability.
13. **Before & After Teaser**: A subtle, non-graphic slider showing one or two transformative smile makeovers. Link to full gallery.
14. **Emergency Dental Care Callout**: Distinct styling (perhaps using the muted destructive tone). "Tooth pain? We offer same-day emergency appointments."
15. **Educational Content / Blog Teaser**: 3 recent articles. E.g., "How to overcome dental anxiety", "The importance of biannual cleanings".
16. **Location & Accessibility Info**: Map snippet, address (Leela Bhawan, Patiala), parking availability, and wheelchair accessibility details.
17. **Operating Hours**: Clear, easy-to-read table of opening times, including any evening or weekend availability.
18. **FAQ Accordion (Top 3-5 Questions)**: Quick answers to common questions (e.g., "Do you accept my insurance?", "What happens on my first visit?").
19. **Final Grand CTA Section**: A large, soft-colored block reinforcing the core message. "Ready for a healthier smile? Let's take care of you." Large "Book Appointment" button.
20. **Global Footer**: Comprehensive links (Services, Legal, About), Contact Info, Social Media icons, Newsletter signup (optional), Copyright info.
