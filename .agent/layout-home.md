# Home Page Layout Architecture

**Intent:** Establish immediate trust, showcase high-tech expertise (implants), and emphasize painless, compassionate care.

## 1. Top Bar (Contact/Location)
- **Visuals:** Thin, dark slate background. White text.
- **Content:** Emergency phone number, clinic address, opening hours summary.

## 2. Navigation Header
- **Visuals:** Sticky white/cream background (blur on scroll). Elegant serif logo.
- **Content:** Logo (Left), Links (Center: About, Services, Implants, Testimonials, Contact), CTA Button (Right: "Book Appointment" in secondary terracotta color).

## 3. Hero Section
- **Visuals:** Full-height or large hero. Soft ambient background video or high-quality photo of welcoming clinic/smiling patient. Deep slate overlay text for contrast.
- **Content:** Headline (e.g., "Precision Dentistry. Warm Family Care."), Subheadline emphasizing implants and painless treatments, Primary CTA ("Schedule a Consultation"), Secondary CTA ("Meet Dr. Singh").

## 4. Trust & Stats Bar
- **Visuals:** Clean, muted background.
- **Content:** 4 columns of stats/icons (e.g., "15+ Years Experience", "10,000+ Happy Smiles", "Advanced Implant Tech", "Painless RCT").

## 5. Introduction / Welcome
- **Visuals:** Split layout (Text Left, Image Right). Image has soft rounded corners (`--radius-xl`).
- **Content:** Brief welcome from Dr. Gurpartap Singh. Emphasizes the clinic's philosophy of balancing clinical excellence with patient comfort. Signature graphic.

## 6. Core Services Grid
- **Visuals:** Grid layout (2x2 or 3x2). Cards with subtle shadows (`--shadow-md`) and hover lift.
- **Content:**
  - Dental Implants
  - Painless Root Canal Treatment (RCT)
  - Cosmetic Dentistry
  - Orthodontics / Braces
  - General Dentistry
  - Pediatric Care
- Each card has an icon, title, short description, and "Learn More" link.

## 7. Spotlight: Advanced Implant Technology
- **Visuals:** Deep slate background. White text. Large compelling imagery of modern technology (CBCT scanner, 3D printing).
- **Content:** Highlighting the precision, longevity, and aesthetics of the clinic's implant procedures. Why choose us for implants?

## 8. Process: Painless RCT
- **Visuals:** Step-by-step horizontal or vertical timeline layout.
- **Content:** 3-4 steps explaining the painless root canal process, alleviating patient anxiety.

## 9. Patient Testimonials Carousel
- **Visuals:** Warm muted background. Carousel of review cards. Quote icon watermark.
- **Content:** Real patient reviews highlighting specific treatments (implants, painless procedures, friendly staff). Video testimonials if available.

## 10. Before & After Gallery (Sneak Peek)
- **Visuals:** Interactive slider or grid of 3 key transformations.
- **Content:** Focus on implant and cosmetic success stories.

## 11. Meet the Team (Brief)
- **Visuals:** Clean layout. Professional headshots with rounded borders (`--radius-lg`).
- **Content:** Focus on Dr. Singh and key specialists.

## 12. Clinic Tour / Atmosphere
- **Visuals:** Masonry grid of high-quality photos showing the reception, clean treatment rooms, and technology.
- **Content:** Short text emphasizing the relaxing, sterile, and modern environment.

## 13. FAQ (Top Questions)
- **Visuals:** Accordion layout.
- **Content:** 3-5 common questions (e.g., "Does an implant hurt?", "Do you take insurance?").

## 14. Insurances & Financing
- **Visuals:** Logo cloud of accepted insurance providers or payment partners.
- **Content:** "Making premium care accessible."

## 15. Blog / Resources (Latest)
- **Visuals:** 3-column grid of recent article cards.
- **Content:** Patient education (e.g., "Caring for your new implant", "Why RCT isn't scary anymore").

## 16. Location & Map Section
- **Visuals:** Split layout. Left: Contact details, hours. Right: Embedded Google Map.
- **Content:** Clear directions to the Patiala clinic.

## 17. Final CTA Banner
- **Visuals:** Solid secondary color (terracotta/sage) background. White text.
- **Content:** "Ready to transform your smile? Schedule your pain-free consultation today." Large button.

## 18. Footer - Brand & Contact
- **Visuals:** Deep slate background.
- **Content:** Logo, clinic description, comprehensive contact details.

## 19. Footer - Quick Links
- **Visuals:** Lists of links.
- **Content:** Services, Company, Patient Resources.

## 20. Footer - Legal & Social
- **Visuals:** Bottom bar, smaller text, muted foreground.
- **Content:** Copyright, Privacy Policy, Terms of Service, Social Media Icons.
