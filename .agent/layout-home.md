# Homepage Layout Intent

**Page Intent:** Establish immediate trust, showcase the multi-decade legacy and 4.6-star authority, and guide patients toward booking a painless consultation.

## Sections

1. **`HeroSection`**:
   - *Layout:* Split 50/50. Left: Powerful, reassuring typography with a clear primary CTA. Right: High-quality, warm imagery of a calm clinic environment or a confident, genuine smile.
   - *Vibe:* Authoritative, welcoming.
2. **`TrustBarSection`**:
   - *Layout:* Horizontal strip spanning full width. Contains logos of dental associations, "4.6 stars, 863 reviews" badge, and "Serving Chandigarh since 1990."
   - *Vibe:* Instant credibility.
3. **`ProblemAgitationSection`**:
   - *Layout:* Centered typography leading into a 3-column grid highlighting common patient fears (pain, cost, judgment).
   - *Vibe:* Empathy. We understand.
4. **`OurPromiseSection`**:
   - *Layout:* Large overlapping image and text box. Focus on "Painless Care."
   - *Vibe:* Reassurance.
5. **`CoreServicesGrid`**:
   - *Layout:* 2x2 or 3x2 grid of cards featuring icons, service titles, and short descriptions (e.g., Implants, Braces, Whitening, General Care).
   - *Vibe:* Comprehensive clinical excellence.
6. **`ServiceHighlight_Implants`**:
   - *Layout:* Alternating left/right image and text layout. Deep dive into dental implants.
7. **`ServiceHighlight_Cosmetic`**:
   - *Layout:* Alternating left/right image and text layout. Focus on aesthetic transformations.
8. **`DoctorProfileSection`**:
   - *Layout:* Clean portrait of Dr. Sarabjeet Singh on one side, biography and credentials on the other. Includes a handwritten signature graphic for personal touch.
   - *Vibe:* Expert, family-trusted.
9. **`ClinicTourGallery`**:
   - *Layout:* A curated masonry or scrolling carousel of clinic interior photos showing a clean, modern, relaxing environment.
   - *Vibe:* Transparency, modern facilities.
10. **`PatientTestimonialsCarousel`**:
    - *Layout:* Large text quotes with patient names, ages, and star ratings sliding horizontally.
    - *Vibe:* Social proof.
11. **`BeforeAfterSliderSection`**:
    - *Layout:* Interactive image comparison sliders for 2-3 specific case studies.
    - *Vibe:* Clinical results.
12. **`TechnologyShowcaseSection`**:
    - *Layout:* 4-column grid detailing specific tech (e.g., 3D Scanning, Digital X-Rays) that enables painless treatment.
    - *Vibe:* Cutting-edge precision.
13. **`InsuranceAndFinancingSection`**:
    - *Layout:* Simple, straightforward block detailing accepted insurances and EMI options.
    - *Vibe:* Accessible, no hidden costs.
14. **`HowItWorksSteps`**:
    - *Layout:* Vertical timeline or 3-step horizontal process (Book -> Consult -> Smile).
    - *Vibe:* Easy, frictionless.
15. **`FAQAccordion`**:
    - *Layout:* Top 5 most asked questions in a clean accordion layout.
16. **`LocationAndHoursMap`**:
    - *Layout:* Split layout. Left: Address (Sector 35C), contact info, hours. Right: Stylized map or photo of the clinic exterior.
17. **`EmergencyCareBanner`**:
    - *Layout:* Distinct, high-contrast banner strip for "Dental Emergency? Call Now."
    - *Vibe:* Urgent but calm assistance.
18. **`BlogPreviewSection`**:
    - *Layout:* 3 recent articles in a card layout to demonstrate ongoing education and thought leadership.
19. **`FinalCTASection`**:
    - *Layout:* Centered, expansive container with a strong headline and the primary booking button.
    - *Vibe:* Warm invitation.
20. **`FooterSection`**:
    - *Layout:* Multi-column layout with navigation links, contact details, social links, legal pages, and a newsletter signup.
