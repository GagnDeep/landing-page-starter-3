# Home Page Layout Architecture

## 1. Hero Section (`hero`)
- **Intent:** Immediate cinematic impact. A full-bleed or elegantly framed hero image/video loop showcasing a signature wedding moment.
- **Rhythm:** Minimal text overlay. Large serif heading establishing the brand identity ("Aman Sidhu Photography").

## 2. Trust Bar (`trust-bar`)
- **Intent:** Subtle social proof.
- **Rhythm:** A delicate, horizontal band with small logos or typography indicating features in premium publications or venues.

## 3. Brand Introduction (`brand-intro`)
- **Intent:** Establish the ethos. A short, powerful statement about the timeless, editorial approach to wedding photography.
- **Rhythm:** Generous padding, centered alignment, large, high-contrast serif typography.

## 4. Featured Portfolio Teaser (`portfolio-teaser`)
- **Intent:** Showcase the best work immediately.
- **Rhythm:** An asymmetrical grid of 3-4 images, overlapping slightly or spaced elegantly, evoking an editorial spread.

## 5. The Candid Approach (`candid-approach`)
- **Intent:** Differentiate the style. Focus on the unposed, authentic moments.
- **Rhythm:** Text and image split-screen or alternating side-by-side layout.

## 6. Signature Services (`signature-services`)
- **Intent:** Outline offerings (e.g., Pre-wedding, Wedding Day, Editorial Shoots).
- **Rhythm:** A clean, 3-column layout with refined icons or small thumbnail images and concise descriptions.

## 7. The Process Overview (`process-overview`)
- **Intent:** Build trust by explaining the seamless client experience.
- **Rhythm:** A vertical timeline or numbered step-by-step layout with subtle connecting lines.

## 8. Client Testimonial 1 (`testimonial-single-1`)
- **Intent:** Deep emotional proof.
- **Rhythm:** A full-width quote in the serif font, accompanied by a subtle background image or texture.

## 9. Gallery Carousel (`gallery-carousel`)
- **Intent:** Interactive exploration of moments.
- **Rhythm:** A horizontal scrolling section of images. Smooth, cinematic scroll mechanics.

## 10. The Artisan Behind the Lens (`artisan-profile`)
- **Intent:** Introduce Aman Sidhu. Personalize the brand.
- **Rhythm:** A striking portrait of Aman alongside a brief, personal narrative.

## 11. Destination Weddings Focus (`destination-focus`)
- **Intent:** Highlight the ability to travel for events.
- **Rhythm:** A visually rich section, perhaps a parallax image of a stunning location with overlaid elegant typography.

## 12. Fine Art Albums (`fine-art-albums`)
- **Intent:** Upsell physical products. Emphasize legacy.
- **Rhythm:** High-quality imagery of the physical albums, laid out in a "still life" style arrangement.

## 13. Wedding Packages Teaser (`wedding-packages`)
- **Intent:** Provide a glimpse into investment options without overwhelming details.
- **Rhythm:** Minimalist cards or a clean, tabular layout with a call-to-action for the full pricing guide.

## 14. Testimonial Carousel (`testimonial-carousel`)
- **Intent:** Broaden social proof.
- **Rhythm:** A sleek, minimal carousel of shorter quotes from various clients.

## 15. Journal/Blog Highlights (`journal-highlights`)
- **Intent:** Share recent stories and keep content fresh.
- **Rhythm:** A 2 or 3-column grid of recent blog posts with compelling cover images and elegant titles.

## 16. FAQ Teaser (`faq-teaser`)
- **Intent:** Address common hesitations immediately.
- **Rhythm:** An accordion style layout with 3-4 key questions, keeping the design clean and uncluttered.

## 17. The "Why Us" Manifesto (`why-us`)
- **Intent:** A final, strong value proposition before the main CTA.
- **Rhythm:** A bold, typography-driven section.

## 18. Featured Video (`featured-video`)
- **Intent:** Emotional resonance through motion.
- **Rhythm:** A large, elegant video player, perhaps initially paused with a beautiful poster image and a subtle play button.

## 19. Final Call to Action (`final-cta`)
- **Intent:** Drive inquiries.
- **Rhythm:** High contrast (e.g., dark background in light mode), clear headline, and a prominent, elegant button ("Inquire Now").

## 20. Footer Pre-amble (`footer-preamble`)
- **Intent:** Smooth transition to the footer. Often contains an instagram feed or a final evocative image.
- **Rhythm:** A strip of images (e.g., Instagram feed) or a full-width cinematic shot leading into the footer links.
