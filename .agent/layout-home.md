# Home Page Layout Blueprint

1. **Global Header (Hero Nav):** Transparent navigation, center-aligned logo, subtle "Book Consultation" button.
2. **Hero Section (Bridal Entrance):** Full-screen ambient video/image of a bridal transformation, large serif headline ("The Art of Aesthetic Elegance"), delicate scroll indicator.
3. **Intro/Philosophy (The Studio):** Generous whitespace, elegant text block detailing Dr. Arshpreet Kaur’s vision.
4. **Signature Service 1 (Bridal Aesthetics):** Asymmetrical layout, high-quality image left, soft narrative right.
5. **Signature Service 2 (Advanced Dentistry):** Image right, text left, emphasizing clinical precision.
6. **Signature Service 3 (Facial Contouring):** Centered dramatic layout, full-width image with text overlay.
7. **Testimonial Carousel (Patient Stories):** Soft pearl background, large quote marks, elegant typography fading in/out.
8. **Feature Highlight (State-of-the-art Tech):** Grid layout of clinical technology with a luxury studio aesthetic.
9. **Meet the Doctor (Dr. Arshpreet):** Editorial portrait of the doctor, brief biography, link to About page.
10. **Before & After Gallery (Transformations):** Curated, non-clinical before/after slider with soft borders.
11. **Value Proposition (Why Choose Us):** 3-column text layout, delicate icons, focusing on comfort, expertise, and luxury.
12. **Video Showcase (Experience Preet Dental):** Cinematic video block showcasing the clinic environment.
13. **Consultation CTA (Begin Your Journey):** Deep charcoal section, glowing primary button, minimalist form teaser.
14. **Process Steps (The Consultation):** Vertical timeline with soft lines, detailing the initial patient journey.
15. **Press/As Seen In (Logos):** Subtle opacity logos of publications or premium dental networks.
16. **FAQ Snippet (Common Questions):** Accordion layout, 3-4 top questions with smooth expansions.
17. **Latest Articles (Aesthetic Insights):** 2-column grid of recent blog posts, large imagery, subtle hover lifts.
18. **Instagram Feed (The Aesthetic Diary):** 4x1 grid of curated social media images.
19. **Newsletter Signup (Stay Inspired):** Minimalist input field, centered, elegant typography.
20. **Global Footer:** Multi-column layout for links, contact info, operating hours, and legal links.
21. **Sub-footer:** Copyright, minimalist design credits.
