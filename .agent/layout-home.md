# Home Page Layout Architecture

## 1. Global Navigation Bar (Header)
- Sticky header, transparent at top, solidifies on scroll.
- Logo centered or left. Desktop nav links, CTA button ("Book Consultation").
- Mobile menu toggle.

## 2. Hero Section: The Bridal Vision
- Full-bleed cinematic video or massive parallax image.
- High-contrast serif display text: "Your Perfect Day, Perfectly Sculpted."
- Primary CTA: "Reserve Your Date"
- Secondary text link: "Explore our gallery"

## 3. Social Proof Banner (Asymmetric)
- Logos/names of premium bridal publications or venues.
- Left-aligned text "Trusted by elite brides across Patiala."

## 4. Introduction: The IKonic Difference
- Staggered 2-column layout.
- Column 1: A striking portrait with a subtle drop shadow.
- Column 2: Elegant serif h2, short body text, and a signature-style sign-off.

## 5. Service Pillars (Interactive Hover Grid)
- 3 to 4 tall, elegant cards.
- Bridal Makeup, Engagement Looks, Party Glam, Hair Artistry.
- Image-heavy, text reveals on hover (Progressive disclosure).

## 6. The Signature Bridal Package Highlight
- Dark-theme section cutting through the light flow.
- Metallic rose gold accents.
- Breakdown of the premium package, feeling exclusive and rare.

## 7. Portfolio Sneak Peek (Masonry Gallery)
- Irregular grid of high-end wedding photos.
- Parallax scroll effect on individual images.
- Button: "View Full Gallery"

## 8. Founder's Quote / Philosophy
- Large, centered serif typography.
- "We don't just apply makeup; we craft the confidence you wear down the aisle."
- Subtle background texture.

## 9. Testimonial Spotlight
- Single, powerful review taking up significant space.
- Star rating in champagne gold.
- Elegant photo of the bride next to her quote.

## 10. Featured Before & After (Slider or Reveal)
- Interactive element letting users drag a slider.
- Demonstrates skill without feeling "cheap".

## 11. Experience Timeline (Step-by-step)
- Vertical line with elegant nodes.
- Consultation -> Trial -> Skin Prep -> The Big Day.
- Alternating left/right text.

## 12. Video Feature (Behind the Scenes)
- Large rounded video player (1rem radius).
- Custom play button with slow pulse animation.

## 13. Awards & Recognitions
- Minimalist grid of badges.
- "Voted #1 Bridal Studio 2023", etc.

## 14. Studio Atmosphere / Location
- Split layout: Wide shot of the luxurious studio interior, map/address details.
- "A sanctuary of beauty in the heart of Patiala."

## 15. FAQ Teaser
- Accordion style, top 3 most asked questions.
- "How far in advance should I book?"
- Link to full FAQ page.

## 16. The Team / Senior Artists
- Circular or soft-cornered portraits.
- Clean typography for names and roles.

## 17. Instagram Feed Strip
- 5-6 square images spanning the full width.
- No gaps, or very tight gaps (4px).
- "Follow the glamour @ikonicbeauty"

## 18. Secondary CTA (Newsletter/Tips)
- Soft blush background (`var(--secondary)`).
- "Receive our Bridal Prep Guide."
- Simple input field + submit button.

## 19. Final Grand CTA
- Dark, moody background (`var(--primary-foreground)` or deep onyx).
- Massive serif heading: "Ready to be an IKonic Bride?"
- Primary Button: "Book Your Consultation Now"

## 20. Global Footer
- Minimalist and structured.
- 4 columns: Brand info, Quick Links, Services, Contact info.
- Copyright and legal links at the very bottom.
