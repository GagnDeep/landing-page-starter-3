# Home Page Layout Structure

**Goal**: A seamless, high-conversion, deeply empathetic journey that immediately establishes clinical-luxury trust and guides users to book an appointment.

1. **Global Alert Banner**
   - **Intent**: Important, timely updates (e.g., "Accepting new patients," "COVID-19 protocols," or "Holiday hours").
   - **Visual**: Subtle background, highly legible text, dismissible.

2. **Primary Navigation (Header)**
   - **Intent**: Easy access to Services, About, Patient Info, Contact, and a prominent "Book Appointment" CTA.
   - **Visual**: Sticky, glassmorphism effect, logo left, links center, CTA right.

3. **Hero Section**
   - **Intent**: Establish immediate emotional connection and trust.
   - **Visual**: Large, warm imagery (family smiling or Dr. Gaurav with patient), "clinical-luxury" typography for value proposition, primary and secondary CTAs.

4. **Trust Bar (Social Proof)**
   - **Intent**: Validate the 5.0-star reputation.
   - **Visual**: Logos of awards, "5.0 Stars on Google" badge, clean grid layout below Hero.

5. **Welcome / Philosophy**
   - **Intent**: Introduce Dr. Gaurav and the clinic's patient-first ethos.
   - **Visual**: Split layout (text + high-quality portrait/clinic photo), warm tone.

6. **Services Teaser (The "Full-Service" Promise)**
   - **Intent**: Highlight comprehensive care (Implants, Pediatric, Cosmetic, General).
   - **Visual**: Grid of 4-6 cards with custom iconography or elegant photography, soft shadows on hover.

7. **Feature Spotlight 1: Pain-Free Dentistry**
   - **Intent**: Alleviate common dental anxiety.
   - **Visual**: Calm, hygienic imagery, reassuring copy, soft background color.

8. **Feature Spotlight 2: Advanced Technology**
   - **Intent**: Demonstrate modern, world-class capabilities.
   - **Visual**: Tech-focused but approachable (e.g., 3D scanners), geometric UI accents.

9. **Before & After Gallery (Teaser)**
   - **Intent**: Visual proof of aesthetic and functional results.
   - **Visual**: Interactive slider or side-by-side comparison cards.

10. **Testimonials Carousel**
    - **Intent**: Deepen trust through real patient stories.
    - **Visual**: Elegant typography, star ratings, subtle motion curve on slide change.

11. **How-It-Works (Patient Journey)**
    - **Intent**: Demystify the first visit.
    - **Visual**: 3-4 step timeline or horizontal sequence (Book -> Consult -> Smile).

12. **Meet the Team (Teaser)**
    - **Intent**: Humanize the practice.
    - **Visual**: Friendly, professional headshots of Dr. Gaurav and key staff.

13. **Insurance & Financing Options**
    - **Intent**: Remove financial barriers and anxiety.
    - **Visual**: Clear typography, logos of accepted insurances, link to financing plans.

14. **Location & Clinic Tour**
    - **Intent**: Show the Sector 46C, Chandigarh clinic is a premium, hygienic space.
    - **Visual**: Auto-playing subtle video loop or high-quality image gallery of the interior.

15. **FAQ Teaser**
    - **Intent**: Answer top 3-4 common questions immediately.
    - **Visual**: Clean accordion layout, readable body text.

16. **Recent Blog Posts / Articles**
    - **Intent**: Establish authority and SEO value.
    - **Visual**: 3-column card grid, blog titles, read more links.

17. **Call-To-Action (Pre-Footer)**
    - **Intent**: Final push to book an appointment.
    - **Visual**: High contrast (Primary color background), large headline, prominent button.

18. **Newsletter Signup (Optional/Subtle)**
    - **Intent**: Capture leads for dental tips.
    - **Visual**: Simple inline form.

19. **Global Footer**
    - **Intent**: Comprehensive navigation, contact info, hours.
    - **Visual**: Multi-column layout, dark or muted background, structured links.

20. **Legal & Copyright Sub-footer**
    - **Intent**: Compliance.
    - **Visual**: Small text, links to Privacy Policy and Terms of Service.
