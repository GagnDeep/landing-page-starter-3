# Dentocare Home Page Layout Documentation

This document outlines the blueprint for the Home page, defining the layout intent, visual rhythm, and structural composition for 20 distinct sections.

## 1. Global Header & Navigation
- **Layout:** Sticky top bar, transparent at the top but gains a frosted glass background on scroll.
- **Rhythm:** Minimalist. Logo on the left, primary navigation links in the center, and a prominent "Book Appointment" CTA on the right.

## 2. Hero Section
- **Layout:** Full-height (or 90vh) split-screen or large typography-led layout. Large, welcoming imagery of the clinic or Dr. Gaurav.
- **Rhythm:** Expansive and breathable. Large serif heading, concise subtext, primary CTA, and a secondary "Explore Services" link.

## 3. Trust Bar / Social Proof
- **Layout:** A subtle, horizontal scrolling or static band immediately below the hero.
- **Rhythm:** Fast and repetitive but visually quiet. Displays logos of associations, "5.0 Star Rating on Google", or recognizable badges.

## 4. Introduction / "Welcome to Dentocare"
- **Layout:** Centered, medium-width text block with a high-quality, warm image or video offset to the side.
- **Rhythm:** Grounding and personal. Introduces Dr. Gaurav and the clinic's philosophy of "hygienic luxury."

## 5. Core Values / The "Why Us" Grid
- **Layout:** A 3-column grid featuring soft icons and short text.
- **Rhythm:** Even and paced. Focuses on hygiene, comfort, and expertise.

## 6. Services Spotlight - Overview
- **Layout:** Full-width section with a sticky heading on one side and a scrollable list of core service categories on the other.
- **Rhythm:** Dynamic but controlled. Encourages exploration of general, pediatric, and cosmetic dentistry.

## 7. Service Deep Dive: Orthodontics
- **Layout:** Card-based layout or alternating image/text blocks highlighting Invisalign and traditional braces.
- **Rhythm:** Focused and educational.

## 8. Service Deep Dive: Implants & Restorative
- **Layout:** Visual before/after slider or high-fidelity diagrams showing the quality of materials.
- **Rhythm:** Reassuring and technical but approachable.

## 9. Pediatric Dentistry Focus
- **Layout:** Warmer, softer imagery, perhaps using secondary/accent colors to feel friendly for families.
- **Rhythm:** Gentle and inviting.

## 10. Technology & Hygiene Standards
- **Layout:** Dark mode or deep slate background section to highlight advanced technology (e.g., 3D imaging, sterilization protocols).
- **Rhythm:** Premium and authoritative.

## 11. "How it Works" / Patient Journey
- **Layout:** A horizontal timeline or a stepped vertical list (1, 2, 3).
- **Rhythm:** Logical and sequential. From booking to the post-treatment smile.

## 12. Testimonials Carousel
- **Layout:** Large, beautifully styled cards. Perhaps overlapping slightly to show depth.
- **Rhythm:** Social proof, text-heavy but broken up by avatars and star ratings.

## 13. Meet Dr. Gaurav (Team Section)
- **Layout:** Large portrait image alongside a professional biography and credentials.
- **Rhythm:** Personal and authoritative. Emphasizes experience and patient care.

## 14. Clinic Tour / Gallery
- **Layout:** A masonry grid of high-quality photos showing the waiting area, treatment rooms, and equipment.
- **Rhythm:** Visual discovery. Emphasizes the clean, luxurious environment.

## 15. Transparent Pricing & Insurance
- **Layout:** Simple, tabular layout or cards detailing accepted insurances, payment plans, and consultation fees.
- **Rhythm:** Clear, direct, and transparent.

## 16. Featured Blog / Patient Education
- **Layout:** 3-column card grid showing recent articles on oral health.
- **Rhythm:** Magazine-like, informative.

## 17. Interactive FAQ
- **Layout:** Accordion-style list centered on the page.
- **Rhythm:** Utility-focused. Easy to scan and read.

## 18. Location & Contact Banner
- **Layout:** Split section: A stylized map on one side, address and opening hours on the other.
- **Rhythm:** Practical and grounding.

## 19. Final Call to Action (CTA)
- **Layout:** Large, bold section with a deep background color, reiterating the main value proposition.
- **Rhythm:** Urgent but welcoming. "Ready for your best smile?"

## 20. Global Footer
- **Layout:** Multi-column layout containing secondary links, legal information, newsletter signup, and contact details.
- **Rhythm:** Structured, dense but organized, closing the page solidly.
