# Dentistree - Home Page Layout Blueprint

**Goal:** Establish trust immediately, clearly showcase multi-specialty expertise (especially orthodontics and pediatric care), and drive booking conversions through a welcoming, kid-friendly yet expert aesthetic.

## Sections (20+)

1. **Global Navigation Bar:** Clean, sticky header with logo, navigation links (Services, About, Patient Info, Contact), and a prominent "Book Appointment" button. Soft shadow on scroll.
2. **Hero Section:** Split layout. Left: Inviting headline ("Gentle Dental Care for the Whole Family"), subheadline, primary CTA ("Book Your Visit"), and secondary CTA ("Meet the Team"). Right: High-quality, warm image of a smiling child with a dentist, framed in a soft rounded container.
3. **Trust Indicators (Strip):** Horizontal band displaying "5.0 Star Rating", "10+ Years Experience", "Multi-Specialty Clinic".
4. **Welcome / Philosophy Section:** Text block emphasizing the boutique, gentle approach to dentistry. "Where Expertise Meets Comfort."
5. **Services Overview Intro:** Section heading "Our Specialties" with a short description.
6. **Service Card - Orthodontics:** Card featuring an icon, title "Orthodontics", short description about braces/aligners, and a "Learn More" link.
7. **Service Card - Pediatric Dentistry:** Card with a kid-friendly icon, title "Pediatric Care", and description of gentle treatments for children.
8. **Service Card - General Dentistry:** Card highlighting routine checkups, cleanings, and preventive care.
9. **Service Card - Cosmetic Dentistry:** Card focusing on whitening, veneers, and smile makeovers.
10. **The Clinic Experience (Visual Break):** Full-width section with a soft teal background (`bg-primary/10`) featuring a photo gallery grid of the clinic's welcoming interior and play area.
11. **Meet the Lead Doctor:** Spotlight on the chief dentist/orthodontist. Photo, brief bio emphasizing credentials and gentle approach.
12. **Why Choose Dentistree?:** List of benefits (e.g., State-of-the-art tech, relaxing environment, kid-focused).
13. **Patient Testimonials Intro:** Heading "What Our Patients Say".
14. **Testimonial Carousel/Grid:** 3-4 cards featuring glowing reviews from parents and patients, emphasizing comfort and results.
15. **Insurance & Financing Strip:** Quick info on accepted insurances and flexible payment plans for orthodontic work.
16. **Pediatric Focus Spotlight:** A dedicated section talking directly to parents about how the clinic reduces dental anxiety for kids (e.g., "The Dentistree Kid's Club").
17. **Before & After Teaser:** A slider or grid showing 2-3 impressive orthodontic transformations.
18. **FAQ Accordion (Top 3):** Quick answers to the most common questions (e.g., "At what age should my child first visit?").
19. **Final Call to Action:** Large, centered section with a soft peach background (`bg-secondary/20`). "Ready for a Brighter Smile?" and a prominent booking button.
20. **Global Footer:** Links to all pages, clinic address, phone number, working hours, social media links, and legal links (Privacy, Terms).
