# Home Page Layout Blueprint

1. **Header**: Sticky navigation, logo, primary CTA ("Book Appointment").
2. **Hero Section**: Large typography (`Cormorant Garamond`), gentle background, secondary CTA, primary CTA.
3. **Trust Indicators**: Logos of partners, associations, or brief stats.
4. **Value Proposition**: "Gentle, Technology-Driven Care" - brief overview.
5. **Specialty Intro**: Highlight Single-Sitting RCT.
6. **Specialty Detail (RCT)**: Visual or short steps explaining the modern RCT process.
7. **Specialty Intro**: Highlight Pediatric Dentistry.
8. **Specialty Detail (Pediatric)**: Focus on gentle care and welcoming environment.
9. **Service Grid**: Quick links/cards for other services (Aligners, Implants, etc.).
10. **The Clinic Experience**: "Not your typical dental clinic" - imagery/text about the calming environment.
11. **Technology Highlights**: Brief showcase of advanced equipment (3D scanners, painless anesthesia).
12. **Testimonial Carousel**: Patient stories focusing on comfort and results.
13. **Meet the Lead Dentist**: Brief bio, photo, and philosophy.
14. **Before/After Gallery**: Interactive or slider showing transformative results.
15. **Pricing/Insurance Teaser**: "Transparent Care" - link to full pricing/insurance page.
16. **FAQ Snippet**: Top 3 questions answered directly on the home page.
17. **Location/Contact Teaser**: Map snippet, address, hours, quick contact info.
18. **Final CTA**: Large section reiterating "Ready to smile? Book today."
19. **Newsletter/Updates**: Optional sign-up for clinic news or dental tips.
20. **Footer**: Comprehensive links, legal, social media, secondary contact info.
