# Layout: Home Page

## Structural Intent
The homepage establishes the premium brand, showcases expertise, and guides the user toward booking a consultation. It follows a narrative flow from emotional connection to clinical proof.

## Visual Rhythm
Sections alternate between high-impact visual moments (full-width imagery, bold typography) and structured, data-driven content. Ample spacing (`--space-5xl`) between major sections ensures a calm, uncluttered experience.

## Sections

1.  **Hero Section:** Full-viewport height. A refined, editorial split layout. Large display typography (Newsreader) declaring "International-Standard Care." High-quality, subtle background imagery or a refined video loop. Primary CTA to book consultation.
2.  **Trust Bar:** A quiet, low-contrast band displaying logos of international affiliations, materials used (e.g., Nobel Biocare), and media features.
3.  **Introduction / The Clinic Statement:** A spacious, text-focused section. A large, elegant quote or statement about the philosophy of "The Dental Masters," emphasizing clinical precision and ethical excellence.
4.  **Clinical Specialties Overview:** A grid (or asymmetrical list) highlighting core services (Implants, Aesthetics, Orthodontics). Each item features a subtle hover effect (scale up 1.02x), an icon/image, and a short description.
5.  **Signature Service: Implants (Deep Dive):** A dedicated, full-width section with a dark background (`--color-bg-primary` in dark mode) highlighting implant expertise. High contrast, authoritative tone.
6.  **The Master Clinicians (Team Teaser):** A refined, editorial presentation of the lead doctors. High-quality portrait photography with subtle overlapping typography. Link to full team page.
7.  **Technology & Precision:** A clean, structural section (possibly alternating image/text blocks) detailing the advanced technology (3D imaging, etc.) used to ensure precision.
8.  **Patient Journey (The Experience):** A stepped, narrative section explaining what a patient can expect from consultation to aftercare. Emphasizes the "calm and trustworthy" aesthetic.
9.  **Before & After (Clinical Evidence):** A highly structured, interactive (or slider) section showing real, high-quality clinical results. Restrained styling, focusing on the clinical outcome.
10. **Testimonials / Client Stories:** Editorial layout of patient quotes, perhaps styled like pull quotes in a premium magazine. Avoid generic "cards."
11. **International Patients / NRI Focus:** A section speaking directly to international clients, detailing concierge services, travel planning, and global standards.
12. **The Environment / Clinic Tour Teaser:** A masonry grid or subtle slider showcasing the luxurious, calm interior of the clinic.
13. **Ethical Excellence (Core Values):** A simple, text-led section detailing the commitment to ethical treatments, transparency, and patient first philosophy.
14. **FAQ Highlights:** An accordion or list of the 3-4 most common questions (e.g., "Do you treat international patients?", "What is the warranty on implants?").
15. **Latest Insights / Blog Teaser:** A refined grid of the latest articles or case studies. Focus on clean typography and high-quality thumbnail imagery.
16. **Why Choose Us (Summary):** A quick, bulleted or icon-led summary of the unique value propositions (e.g., 20+ years experience, international standards, in-house lab).
17. **Location & Contact Teaser:** A clean, map-integrated (or abstract map) section showing the Sector 21C, Chandigarh location, alongside quick contact details.
18. **Newsletter / Updates Signup:** A minimal, inline form for users to subscribe to clinic news or oral health tips.
19. **Final CTA (Book Consultation):** A large, impactful block, possibly repeating elements of the Hero but focused purely on conversion. High contrast.
20. **Footer:** A comprehensive, structured footer with navigation links, contact info, legal links, and social icons. Clean typography, aligned to the grid.
