# Layout Blueprint: Home Page

**Purpose**: The primary entry point. Must immediately establish the clinic's 4.9-star reputation, highlight advanced implant capabilities, and drive appointment bookings through a warm-professional visual hierarchy.

## Sections

1.  **Global Navigation Bar**: Sticky header. Left: Logo. Center: Primary links (About, Services, Patient Info). Right: CTA (Book Appointment, phone number).
2.  **Hero Section**: Full-height or large viewport presentation. Split layout: Left holds strong typography (H1 serif), establishing trust and the clinic name. Right holds a high-quality, welcoming clinical image. Generous padding.
3.  **Trust Bar (Logos/Badges)**: Horizontal strip directly below the hero. Subdued contrast. Displays affiliations, "4.9 Star Rated", and years in practice.
4.  **Introduction / "Why Us" Teaser**: Centered, narrow max-width text block (H2). Highlighting Dr. Anshum Gupta's expertise and the clinic's patient-first philosophy.
5.  **Core Services Grid (Overview)**: 3-column or 4-column CSS grid. Distinct cards for Implants, Cosmetic, General, and Orthodontics. Soft borders, subtle hover lift.
6.  **Dr. Anshum Gupta Profile Snippet**: Asymmetric layout. Image of the doctor on one side, professional bio and credentials on the other. Uses secondary (teal) background to break up white space.
7.  **Technology & Hygiene Highlights**: Icons + Text rows. Highlighting advanced equipment (CBCT, scanners) and strict sterilization protocols. Minimalist, icon-driven design.
8.  **Patient Testimonials Carousel**: Warm tone section. Large serif quote marks. Clean cards displaying real patient feedback to reinforce the 4.9-star rating.
9.  **Before/After Gallery Teaser**: Interactive slider or a curated 2x2 grid of successful cases. Must look clinical but beautiful.
10. **The Patient Journey / Process**: Step-by-step horizontal or vertical timeline (Consultation -> Plan -> Treatment -> Smile). Uses accent color for timeline nodes.
11. **Featured Video/Tour**: A full-bleed or large contained video block. Auto-playing subtle loop or play button for a clinic tour.
12. **Insurance & Financing Banner**: A solid primary-color banner. Brief text explaining accepted insurances and EMI options, with a link to learn more.
13. **Emergency Dental Care Callout**: High contrast, perhaps utilizing a refined destructive/alert color scheme (tasteful, not alarming) to highlight immediate care availability.
14. **Blog / Latest News Teaser**: 3-column grid of the latest dental advice or clinic news. Image top, text bottom.
15. **FAQ Snippet**: Accordion style. Top 3-4 most asked questions (e.g., "Does an implant hurt?", "How to book?").
16. **Location Map & Info**: Split layout. Interactive or stylized map on one side, address, working hours, and contact details on the other.
17. **Appointment Booking Form (Inline)**: A streamlined, embedded contact form to capture leads without leaving the page.
18. **Newsletter / Updates Signup**: Small, clean strip. Input field and submit button.
19. **Global Pre-Footer**: Large, bold call to action. "Ready for your new smile?" with primary and secondary buttons.
20. **Global Footer**: Multi-column. Links, legal, social icons, and secondary contact info. Dark mode styling.
