# A-1 Dental Care Clinic — Inner Page Layouts

This document outlines the visual rhythm and layout intent for all non-homepage routes.

## 1. About Page (`/about`)
**Vibe:** Story-driven, personal, deeply rooted in the Patiala community.
*   **Hero:** A warm, candid shot of the clinic team. Large, inviting typography.
*   **The Origin Story:** A narrative text section detailing Dr. Pankaj's vision and why he started the clinic. Generous reading width (`max-w-prose`).
*   **Our Core Values:** A 3-column grid (Ethical, Trust, Painless).
*   **Meet the Team:** A grid of portraits. Hover states reveal a fun fact or quote.
*   **Community Involvement:** A section highlighting work within Patiala (if applicable).
*   **CTA:** "Experience the difference."

## 2. Features/Services Page (`/services`)
**Vibe:** Informative, reassuring, organized.
*   **Hero:** Clear, outcome-focused headline ("Comprehensive care for every smile").
*   **Service Categories Navigation:** A sticky sub-nav or sidebar to jump between General, Cosmetic, Restorative, etc.
*   **Service Detail Blocks:** Large alternating sections. Each service must have a "How it feels" or "Why it's painless" sub-section.
*   **Before/After Gallery:** A subtle, tasteful slider or comparison component.
*   **CTA:** "Book a consultation."

## 3. Pricing & Insurance (`/pricing`)
**Vibe:** Transparent, clear, ethical.
*   **Hero:** "Honest pricing. No surprises."
*   **The Philosophy:** A text block explaining the ethical approach to billing.
*   **Estimated Costs Grid:** A clean, easy-to-read table or list of common procedures (consultations, cleanings, fillings).
*   **Insurance & Financing:** A section detailing accepted plans and payment options (e.g., EMIs).
*   **FAQ (Billing specific):** Accordion for financial questions.
*   **CTA:** "Get a personalized treatment plan."

## 4. Blog / Patient Education (`/blog`)
**Vibe:** Educational, authoritative but accessible.
*   **Hero:** "Insights for a healthier smile."
*   **Featured Article:** A large, prominent card highlighting the most important recent post.
*   **Category Filters:** Simple pill-shaped buttons to filter (e.g., Prevention, Cosmetic, Kids).
*   **Article Grid:** Clean, uniform cards with soft shadows.
*   **Article Detail Layout:** Narrow reading column (`max-w-prose`), large legible typography (`text-lg` or `text-xl` for body), rich media support.

## 5. FAQ Page (`/faq`)
**Vibe:** Helpful, organized, calm.
*   **Hero:** "Questions? We have answers."
*   **Category Jump Links:** Quick links to sections (General, Pain, Billing, Procedures).
*   **Accordion Lists:** Grouped by category. Smooth, satisfying expand/collapse animations.
*   **Still have questions?:** A contact block at the bottom.

## 6. Contact Page (`/contact`)
**Vibe:** Accessible, immediate, welcoming.
*   **Hero:** "We're here for you."
*   **Split Layout:**
    *   *Left:* Contact information (Phone, Email, Address), opening hours, and an embedded stylized map.
    *   *Right:* A clean, friendly contact/booking form with clear floating labels.
*   **Emergency Callout:** A highlighted section for immediate urgent care needs.

## 7. Careers Page (`/careers`)
**Vibe:** Professional, growth-oriented, warm culture.
*   **Hero:** "Join the A-1 Dental Family."
*   **Culture Statement:** Why it's great to work here (respect, modern tech, work-life balance).
*   **Open Roles:** An accordion or list of current openings.
*   **Application Process:** A simple timeline explaining the hiring steps.

## 8. Legal Pages (`/terms`, `/privacy`)
**Vibe:** Utilitarian, readable.
*   **Layout:** Simple, narrow reading column.
*   **Typography:** Clear hierarchy, slightly smaller text than main marketing pages.
*   **Navigation:** A sticky sidebar to jump to sections of the legal document.
