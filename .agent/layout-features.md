# Features/Services Page Layout Structure

1. **Hero**: High-end service imagery, overarching promise of care.
2. **Service Categories Index**: Quick links to General, Cosmetic, Orthodontic, Implant.
3. **Detailed Service Sections**:
   - Alternating left/right image and text blocks for each major service.
   - Includes specific benefits and process.
4. **Technology Showcase**: Highlight specialized equipment used.
5. **Patient Success Stories**: Mini-testimonials relevant to specific services.
6. **CTA**: Schedule specific service.
