# Dentistree - Features / Services Page Layout Blueprint

**Goal:** Provide detailed, structured information about all dental services offered, ensuring patients understand the treatments and feel confident in the clinic's expertise.

## Sections (20+)

1. **Hero Section:** "Comprehensive Care for Every Smile". Brief intro explaining the multi-specialty nature of the clinic.
2. **Page Navigation (Jump Links):** Sticky sub-nav to jump between Orthodontics, Pediatric, General, and Cosmetic.
3. **Category 1: Orthodontics (Header):** Large section title and description of the orthodontic approach.
4. **Feature: Traditional Braces:** Detail block explaining modern braces, who they are for, and benefits.
5. **Feature: Clear Aligners (Invisalign):** Detail block on invisible aligners, process, and aesthetics.
6. **Feature: Early Interceptive Orthodontics:** Explaining phase 1 treatment for children.
7. **Category 2: Pediatric Dentistry (Header):** Section title focusing on kids' care.
8. **Feature: First Dental Visit:** What parents and kids can expect during the milestone first visit.
9. **Feature: Preventative Care for Kids:** Sealants, fluoride treatments, and education.
10. **Feature: Gentle Restorations:** Kid-friendly approaches to fillings and crowns.
11. **Category 3: General Dentistry (Header):** Routine care section.
12. **Feature: Exams & Cleanings:** Importance of bi-annual visits.
13. **Feature: Fillings & Root Canals:** Pain-free restorative procedures.
14. **Feature: Extractions & Wisdom Teeth:** Safe and comfortable removal processes.
15. **Category 4: Cosmetic Dentistry (Header):** Aesthetic services section.
16. **Feature: Professional Whitening:** Safe, effective brightening options.
17. **Feature: Porcelain Veneers:** Smile design and makeover details.
18. **Technology Highlight:** How 3D imaging and intraoral cameras improve these services.
19. **CTA / Next Steps:** "Not sure what you need? Schedule a consultation."
20. **Global Footer:** Standard footer.
