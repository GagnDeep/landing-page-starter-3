# Layout: Features (Services) Page
**Goal:** Clearly detail all dental services offered, ensuring users can easily find what they need.
**Rhythm:** Categorized, easily scannable grids with detailed drill-downs.

1. **Global Header (Sticky)**: Standard navigation.
2. **Hero Section**: Title "Comprehensive Dental Care." Subtitle emphasizing 24/7 availability for all needs.
3. **Quick Jump Menu**: Sticky anchor links to service categories (General, Cosmetic, Emergency, etc.).
4. **Category 1: Emergency Dentistry**: High priority section. Red/Orange accents. List of urgent conditions treated.
5. **Emergency CTA**: Prominent button "Call 24/7 Emergency Line".
6. **Category 2: General & Preventive Care**: Routine checkups, cleanings, x-rays. Use calm blues.
7. **Category 3: Cosmetic Dentistry**: Teeth whitening, veneers. Focus on aesthetic imagery.
8. **Category 4: Restorative Dentistry**: Crowns, bridges, dentures. Focus on restoring function.
9. **Category 5: Orthodontics**: Braces, clear aligners (Invisalign). Target both teens and adults.
10. **Category 6: Oral Surgery**: Extractions, wisdom teeth. Emphasize pain-free procedures.
11. **Category 7: Implant Dentistry**: Detailed section on single and full-mouth implants.
12. **Category 8: Pediatric Dentistry**: Focus on gentle care for children.
13. **Technology Behind the Services**: Explain the tools used across these services (e.g., 3D scanners).
14. **Service Process**: Step-by-step generic process (Consultation -> Plan -> Treatment -> Aftercare).
15. **Before & After Gallery**: Filterable gallery of results based on service category.
16. **Patient Success Stories**: Brief quotes related to specific services.
17. **FAQ for Services**: Accordion with common questions about procedures (pain, duration).
18. **Booking CTA**: "Schedule your consultation today."
19. **Global Footer (Top)**: Standard footer links.
20. **Global Footer (Bottom)**: Legal links.
