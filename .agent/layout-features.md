# Layout Blueprint: Features (Services) Page

1. **Header**: Global navigation.
2. **Hero Section**: Title: "Comprehensive Dental Services." Subtitle: "Gentle, modern care for every stage of life."
3. **Service Category Menu (Sticky/Anchor Links)**: Quick links to jump to Preventive, Restorative, Cosmetic, etc.
4. **Preventive Dentistry Section**: Details on cleanings, exams, oral cancer screenings, sealants.
5. **Restorative Dentistry Section**: Details on fillings, crowns, bridges, implants, root canals. Emphasis on pain-free procedures.
6. **Cosmetic Dentistry Section**: Details on teeth whitening, veneers, smile makeovers.
7. **Pediatric Care Section**: Special focus on making kids comfortable.
8. **Orthodontics Section**: Clear aligners, traditional braces.
9. **Emergency Services Section**: What to do in an emergency, how to contact.
10. **Technology Highlight**: How modern tech makes these services better/faster.
11. **Final CTA**: "Not sure what you need? Schedule a consultation."
12. **Footer**: Global footer.
