# Layout Documentation: Services/Features Page

## Structural Intent
This page acts as a deep-dive menu into the core treatments (Botox, Keratin, Bespoke Color). The layout is structured, informative, and visually rich to justify premium pricing.

## Section Details

### 1. Hero
- **Layout:** Centered, bold typography over a subtle, looping background video or abstract texture.
- **Rhythm:** Focused, minimal distraction.
- **Intent:** Introduce the suite of premium treatments.

### 2. The Signature Treatments (Alternating Rows)
- **Layout:** A series of alternating 50/50 split sections (Text Left/Image Right, then Image Left/Text Right).
- **Rhythm:** Zig-zag pattern, rhythmic and predictable.
- **Intent:** Provide detailed, easily digestible breakdowns of complex services.

### 3. Ingredient / Technology Spotlight
- **Layout:** A 3 or 4-column grid with small icons or macro-photography of products.
- **Rhythm:** Uniform, technical.
- **Intent:** Highlight the science and premium products behind the treatments.

### 4. Before & After Showcase
- **Layout:** A grid of interactive sliders or high-contrast side-by-side images.
- **Rhythm:** Visual evidence, grid-based.
- **Intent:** Undeniable proof of expertise.

### 5. The Process / What to Expect
- **Layout:** A numbered list or a stepped diagram.
- **Rhythm:** Step-by-step logical flow.
- **Intent:** Manage expectations and demystify the experience.

### 6. CTA (Consultation)
- **Layout:** Full-width bold section inviting users to book a consultation.
- **Rhythm:** High impact.
- **Intent:** Drive conversions based on the established value.
