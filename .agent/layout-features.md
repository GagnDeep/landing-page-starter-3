# Layout Blueprint: Features / Services Overview

This document outlines the structure for highlighting the core features and service categories, acting as a gateway to specific offerings.

## 1. Hero: Services Gateway
- **Intent:** Clear declaration of offerings.
- **Rhythm:** Clean, typography-driven hero. No background image, just soft `--background`.
- **Elements:** "Bespoke Beauty Services," short subheadline.

## 2. Category Navigation (Sticky Tabs)
- **Intent:** Allow quick jumping between Hair, Makeup, Nails, Academy.
- **Rhythm:** Horizontal scrolling or flex-wrap tab list, sticky below the main header.
- **Elements:** Text labels with active state underline (accent color).

## 3. Hair Artistry Category
- **Intent:** Showcase hair services.
- **Rhythm:** Large introductory image, followed by a 2-column list of specific services.
- **Elements:** Styling, Coloring, Treatments. "View Full Menu" link.

## 4. Bridal & Event Makeup
- **Intent:** Highlight high-ticket makeup services.
- **Rhythm:** Alternating image/text blocks (zig-zag layout).
- **Elements:** Focus on transformation and longevity of the makeup.

## 5. Nail & Spa Aesthetics
- **Intent:** Detail supplementary services.
- **Rhythm:** Grid of square cards with soft hover effects.
- **Elements:** Manicures, Pedicures, Spa rituals.

## 6. The Home Service Protocol (Feature Focus)
- **Intent:** Detail the specific features of the home service (hygiene, equipment).
- **Rhythm:** 3-column layout with custom iconography.
- **Elements:** "Sterilized Kits," "Punctual Arrivals," "Complete Cleanup."

## 7. Featured Service Video
- **Intent:** Visual demonstration of a signature service.
- **Rhythm:** Full-width video container.
- **Elements:** High-quality process video, minimal playback controls.

## 8. Consultations
- **Intent:** Encourage users who are unsure what they need.
- **Rhythm:** Distinct highlight box, warm background (`--secondary`).
- **Elements:** "Not sure? Book a free virtual consultation."

## 9. Product Recommendations
- **Intent:** Link services to retail products.
- **Rhythm:** Carousel of products related to the services above.
- **Elements:** Product image, name, "Shop" button.

## 10. CTA: View Full Pricing
- **Intent:** Drive users to the detailed pricing and booking page.
- **Rhythm:** Centered, large button.
- **Elements:** "Explore Pricing & Packages."
