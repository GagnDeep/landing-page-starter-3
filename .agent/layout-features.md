# Features Page Layout Architecture

## 1. Hero Section (`features-hero`)
- **Intent:** Introduce the comprehensive nature of the services.
- **Rhythm:** A split-screen layout with a compelling image on one side and introductory text on the other.

## 2. Core Feature 1: The Editorial Approach (`feature-editorial`)
- **Intent:** Highlight the cinematic style.
- **Rhythm:** Alternating image-left, text-right layout.

## 3. Core Feature 2: Unobtrusive Presence (`feature-candid`)
- **Intent:** Emphasize the candid, documentary style.
- **Rhythm:** Alternating text-left, image-right layout.

## 4. Core Feature 3: Heirloom Quality (`feature-heirloom`)
- **Intent:** Focus on the final deliverables (albums, prints).
- **Rhythm:** Alternating image-left, text-right layout.

## 5. Technology & Gear (`tech-gear`)
- **Intent:** Reassure clients of professional-grade equipment (subtly, not overly technical).
- **Rhythm:** A clean list or a visually subdued section outlining the tools used to capture the day.

## 6. The Editing Process (`editing-process`)
- **Intent:** Explain the meticulous post-production work.
- **Rhythm:** A subtle before/after image slider or a two-column explanation of color grading and retouching.

## 7. Deliverables Timeline (`deliverables-timeline`)
- **Intent:** Manage expectations on when clients receive their photos.
- **Rhythm:** A simple, elegant timeline or numbered list.

## 8. Final CTA (`features-cta`)
- **Intent:** Drive them to pricing or contact.
- **Rhythm:** Standard high-contrast CTA block.
