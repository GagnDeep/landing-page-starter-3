# Layout: Features/Services Page Blueprint

This document outlines the structure for detailing the specific services and features of the clinic.

1. **Hero Section:** Clear, authoritative headline defining the scope of expertise.
2. **Service Navigation:** Sticky sub-nav or sidebar to jump between categories (General, Cosmetic, Implants, Orthodontics).
3. **Service Details (Repeating Pattern):**
   - High-quality abstract or clinical imagery.
   - Clear description, benefits, and "What to expect".
   - Pricing/Insurance note if applicable.
4. **Technology Highlight:** Specific equipment used (e.g., 3D Scanners, Laser Dentistry) that makes the service superior.
5. **Patient Outcomes:** Specific testimonials related to the services on the page.
6. **FAQ per Service:** Accordions specific to the anxieties of each procedure.
7. **Consultation CTA:** "Schedule a consultation to discuss your personalized treatment plan."
