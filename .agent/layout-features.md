# Features/Services Layout Blueprint

## Section 1: Services Hub Hero
- **Intent:** "Comprehensive Dental Care." Overview of what the clinic offers.
- **Rhythm:** Bold headline, subtle background animation (e.g., slow wave).

## Section 2: Specialty Spotlight: Invisible Aligners
- **Intent:** The flagship service. Deep dive into benefits.
- **Rhythm:** Massive, full-screen takeover section, dark theme for contrast.

## Section 3: The Aligner Process
- **Intent:** How it works (Scan, Plan, Wear, Smile).
- **Rhythm:** 4-step horizontal process bar.

## Section 4: Aligner Benefits vs Traditional Braces
- **Intent:** Comparison chart.
- **Rhythm:** Table or side-by-side list with green checks/red crosses.

## Section 5: Specialty Spotlight: Aesthetic Dentistry
- **Intent:** Veneers, bonding, smile makeovers.
- **Rhythm:** Split screen (Before/After interactive slider).

## Section 6: Teeth Whitening Services
- **Intent:** Quick, noticeable results.
- **Rhythm:** Clean, bright, light-themed section.

## Section 7: General Dentistry & Preventative Care
- **Intent:** Cleanings, exams, X-rays.
- **Rhythm:** 3-column card grid.

## Section 8: Painless Root Canals
- **Intent:** Address the biggest fear. Emphasize modern techniques.
- **Rhythm:** Soft, calming colors, reassuring typography.

## Section 9: Dental Implants & Restorations
- **Intent:** Long-term solutions for missing teeth.
- **Rhythm:** Informational layout, diagram of an implant.

## Section 10: Pediatric Dentistry
- **Intent:** Gentle care for children.
- **Rhythm:** Slightly more playful font weights, warmer colors.

## Section 11: Emergency Dental Care
- **Intent:** What to do in an emergency. 24/7 availability.
- **Rhythm:** High urgency styling, red/accent borders.

## Section 12: Technology Used per Service
- **Intent:** Show the tools behind the treatments.
- **Rhythm:** Tooltip-heavy interactive diagram.

## Section 13: Service Guarantees
- **Intent:** Assurances of quality and comfort.
- **Rhythm:** Badge icons with short text.

## Section 14: Maintenance & Aftercare
- **Intent:** How to keep the results.
- **Rhythm:** Downloadable PDF link block or simple list.

## Section 15: Featured Patient Case Study
- **Intent:** In-depth look at a complex smile transformation.
- **Rhythm:** Editorial layout, large imagery, multi-paragraph text.

## Section 16: Custom Treatment Plans
- **Intent:** Every smile is unique.
- **Rhythm:** Centered text block.

## Section 17: Service FAQ
- **Intent:** Specific questions about treatments (pain, duration).
- **Rhythm:** Accordion list.

## Section 18: Pricing & Insurance (Teaser)
- **Intent:** Link to full pricing page.
- **Rhythm:** Soft banner.

## Section 19: Book a Specific Service
- **Intent:** CTA with a dropdown to select the desired service.
- **Rhythm:** Form-focused layout.

## Section 20: Standard Footer
- **Intent:** End of page navigation.
- **Rhythm:** Inherits global footer.
