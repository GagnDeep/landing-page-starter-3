# Layout Blueprint: Services (Features)

**Purpose**: A comprehensive catalog of clinical services. Must clearly explain complex dental procedures in accessible language, emphasizing the clinic's specialized implant capabilities.

## Sections

1.  **Global Navigation Bar**: Standard sticky header.
2.  **Page Header (Hero)**: H1 ("Specialized Dental Care"). Subtitle emphasizing comprehensive and painless treatments.
3.  **Services Navigation / Filter Bar**: Sticky sub-nav to jump to specific categories (Implants, Cosmetic, General, Ortho).
4.  **Category: Dental Implants (Highlight)**: The flagship service. Large imagery. Explains single, multiple, and full-mouth (All-on-4) implants.
5.  **Implant Technology & Process**: Diagram or step-by-step section specifically for implants.
6.  **Category: Cosmetic Dentistry**: Section detailing Veneers, Teeth Whitening, and Smile Designing. Focus on aesthetic outcomes.
7.  **Cosmetic Gallery Teaser**: Small before/after slider for cosmetic cases.
8.  **Category: General Dentistry**: Fillings, RCT (Root Canal Treatment), Extractions. Emphasize painless techniques.
9.  **Category: Orthodontics**: Braces and Clear Aligners. Target demographics (teens and adults).
10. **Category: Pediatric Dentistry**: Softened tone. Emphasizing a child-friendly environment and preventative care.
11. **Category: Periodontics (Gum Care)**: Scaling, flap surgery. Focus on foundational oral health.
12. **Category: Prosthodontics**: Crowns, Bridges, Dentures.
13. **Emergency Services Detail**: What constitutes an emergency, immediate steps, and contact protocol.
14. **Technology Integration**: How specific tools (Lasers, Intraoral cameras) enhance the services listed above.
15. **Sedation & Comfort Options**: Details on local anesthesia techniques, conscious sedation (if applicable), and anxiety management.
16. **Post-Treatment Care Overview**: Brief guidelines on what to do after major procedures, linking to detailed PDF/pages.
17. **Service-Specific Testimonials**: Carousel filtering testimonials based on the service category (e.g., only implant reviews).
18. **Pricing & Insurance Teaser**: Brief section linking to the full pricing/financial page.
19. **Global Pre-Footer**: Specific CTA ("Schedule a Consultation").
20. **Global Footer**: Standard footer.
