# Layout Blueprint: Features / Technology Page
**Status:** Defined (Phase 1)
**Vibe:** Modern, Reassuring, Pain-free

## Sections
1. **Hero:** Focus on modern, comfortable care.
2. **Comfort Menu:** List of amenities (e.g., noise-canceling headphones, blankets).
3. **Advanced Technology Grid:** Cards detailing equipment (e.g., Digital X-Rays, Intraoral Cameras) and how they benefit the patient (less radiation, faster).
4. **Safety & Sterilization:** Reassuring section on hygiene protocols.
5. **Patient Journey:** Step-by-step graphic of what to expect during a visit.
6. **Final CTA:** Book Appointment.
