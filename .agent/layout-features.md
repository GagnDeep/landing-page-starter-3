# Features / Services Page Layout Architecture

## Section 1: Hero (`Services Hero`)
- **Intent:** Introduce the comprehensive list of services.
- **Rhythm:** Clean, typography-driven header with subtle background texture.

## Section 2: Nail Artistry (`Nail Services`)
- **Intent:** Deep dive into nail services (chrome, gel, extensions).
- **Rhythm:** Alternating image/text blocks detailing the process, hygiene, and options.

## Section 3: Hair Care & Styling (`Hair Services`)
- **Intent:** Focus on advanced cuts, curly hair care, and coloring.
- **Rhythm:** Similar alternating rhythm, featuring before/after glimpses.

## Section 4: Spa & Relax (`Spa Services`)
- **Intent:** Detail relaxing treatments.
- **Rhythm:** 3-column grid of service cards.

## Section 5: Add-ons & Extras (`Enhancements`)
- **Intent:** Upsell smaller services.
- **Rhythm:** Simple list format or compact cards.

## Section 6: Booking CTA (`Book Now`)
- **Intent:** Conversion.
- **Rhythm:** High-contrast block urging the user to book an appointment.
