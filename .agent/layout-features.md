# Layout: Features (Clinical Services) Page

## Structural Intent
This page serves as the master directory for all clinical services offered, organized logically and presented with authority. It acts as a hub linking to individual service detail pages.

## Visual Rhythm
Highly structured and easy to scan. Uses repeating patterns (e.g., alternating image/text blocks or a well-defined grid) to present a large amount of information cleanly without overwhelming the user.

## Sections

1.  **Hero Section:** Title: "Clinical Excellence." Subtitle: "Comprehensive, international-standard dental care." Clean, typographic focus with minimal imagery.
2.  **Service Navigation / Anchor Links:** A sticky or prominent horizontal menu allowing users to jump quickly to specific service categories (e.g., Implants, Aesthetics, General).
3.  **Category 1: Implantology (Hero Service):** A large, prominent section. High-quality image or abstract graphic representing implants. Detailed description of the implant process.
4.  **Implant Sub-services:** A grid detailing specific implant services (Single Tooth, All-on-4, Zygomatic Implants, Bone Grafting). Links to detailed pages.
5.  **Category 2: Aesthetic Dentistry:** A section focused on the art of smiles. Lighter, more elegant tone.
6.  **Aesthetic Sub-services:** Grid detailing Veneers, Smile Designing, Teeth Whitening, Gum Contouring.
7.  **Category 3: Orthodontics & Aligners:** Focused on precision and modern alignment techniques.
8.  **Orthodontic Sub-services:** Grid detailing Clear Aligners (Invisalign), Ceramic Braces, Lingual Braces.
9.  **Category 4: Restorative & Endodontics:** Emphasizing pain-free, precise treatments to save teeth.
10. **Restorative Sub-services:** Grid detailing Root Canal Treatment (Microscopic), Crowns & Bridges, Inlays/Onlays.
11. **Category 5: Periodontics (Gum Care):** Highlighting the foundation of oral health.
12. **Periodontic Sub-services:** Grid detailing Flap Surgery, Laser Gum Treatment, Scaling & Root Planing.
13. **Category 6: Pediatric Dentistry:** A slightly softer, more welcoming tone emphasizing gentle care for children.
14. **Category 7: Oral & Maxillofacial Surgery:** Authoritative tone for complex procedures like wisdom tooth extraction or jaw surgery.
15. **Technology Integration:** A section explaining how advanced technology (3D scanning, lasers) enhances all the above services.
16. **The Diagnostic Process:** A step-by-step breakdown of how a treatment plan is formulated (Consultation, Scans, Discussion).
17. **Materials & Quality:** A dedicated block explaining the premium materials used (e.g., specific brands of implants or ceramics) to justify the high-end positioning.
18. **Before & After Gallery Teaser:** A link or small slider showing the results of the various services.
19. **Final CTA:** "Schedule Your Comprehensive Evaluation." High contrast block.
20. **Footer:** Standard global footer.
