# Features / Services Page Layout Blueprint

## Visual Rhythm & Layout Intent
Detailed, structured presentation of all clinical services. Easy to navigate, reassuring, and informative.

## Sections
1. **Hero:** "Our Clinical Services", overview text.
2. **Service Index/Navigation:** Quick links or tabs to jump to specific treatments.
3. **Dental Implants:** Details, technology used, benefits.
4. **Clear Aligners & Orthodontics:** Process, brands used, suitability.
5. **Cosmetic Dentistry (Smile Design, Veneers):** Aesthetic focus, before/after examples.
6. **General & Preventive Care:** Root canals, extractions, cleaning.
7. **Pediatric Dentistry:** Approach to child dental care.
8. **Technology & Equipment:** Detail on the specific tech enabling these features.
9. **FAQ (Service Specific):** Accordion for common questions per service category.
10. **Final CTA:** Book a consultation for a specific service.
