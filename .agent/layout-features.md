# Features (Services) Page Layout Blueprint

1. **Global Notice Bar**: Top banner.
2. **Primary Navigation**: Sticky header.
3. **Hero Section**: "Comprehensive Dental Care" with a reassuring image.
4. **Service Navigation/Anchor Links**: Quick links to jump to specific service categories on the page.
5. **Pediatric Dentistry (Category Header)**: Intro text about specialized care for children.
6. **Pediatric Services Details**: Cards/list (e.g., Sealants, Fluoride, First Visit).
7. **Orthodontics & Aligners (Category Header)**: Intro text about straightening smiles.
8. **Invisible Aligners Deep Dive**: Highlighting clear aligners, process, and benefits over traditional braces.
9. **Traditional Braces**: Information for cases where traditional methods are best.
10. **Aesthetic/Cosmetic Dentistry (Category Header)**: Enhancing the natural smile.
11. **Cosmetic Services Details**: Teeth Whitening, Veneers, Smile Makeovers.
12. **General & Preventive Care (Category Header)**: The foundation of dental health.
13. **Preventive Services Details**: Checkups, Cleanings, Oral Cancer Screenings.
14. **Restorative Dentistry (Category Header)**: Repairing and restoring function.
15. **Restorative Services Details**: Fillings, Crowns, Bridges, Implants.
16. **Pain-Free / Sedation Options**: Explaining nitrous oxide or other comfort measures.
17. **Technology Spotlight**: How specific tools (e.g., 3D imaging) improve these services.
18. **Patient Education**: Links to blog posts or resources about specific treatments.
19. **Book a Consultation CTA**: Encouraging users to discuss which service is right for them.
20. **Global Footer**: Standard footer.
