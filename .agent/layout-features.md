# Dentocare Features (Services) Page Layout

## 1. Hero Section
- **Layout:** Service-focused hero with high-quality clinical imagery.
- **Rhythm:** Clear and direct.

## 2. General Dentistry
- **Layout:** Grid or list detailing routine checkups, cleanings, and fillings.
- **Rhythm:** Informative.

## 3. Cosmetic Dentistry
- **Layout:** Before/after galleries and detailed descriptions of veneers, whitening, etc.
- **Rhythm:** Aspirational and visual.

## 4. Orthodontics
- **Layout:** Detailed sections on braces and clear aligners.
- **Rhythm:** Educational.

## 5. Restorative & Implants
- **Layout:** Technical but accessible diagrams and explanations of procedures.
- **Rhythm:** Reassuring.

## 6. Pediatric Care
- **Layout:** Soft, friendly section focusing on children's dental health.
- **Rhythm:** Welcoming.

## 7. Advanced Technology
- **Layout:** Highlights of clinic tech (3D scanning, laser dentistry).
- **Rhythm:** Premium.

## 8. CTA
- **Layout:** Action-oriented block.
- **Rhythm:** Urgent but calm.
