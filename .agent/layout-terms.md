# Terms of Service Layout Blueprint

## Section 1: Legal Header
- **Intent:** Clear, serious, unambiguous title. Last updated date.
- **Rhythm:** Minimalist, centered, no imagery.

## Section 2: Table of Contents
- **Intent:** Easy navigation of a long document.
- **Rhythm:** Sticky sidebar on desktop, inline list on mobile.

## Section 3: Acceptance of Terms
- **Intent:** Standard legal boilerplate.
- **Rhythm:** Standard paragraph text.

## Section 4: Use of the Website
- **Intent:** Rules for interacting with the site.
- **Rhythm:** Standard paragraph text.

## Section 5: Medical Disclaimer
- **Intent:** Crucial. State that the website is not a substitute for professional medical advice.
- **Rhythm:** Highlighted box, bold text.

## Section 6: User Accounts (If applicable)
- **Intent:** Rules regarding patient portal access.
- **Rhythm:** Standard paragraph text.

## Section 7: Booking & Cancellations
- **Intent:** Legal standing for appointment policies.
- **Rhythm:** Bulleted list within a paragraph block.

## Section 8: Payment Terms
- **Intent:** How fees are handled legally.
- **Rhythm:** Standard paragraph text.

## Section 9: Intellectual Property
- **Intent:** Protecting the clinic's content, logos, images.
- **Rhythm:** Standard paragraph text.

## Section 10: User-Generated Content
- **Intent:** Rules regarding reviews or submitted photos.
- **Rhythm:** Standard paragraph text.

## Section 11: Third-Party Links
- **Intent:** Disclaimer regarding links to aligner manufacturers, etc.
- **Rhythm:** Standard paragraph text.

## Section 12: Limitation of Liability
- **Intent:** Standard legal protection.
- **Rhythm:** ALL CAPS OR BOLD TEXT (as legally required).

## Section 13: Indemnification
- **Intent:** User agrees not to sue for certain things.
- **Rhythm:** Standard paragraph text.

## Section 14: Governing Law
- **Intent:** Jurisdiction (Chandigarh, India).
- **Rhythm:** Standard paragraph text.

## Section 15: Changes to Terms
- **Intent:** Right to update the document.
- **Rhythm:** Standard paragraph text.

## Section 16: Severability
- **Intent:** Standard legal boilerplate.
- **Rhythm:** Standard paragraph text.

## Section 17: Entire Agreement
- **Intent:** Standard legal boilerplate.
- **Rhythm:** Standard paragraph text.

## Section 18: Contact Information for Legal Matters
- **Intent:** Where to send notices.
- **Rhythm:** Contact block format.

## Section 19: Print Document Option
- **Intent:** Accessibility/Utility.
- **Rhythm:** Small print icon/button.

## Section 20: Standard Footer
- **Intent:** Consistent navigation ending.
- **Rhythm:** Inherits global footer.
