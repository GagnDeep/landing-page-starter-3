# Layout Blueprint: Terms of Service

**Purpose**: Legal protection and clear outlining of clinic policies. Layout must be clean, readable, and accessible.

## Sections

1.  **Global Navigation Bar**: Standard sticky header.
2.  **Page Header (Hero)**: Minimalist H1 ("Terms of Service"). Last updated date.
3.  **Introduction**: Legal preamble.
4.  **Content Container**: Narrow max-width (`max-w-3xl`) for optimal reading length. Plain typography.
5.  **Section: Medical Disclaimer**: Clarifying that website information is not medical advice.
6.  **Section: Patient-Dentist Relationship**: Defining when the relationship begins (in-clinic, not online).
7.  **Section: Treatment Plans & Estimates**: Clarifying that estimates can change based on clinical findings.
8.  **Section: Appointments & Cancellations**: Official policy on no-shows and late fees.
9.  **Section: Payment Terms**: Obligations for payment at the time of service.
10. **Section: Intellectual Property**: Copyright notice regarding website content, images, and logos.
11. **Section: User Conduct**: Rules for using the website or interacting with clinic staff online.
12. **Section: Third-Party Links**: Disclaimer regarding external links (e.g., to financing partners).
13. **Section: Limitation of Liability**: Standard legal protection clause.
14. **Section: Indemnification**: Standard legal clause.
15. **Section: Governing Law**: Specifying jurisdiction (Patiala, Punjab, India).
16. **Section: Changes to Terms**: Right to modify the agreement.
17. **Section: Severability**: Legal boilerplate.
18. **Contact for Legal Queries**: Email address for the clinic administration.
19. **Print Button**: Utility link to print the page for records.
20. **Global Footer**: Standard footer.
