# Layout: Terms of Service Page

## Structural Intent
To clearly, legally, and professionally state the terms under which users interact with the website and the clinic. The layout should prioritize readability and trust.

## Visual Rhythm
Minimalist and text-heavy. A clean, single-column reading experience with strong typographic hierarchy to separate clauses and sections. Ample line height for readability.

## Sections

1.  **Hero Section:** Title: "Terms of Service." Subtitle: "Last updated: [Date]." Minimal styling, purely informative.
2.  **Introduction:** A brief statement explaining that by using the website or booking a consultation, the user agrees to these terms.
3.  **Use of Website Content:** Terms regarding copyright, intellectual property, and the non-medical nature of the website information (disclaimer that web info does not replace a consultation).
4.  **Booking & Appointments:** Rules regarding appointment deposits, cancellations, and no-shows.
5.  **Financial Terms:** General statements on payment expectations, non-refundable fees, and estimates.
6.  **Medical Disclaimer:** A strict legal disclaimer that all medical procedures carry risks and individual results may vary.
7.  **User Conduct:** Rules for interacting with the clinic (online or in person), prohibiting abusive behavior.
8.  **Governing Law:** Stating that the terms are governed by the laws of Chandigarh / India.
9.  **Changes to Terms:** A note that the clinic can update these terms at any time.
10. **Contact for Legal Queries:** Email address for the legal or administrative team.
11. **Footer:** Standard global footer.
