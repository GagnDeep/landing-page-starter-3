# Terms of Service Layout Blueprint

## Goal
Present legal information clearly and accessibly, maintaining the brand's sophisticated typography even in dense text.

## Sections
1. **Hero Section:** "Terms of Service." Last updated date.
2. **Table of Contents:** Sticky sidebar or top list of links to jump to specific sections.
3. **Content Blocks:** Standard legal sections (e.g., Use of Site, Medical Disclaimer, Intellectual Property, Limitation of Liability). Structured with clear `h2` and `h3` tags.
4. **Contact Info for Legal Inquiries:** "If you have questions about these terms..."
