# Layout Blueprint: Terms Page

1. **Header**: Global navigation.
2. **Hero Section**: Title: "Terms of Service." Subtitle: "Please read these terms carefully." (Minimalist styling).
3. **Last Updated Date**: Clear timestamp.
4. **Introduction / Acceptance of Terms**: Standard legal text block.
5. **Use of Website**: Rules regarding acceptable use, intellectual property.
6. **Medical Disclaimer**: Crucial section stating website info is not a substitute for professional medical advice.
7. **Appointment Policies**: General terms regarding booking, cancellations, and no-shows.
8. **Limitation of Liability**: Standard legal protections.
9. **Contact for Legal Queries**: Where to send questions regarding terms.
10. **Footer**: Global footer.
