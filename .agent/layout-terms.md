# Layout: Terms of Service Page
**Goal:** Clearly and legally outline the rules for using the website and clinic services.
**Rhythm:** Standard legal format, highly readable text.

1. **Global Header (Sticky)**: Standard navigation.
2. **Hero Section**: Simple title "Terms of Service". Date of last update.
3. **Table of Contents**: Sticky sidebar or top list of links to jump to sections.
4. **1. Acceptance of Terms**: Standard legal boilerplate.
5. **2. Services Provided**: Description of the dental services and website use.
6. **3. Appointments and Cancellations**: Policy on booking, no-shows, and cancellation fees.
7. **4. Payment and Billing**: Rules regarding payments, insurance processing, and estimates.
8. **5. Patient Responsibilities**: Expectations of patient behavior and accurate medical history provision.
9. **6. Intellectual Property**: Ownership of website content, logo, and materials.
10. **7. Limitation of Liability**: Standard medical/dental liability disclaimers.
11. **8. Privacy Policy Reference**: Link to the separate privacy policy page.
12. **9. External Links**: Disclaimer regarding third-party links on the site.
13. **10. Modifications to Terms**: Statement that terms can change.
14. **11. Governing Law**: Jurisdiction (Chandigarh, India).
15. **12. Contact Information**: How to contact for legal inquiries.
16. **Print Button**: Easy way to print the document.
17. **Back to Top Button**: Floating action button.
18. **Newsletter Signup**: Standard footer signup.
19. **Global Footer (Top)**: Standard footer links.
20. **Global Footer (Bottom)**: Legal links.
