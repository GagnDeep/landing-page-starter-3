# Terms of Service Layout Blueprint

**Goal:** Legal compliance presented cleanly.

1. **Header:** Simple text header "Terms and Conditions".
2. **Last Updated Date:** Prominent display.
3. **Introduction:** Scope of the agreement.
4. **Use of Website:** Rules for using the online booking and information.
5. **Medical Disclaimer:** Explicit statement that website info is not a substitute for professional medical advice.
6. **Appointment Policies:** Cancellation and no-show policies.
7. **Intellectual Property:** Copyright notice for website content.
8. **Contact for Legal Queries:** Email address for the privacy officer/admin.
