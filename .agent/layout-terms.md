# Terms of Service Page Layout Blueprint

1. **Hero Header:** Page title "Terms of Service".
2. **Last Updated Date:** Clear indication of when the terms were last revised.
3. **Content Sections:** Standard legal text sections (e.g., Use of Website, Intellectual Property, Limitation of Liability, Governing Law). Uses clean, readable typography with clear headings.
4. **Contact Info for Legal Inquiries:** Email address for legal questions.
