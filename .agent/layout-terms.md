# Terms of Service Page Layout Blueprint

1. **Global Notice Bar**: Top banner.
2. **Primary Navigation**: Sticky header.
3. **Hero Section**: Clean, simple "Terms of Service" or "Terms and Conditions".
4. **Last Updated Date**: Prominent text indicating the document version.
5. **Acceptance of Terms**: Standard legal preamble.
6. **Use of the Website**: Rules for navigating and using site features.
7. **Medical Disclaimer**: Crucial section stating the site is for information, not a substitute for professional medical advice.
8. **Patient-Provider Relationship**: Clarifying that using the site doesn't create a doctor-patient relationship until a formal appointment is made.
9. **Booking & Cancellations Policy**: Rules regarding online appointment requests.
10. **Intellectual Property**: Copyright notice for site content, images, and logos.
11. **User Communications**: Policy on submitting forms or emails.
12. **Third-Party Links**: Disclaimer regarding external links provided on the site.
13. **Limitation of Liability**: Standard legal protection clause.
14. **Indemnification**: Standard legal clause.
15. **Changes to Terms**: Right to modify the terms.
16. **Governing Law**: Specifying jurisdiction (e.g., Chandigarh, India).
17. **Severability**: Standard legal clause.
18. **Contact Information for Legal Inquiries**: Specific email or address for terms-related questions.
19. **Printable Version Link**: Option to print the document.
20. **Global Footer**: Standard footer.
