# Dentistree - Terms of Service Layout Blueprint

**Goal:** Provide clear, legally sound terms of service in a readable format.

## Sections (20+)

1. **Hero Section:** "Terms of Service".
2. **Last Updated Date:** Text indicating the effective date.
3. **1. Agreement to Terms:** Introduction to the binding nature of the document.
4. **2. Services Provided:** Description of the website's purpose and the clinic's medical disclaimer (website is not medical advice).
5. **3. User Accounts:** Rules regarding patient portal access (if applicable).
6. **4. Appointment Policies:** Rules on booking, cancellations, and no-shows.
7. **5. Payment Terms:** General statement on responsibility for payment and insurance claims.
8. **6. Intellectual Property:** Copyright notice for website content, images, and logos.
9. **7. User Content:** Rules for submitting reviews or testimonials.
10. **8. Prohibited Activities:** Restrictions on misusing the website.
11. **9. Third-Party Links:** Disclaimer regarding external links on the blog/site.
12. **10. Disclaimer of Warranties:** Standard legal "as-is" clause.
13. **11. Limitation of Liability:** Caps on legal liability.
14. **12. Indemnification:** User responsibility clause.
15. **13. Governing Law:** Jurisdiction clause (e.g., Chandigarh laws apply).
16. **14. Dispute Resolution:** How legal disputes will be handled.
17. **15. Severability:** Standard legal clause.
18. **16. Changes to Terms:** Right to update the document.
19. **Contact Information for Legal Inquiries:** Email address for questions about terms.
20. **Global Footer:** Standard footer.
