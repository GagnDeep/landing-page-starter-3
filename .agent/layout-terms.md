# Terms Page Layout Architecture

## 1. Header (`terms-header`)
- **Intent:** Clear, legal indication.
- **Rhythm:** Simple, text-only header ("Terms of Service").

## 2. Last Updated Date (`terms-date`)
- **Intent:** Legal clarity.
- **Rhythm:** Small, muted text at the top.

## 3. Content Sections (`terms-content`)
- **Intent:** Present legal text legibly.
- **Rhythm:** A long-form text layout. Use clear headings (h2, h3) and ordered lists. Keep line lengths readable (max ~70 characters).

## 4. Contact for Legal Inquiries (`terms-contact`)
- **Intent:** Provide a point of contact.
- **Rhythm:** A short sentence at the end with an email address.
