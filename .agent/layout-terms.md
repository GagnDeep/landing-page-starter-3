# Terms & Conditions Layout Blueprint

## Visual Rhythm & Layout Intent
Standard legal layout. Clean typography, high readability, no distractions.

## Sections
1. **Hero/Header:** "Terms and Conditions", Last updated date.
2. **Content Body:** Standard legal text sections (Acceptance of terms, Clinic policies, Cancellation policies, Payment terms, Liability).
3. **Contact Information:** Where to direct legal inquiries.
