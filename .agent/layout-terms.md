# Layout Documentation: Terms & Conditions Page

## Structural Intent
Purely functional, highly legible typography. No distractions.

## Section Details

### 1. Simple Header
- **Layout:** Left-aligned or centered standard heading.

### 2. Long-form Text
- **Layout:** A narrow, readable max-width container (e.g., `max-w-3xl`).
- **Rhythm:** Standard paragraph spacing, clear hierarchical headings (H2, H3).
- **Intent:** Legal compliance and readability.
