# Layout Blueprint: Terms of Service

This document outlines the structure for the Terms of Service page, ensuring legal compliance while maintaining brand aesthetics.

## 1. Header
- **Intent:** Clear identification of the document.
- **Rhythm:** Minimal, typographic header.
- **Elements:** "Terms of Service," "Last Updated: [Date]."

## 2. Introduction
- **Intent:** State the purpose of the document.
- **Rhythm:** Standard text block.
- **Elements:** Agreement to terms by using the site/services.

## 3. Services Overview
- **Intent:** Define what is being provided.
- **Rhythm:** Text section with clear subheadings.
- **Elements:** Description of salon, home, and academy services.

## 4. User Responsibilities
- **Intent:** Outline client obligations.
- **Rhythm:** Bulleted list for readability.
- **Elements:** Providing accurate info, appropriate environment for home services.

## 5. Booking, Cancellation, and Refunds
- **Intent:** State the financial policies clearly.
- **Rhythm:** Distinct section, perhaps with slightly bolder typography for emphasis.
- **Elements:** Deposit requirements, 24/48 hour cancellation rules.

## 6. Intellectual Property
- **Intent:** Protect brand assets.
- **Rhythm:** Standard text block.
- **Elements:** Ownership of website content, logos, images.

## 7. Limitation of Liability
- **Intent:** Legal protection.
- **Rhythm:** Standard text block, often in all-caps as legally required (though styled elegantly).
- **Elements:** Standard legal disclaimers regarding service outcomes or allergic reactions.

## 8. Governing Law
- **Intent:** Establish jurisdiction.
- **Rhythm:** Short text block.
- **Elements:** "These terms are governed by the laws of India/Punjab."

## 9. Changes to Terms
- **Intent:** Right to update the document.
- **Rhythm:** Short text block.
- **Elements:** Notification process for changes.

## 10. Contact Information
- **Intent:** Where to send legal inquiries.
- **Rhythm:** Simple contact block.
- **Elements:** Legal email address or mailing address.
