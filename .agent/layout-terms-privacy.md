# Layout Blueprint: Terms & Privacy Pages

## Structural Slots (Applies to both pages)

1.  **Global Top Bar & Header** (Inherited)
2.  **Page Hero (Minimal)**
    *   *Intent:* Plain title (e.g., "Privacy Policy" or "Terms of Service"). Last updated date.
3.  **Content Container (Single Column, Narrow)**
    *   *Intent:* highly readable, well-spaced typography for legal text. Proper H2/H3 hierarchy for sections.
4.  **Table of Contents (Sticky sidebar on desktop)**
    *   *Intent:* Easy navigation through long legal documents.
5.  **Contact for Legal Queries**
    *   *Intent:* Brief block at the end indicating who to contact regarding privacy or terms.
6.  **Global Footer** (Inherited)
