# About Page Layout Blueprint

**Goal:** Deepen trust through the clinic's history, team expertise, and core philosophy.

1. **Hero Section:** Warm image of the clinic or team, title "Our Story, Your Smile".
2. **Clinic History / Legacy:** Timeline of Naresh Dental Clinic's establishment and growth in Patiala.
3. **Core Values:** Icons and text detailing Integrity, Hygiene, Compassion, Innovation.
4. **Detailed Profile: Dr. Anshum Gupta:** Extensive biography, education, certifications, and personal philosophy.
5. **The Specialist Team:** Grid of other doctors, hygienists, and support staff with brief bios.
6. **The Facility:** Highlighting the "clinical-yet-inviting" atmosphere, waiting room comforts, and treatment room technology.
7. **Community Involvement:** Any local outreach, free camps, or educational seminars in Patiala.
8. **Final CTA:** "Join Our Dental Family" linking to booking.
