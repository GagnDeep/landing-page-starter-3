# About Us Page Layout Intent

**Page Intent:** Tell the story of Dr. Sarabjeet Singh and the clinic's multi-decade history in Chandigarh.

## Sections
1. **`AboutHero`**: Large, sweeping header with a historic or foundational clinic photo.
2. **`OurStoryTimeline`**: Vertical timeline tracing the clinic from its founding to present day.
3. **`MeetTheDoctorDeepDive`**: Extensive biography, education, and philosophy of Dr. Singh.
4. **`OurTeamGrid`**: Photos and short bios of hygienists, assistants, and receptionists.
5. **`ClinicValuesBlocks`**: 3-column layout highlighting values (Painless, Honest, Advanced).
6. **`CommunityInvolvement`**: Section highlighting local charity or community work in Chandigarh.
7. **`AboutCTA`**: Invitation to experience the clinic personally.
