# Layout Documentation: About Page

## Structural Intent
The About page tells the story of Amil's Hair Lounge and Ajay Sir. The rhythm is narrative-driven, focusing on large imagery, expansive typography, and an editorial grid that feels like reading a premium magazine profile.

## Section Details

### 1. Hero (The Origin Story)
- **Layout:** 60/40 text-to-image split. Large serif typography for a bold opening statement.
- **Rhythm:** Highly asymmetrical, leaning heavily on whitespace.
- **Intent:** Set a personal, elevated tone.

### 2. The Philosophy
- **Layout:** Centered blockquote or statement text.
- **Rhythm:** Breathable, surrounded by vast empty space.
- **Intent:** Emphasize the core values (affordable luxury, rigorous education, expert treatments).

### 3. Ajay Sir (The Mastermind)
- **Layout:** Immersive portrait spanning one side, with a dense, structured biography on the other. Includes a "signature" graphic or personal quote.
- **Rhythm:** Dense text block balanced against a heavy image weight.
- **Intent:** Solidify authority and build a personal connection.

### 4. The Salon Space (Gallery)
- **Layout:** An asymmetrical masonry gallery highlighting the Leela Bhawan location.
- **Rhythm:** Visual exploration, varying aspect ratios.
- **Intent:** Showcase the premium physical environment.

### 5. The Team
- **Layout:** Clean, symmetrical grid of portraits.
- **Rhythm:** Orderly and respectful.
- **Intent:** Highlight the broader talent pool.

### 6. Timeline / Milestones
- **Layout:** A subtle vertical timeline or horizontal scroll detailing the salon's growth.
- **Rhythm:** Linear, chronological pacing.
- **Intent:** Establish longevity and success.

### 7. Footer & CTA
- **Layout:** Standard site footer preceded by a "Book an Appointment" CTA.
