# Layout: About Page
**Goal:** Build a deep personal connection and demonstrate clinical authority.
**Rhythm:** Narrative flow, moving from the founder's story to the team and clinic philosophy.

1. **Global Header (Sticky)**: Standard navigation.
2. **Hero Section**: Elegant, warm image of the clinic exterior or reception. Title: "Our Story."
3. **Mission & Vision Statement**: Centered, large text (Fraunces) outlining the core philosophy of compassionate care.
4. **Founder's Story (Dr. Kochar)**: Detailed biography. Left text, Right image. Focus on experience and patient care philosophy.
5. **Timeline / History**: Horizontal or vertical timeline of the clinic's growth and milestones.
6. **Our Core Values (Grid)**: 4-6 cards detailing values like Integrity, Empathy, Innovation.
7. **Meet the Specialists (Grid)**: Profiles of other doctors/specialists. Image, name, title, short bio.
8. **Meet the Support Staff**: Group photo or smaller grid for hygienists, receptionists. "The faces behind your smile."
9. **Clinic Environment / Tour**: Image gallery or grid showing treatment rooms, waiting area (focusing on comfort).
10. **Technology & Equipment**: Highlight state-of-the-art tools used to ensure painless and effective treatments.
11. **Community Involvement**: Section detailing local outreach, free camps, or sponsorships.
12. **Awards & Recognitions**: Grid of badges or certificates.
13. **Patient Care Approach**: Step-by-step graphic on what a patient experiences from walk-in to follow-up.
14. **Special Focus: Anxious Patients**: Section explaining how the clinic handles dental anxiety (sedation, environment).
15. **Special Focus: Elderly Care**: Section detailing accessibility and specialized geriatric dentistry.
16. **Special Focus: Pediatric Care**: Highlighting a kid-friendly environment.
17. **Testimonials (Deep Dive)**: 2-3 longer form stories or video testimonials from long-term patients.
18. **Call to Action**: "Join our dental family." Button to book an appointment.
19. **Global Footer (Top)**: Standard footer links.
20. **Global Footer (Bottom)**: Legal links.
