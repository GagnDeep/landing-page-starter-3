# About Page Layout Architecture

## 1. Page Header
- **Visuals:** Medium height banner, muted background or soft image overlay.
- **Content:** Title "About Us", Subtitle "Committed to Your Smile's Health and Beauty".

## 2. Our Story / History
- **Visuals:** Split layout.
- **Content:** The clinic's origins in Patiala, mission, and vision.

## 3. Meet Dr. Singh
- **Visuals:** Large professional portrait alongside detailed bio.
- **Content:** Credentials, specialization in implants, philosophy of care.

## 4. The Team
- **Visuals:** Grid of team members.
- **Content:** Dentists, hygienists, staff.

## 5. Clinic Tour Gallery
- **Visuals:** Carousel or masonry grid.
- **Content:** High-quality images of the clinic environment.

## 6. Call to Action
- **Visuals:** Centered text with button.
- **Content:** "Experience the difference. Book your visit."
