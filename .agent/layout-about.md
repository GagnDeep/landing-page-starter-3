# About Page Layout Blueprint

1. **Global Notice Bar**: Top banner for announcements.
2. **Primary Navigation**: Sticky header.
3. **Hero Section**: Large, high-quality photo of the clinic exterior or team, with the title "Our Story."
4. **The Dentistree Philosophy**: Core values and mission statement focusing on patient comfort and high-end care.
5. **Meet Dr. Siddharth Mahajan**: Detailed bio, credentials, specialty focus (e.g., orthodontics/aligners), and a personal quote.
6. **Meet Dr. Isha Mahajan**: Detailed bio, credentials, specialty focus (e.g., pediatric/general), and a personal quote.
7. **The Team / Support Staff**: Grid of hygienists, assistants, and front desk staff with warm, smiling photos.
8. **Clinic History / Journey**: Timeline or narrative of how Dentistree started and its commitment to Chandigarh.
9. **Our Approach to Dental Anxiety**: Dedicated section explaining techniques and environment designed to calm nervous patients.
10. **The Clinic Environment**: Highlighting the interior design choices that make it feel unlike a traditional "scary" clinic.
11. **Technology & Innovation**: Detail the modern equipment used (e.g., CBCT, intraoral scanners) and how it benefits the patient.
12. **Community Involvement**: Highlighting any local outreach, school visits, or charity work.
13. **Awards & Recognitions**: Display of local awards, certifications, or media mentions.
14. **Continuing Education**: Highlighting the doctors' commitment to staying updated with the latest dental advancements.
15. **Patient Testimonials (Focus on Trust)**: Reviews specifically mentioning the doctors' bedside manner and trust.
16. **Behind the Scenes**: Candid photos of the team working or interacting.
17. **Core Values Grid**: Icons and short text for values like "Integrity, Compassion, Excellence."
18. **Join Our Patient Family (CTA)**: Warm invitation to become a patient.
19. **Location/Contact Teaser**: Quick link to the contact page and map.
20. **Global Footer**: Standard footer.
