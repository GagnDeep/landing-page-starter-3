# Layout Blueprint: About Us

**Purpose**: To build deep trust. Focuses on the history of the clinic, the expertise of Dr. Anshum Gupta and the staff, and the core values of hygiene and patient comfort.

## Sections

1.  **Global Navigation Bar**: Standard sticky header.
2.  **Page Header (Hero)**: Minimal hero. Centered H1 ("About Naresh Dental Clinic"). Subtle background texture or soft gradient using the secondary teal color.
3.  **Our Story / Mission Statement**: Large, readable serif typography. A narrative block explaining the founding, vision, and commitment to Patiala.
4.  **Clinic History Timeline**: Vertical timeline detailing milestones (founding year, facility upgrades, awards).
5.  **Meet the Lead Dentist (Dr. Anshum Gupta)**: Deep dive profile. Large portrait. Extensive bio, education, specialties (Implants), and personal philosophy.
6.  **Credentials & Affiliations**: Grid of logos or certificates (IDA, ISO, etc.) rendered in monochrome to maintain elegance.
7.  **The Dental Team Grid**: 3-column grid of supporting dentists, hygienists, and support staff. Clean portrait photography, name, and title.
8.  **Our Core Values**: 2x2 or 3x1 grid with custom icons. Emphasizing Painless Treatment, Sterilization, Advanced Tech, and Transparency.
9.  **The Clinic Environment (Gallery)**: Masonry or neat grid of high-quality photos showing the waiting area, operatory, and sterilization room.
10. **Focus on Hygiene & Sterilization**: Dedicated section. Dark or primary-colored background to signify seriousness. Detailed text on autoclave, disposable protocols, etc.
11. **Technology Suite Overview**: List or grid detailing specific advanced machinery used (e.g., OPG, 3D Scanners) and patient benefits.
12. **Community Involvement**: Brief section on any free camps, educational seminars, or local Patiala community work.
13. **Patient Experience / What to Expect**: Step-by-step guide on what a first visit feels like.
14. **Awards and Recognition**: Badge display with brief descriptions of any local or national healthcare awards.
15. **Testimonial Feature**: A single, powerful, long-form patient story.
16. **Our Partners (Labs/Materials)**: Logos of premium dental material brands used (e.g., Nobel Biocare, 3M).
17. **Careers Teaser**: Brief callout linking to the careers page for hiring talent.
18. **FAQ (About Specifics)**: Accordion for questions like "Do you treat children?", "Accessibility?".
19. **Global Pre-Footer**: Booking CTA.
20. **Global Footer**: Standard footer.
