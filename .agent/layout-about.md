# Layout Blueprint: About Page

This document outlines the structure for the About page, focusing on the brand story, the founder's expertise, and the dual nature of the salon and academy.

## 1. Hero: The Brand Story
- **Intent:** Set a reflective, premium tone.
- **Rhythm:** Half-height hero (`min-h-[50vh]`), centered text over a subtle atmospheric image.
- **Elements:** Large serif headline ("Crafting Beauty, Elevating Confidence"), introductory paragraph.

## 2. The Visionary: Ajay Sir
- **Intent:** Establish the founder's authority and personal touch.
- **Rhythm:** 2-column split. Image on the left, text on the right. Generous `space-24` padding.
- **Elements:** High-end portrait, biographical text detailing experience and philosophy, signature.

## 3. Our Dual Mission (Salon & Academy)
- **Intent:** Clearly delineate the two core offerings.
- **Rhythm:** 2 large side-by-side cards. Soft background.
- **Elements:** One card for the premium salon experience, one for the rigorous academy. Distinct icons or subtle imagery for each.

## 4. The Studio Experience (Punjabi Bagh)
- **Intent:** Showcase the physical luxury of the location.
- **Rhythm:** Full-width image gallery or alternating text/image blocks.
- **Elements:** High-quality interior photography, descriptions of the ambiance and amenities.

## 5. The "Home Salon" Convenience
- **Intent:** Explain the logistics and luxury of the home service.
- **Rhythm:** 3-column grid of steps.
- **Elements:** Step 1: Book, Step 2: We Arrive, Step 3: Transform. Minimalist icons.

## 6. Meet the Elite Team
- **Intent:** Humanize the brand and show the depth of talent.
- **Rhythm:** Grid layout (3 or 4 columns). `space-16` padding.
- **Elements:** Professional headshots, names, titles (e.g., "Senior Colorist"). Hover states to reveal a short bio quote.

## 7. Our Premium Partners
- **Intent:** Reinforce quality through brand association.
- **Rhythm:** Horizontal logo band.
- **Elements:** Grayscale logos of high-end product lines used.

## 8. Core Values
- **Intent:** Communicate the ethical and professional standards.
- **Rhythm:** 2x2 grid of text blocks.
- **Elements:** Headings like "Hygiene First," "Continuous Education," "Bespoke Artistry."

## 9. Milestones / History
- **Intent:** Show longevity and growth.
- **Rhythm:** Vertical timeline layout.
- **Elements:** Years and short descriptions of key events (e.g., "Studio Opening," "Academy Launch").

## 10. Pre-Footer Booking/Contact CTA
- **Intent:** Drive user to book a service or inquire about the academy.
- **Rhythm:** Centered block, deep primary background.
- **Elements:** Dual CTAs: "Book an Appointment" and "Explore the Academy."
