# About Page Layout Architecture

## 1. Global Header
- Standard sticky navigation.

## 2. Hero: The Origin
- Medium height hero. Split screen: Founder portrait (left) / Typography (right).
- "Crafting Elegance Since 2015" in high-contrast serif.

## 3. Our Philosophy
- Centered, narrow max-width text block (`max-w-prose`).
- Large body typography, high line-height for elegant reading.

## 4. The Studio Experience
- Full-width image breaking the grid.
- Parallax effect showing the luxurious interior of the Patiala salon.

## 5. Meet the Founder
- Deep dive text + image layout. Asymmetric spacing.
- Focus on the journey, training, and passion for bridal beauty.

## 6. The Team (Grid)
- 3-column grid of senior artists.
- Hover reveals a quote from each artist about their style.

## 7. Our Promise / Core Values
- 3 distinct icons/sections.
- Hygiene, Premium Products (MAC, Huda, Charlotte Tilbury), Timeless Techniques.

## 8. Milestone Timeline
- Horizontal scrolling section or elegant vertical list of salon growth and awards.
