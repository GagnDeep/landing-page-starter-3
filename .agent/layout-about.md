# About Page Layout Blueprint

## Visual Rhythm & Layout Intent
Focus on the story, expertise, and philosophy of The Dental Masters. A balance of professional authority and approachability.

## Sections
1. **Hero:** Title "Our Story" / "Meet The Masters", elegant background.
2. **Mission & Vision:** Core philosophy regarding ethical practice and premium care.
3. **Dr. Shruti Singh Kanwar Profile:** Detailed bio, qualifications, specialties.
4. **Dr. Aakriti Profile:** Detailed bio, qualifications, specialties.
5. **The Clinic History/Journey:** Brief timeline or narrative of the clinic's establishment in Chandigarh.
6. **Our Values:** Core tenets (Hygiene, Precision, Empathy, etc.).
7. **Clinic Gallery:** High-quality images of the facility.
8. **Final CTA:** Link to book a consultation.
