# About Page Layout Architecture

## Section 1: Hero (`About Hero`)
- **Intent:** Set a welcoming, personal tone.
- **Rhythm:** Medium-height hero with a warm interior shot of the salon. Centered page title.

## Section 2: Our Story (`The Journey`)
- **Intent:** Explain the origins and the vision of Boho Salon.
- **Rhythm:** Split layout: rich text on the left, an artistic historical or founder photo on the right.

## Section 3: The Vibe (`Philosophy`)
- **Intent:** Describe the boho-modern, relaxing atmosphere.
- **Rhythm:** Full-width image background with a blockquote overlay.

## Section 4: The Team (`Stylists`)
- **Intent:** Introduce the key talent.
- **Rhythm:** Grid of portraits. Hovering reveals their specialty (e.g., "Nail Artist", "Curly Hair Specialist").

## Section 5: The Space (`Gallery`)
- **Intent:** Show off the Sector 17 location.
- **Rhythm:** Collage or masonry grid of high-quality interior images.

## Section 6: Join Us (`Careers Callout`)
- **Intent:** Lead into the careers page.
- **Rhythm:** Simple, centered CTA block with a muted background.
