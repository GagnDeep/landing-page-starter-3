# About Page Layout Blueprint

## Section 1: Hero
- **Intent:** Establish the mission ("Crafting Confident Smiles"). Warm, human-centric imagery.
- **Rhythm:** Centered text over a soft, sweeping background graphic.

## Section 2: Our Philosophy
- **Intent:** Explain the "why" behind the clinic. Focus on painless, modern care.
- **Rhythm:** Narrow text column, large typography, deep philosophical tone.

## Section 3: Meet Dr. Sumeet Gupta (Deep Dive)
- **Intent:** Comprehensive biography, education, and specializations.
- **Rhythm:** Large portrait, long-form text with pull quotes.

## Section 4: The Team Behind the Smiles
- **Intent:** Introduce hygienists, assistants, and support staff.
- **Rhythm:** Grid of circular portraits with name and title.

## Section 5: The Clinic Journey (Timeline)
- **Intent:** History of the clinic, milestones, growth.
- **Rhythm:** Vertical timeline with staggered text blocks.

## Section 6: Our Core Values
- **Intent:** Empathy, Excellence, Transparency, Comfort.
- **Rhythm:** 4 horizontal cards with distinct icons.

## Section 7: The "Modern Clinic" Experience
- **Intent:** What to expect when you walk in (aromatherapy, calming music, comfortable seating).
- **Rhythm:** Full-width image background with a frosted glass content card overlay.

## Section 8: Technology at a Glance
- **Intent:** List the advanced equipment used (3D scanners, digital X-rays).
- **Rhythm:** Icon-heavy list layout.

## Section 9: Commitment to Safety & Hygiene
- **Intent:** Reassure patients about sterilization protocols.
- **Rhythm:** Muted background, bulleted list with checkmarks.

## Section 10: Awards & Recognitions
- **Intent:** Build authority.
- **Rhythm:** Carousel of award badges/logos.

## Section 11: Community Involvement
- **Intent:** Show the clinic gives back to the Chandigarh community.
- **Rhythm:** Photo grid with brief captions.

## Section 12: Patient Demographics & Focus
- **Intent:** Who we serve (families, professionals, specialized aligner cases).
- **Rhythm:** 3-column feature block.

## Section 13: The Consultation Process
- **Intent:** Step-by-step of the first visit.
- **Rhythm:** Stepper component with connecting lines.

## Section 14: Behind the Scenes (Video/Visual)
- **Intent:** A day in the life at the clinic.
- **Rhythm:** Large video player container.

## Section 15: Why Choose Us (Summary)
- **Intent:** Distill the page into 3 main bullet points.
- **Rhythm:** Large, bold typography, centered.

## Section 16: Testimonial Highlight
- **Intent:** A profound, life-changing smile story.
- **Rhythm:** Blockquote styling, enormous font size.

## Section 17: Partnerships & Affiliations
- **Intent:** Brands we work with (e.g., aligner manufacturers).
- **Rhythm:** Simple logo strip.

## Section 18: Join Our Team (Soft pitch)
- **Intent:** Link to careers page for interested professionals.
- **Rhythm:** Small banner, secondary styling.

## Section 19: Ready to Meet Us? (CTA)
- **Intent:** Drive appointments.
- **Rhythm:** High contrast, centered action block.

## Section 20: Standard Footer
- **Intent:** Consistent navigation ending.
- **Rhythm:** Inherits global footer.
