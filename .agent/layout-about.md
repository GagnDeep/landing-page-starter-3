# Layout Blueprint: About Page
**Status:** Defined (Phase 1)
**Vibe:** Trust, Heritage, Personal Connection

## Sections
1. **Hero (Our Story):** Warm image of the clinic/team, strong welcoming headline.
2. **Meet Dr. Bansal:** Detailed biography, credentials, and personal philosophy. Photo + Text.
3. **Meet the Team:** Grid of staff members (hygienists, reception) with brief friendly bios.
4. **Clinic History/Values:** Timeline or icon-based grid explaining the commitment to the neighborhood.
5. **Office Tour Gallery:** High-quality images of the waiting room and treatment areas.
6. **Final CTA:** "Join our patient family" -> Book Appointment.
