# Layout Blueprint: About Us Page

## Structural Slots

1.  **Global Top Bar & Header** (Inherited from Home)
2.  **Page Hero**
    *   *Intent:* Atmospheric clinic shot or team photo. Title: "Our Story" or "Redefining Dental Care".
3.  **Mission & Vision Statement**
    *   *Intent:* Large typography block. Focus on the transition from "feared medical visit" to "premium self-care experience."
4.  **The Clinic's History / Founding Story**
    *   *Intent:* Narrative section. How the clinic started in Patiala/Chandigarh and its growth.
5.  **Meet the Founders (Detailed Profiles)**
    *   *Intent:* Deep dive into Dr. Abhishek Tayal and Dr. Suruchi. Qualifications, specializations, and personal philosophy.
6.  **The Extended Team Roster**
    *   *Intent:* Grid of hygienists, specialists, and support staff. Shows depth of the team.
7.  **Core Values Grid**
    *   *Intent:* Icon + text blocks outlining values (e.g., Transparency, Empathy, Precision, Hygiene).
8.  **Tour the Clinic (Visual Focus)**
    *   *Intent:* High-quality masonry gallery of the interior.
9.  **Community Involvement / Philanthropy**
    *   *Intent:* Build local goodwill. Highlight any free camps or community education.
10. **Final CTA & Footer** (Inherited)
