# Layout: About Page

## Structural Intent
The About page tells the story of "The Dental Masters," focusing on the founders' expertise, the clinic's philosophy, and the commitment to ethical, international-standard care.

## Visual Rhythm
A narrative flow that alternates between personal, editorial photography and structured, authoritative text blocks. The pacing is deliberate and slow.

## Sections

1.  **Hero Section:** Editorial layout. Title: "Our Philosophy." Subtitle emphasizing ethical excellence and clinical precision. Subtle background image of the clinic or founders.
2.  **The Origin Story:** A text-heavy, beautifully typeset section detailing how and why the clinic was founded. Use drop caps or pull quotes.
3.  **Core Values Grid:** A clean, spacious grid detailing the core pillars (e.g., Transparency, Precision, Empathy, Global Standards).
4.  **Founder Profile 1 (Lead Clinician):** A deep dive into the primary doctor's credentials. High-quality portrait, detailed bio, list of international affiliations and degrees.
5.  **Founder Profile 2 (Lead Clinician):** Similar structure to Profile 1 for the second key doctor, perhaps mirroring the layout horizontally for visual interest.
6.  **The Team (Associates & Specialists):** A structured grid of the supporting doctors and specialists. Professional, cohesive headshots.
7.  **The Support Staff:** Acknowledging the hygienists, reception, and coordination team. Emphasizes the complete patient experience.
8.  **Our Approach to Care:** A narrative section explaining the "patient-first" methodology, from consultation to post-treatment.
9.  **Clinical Ethics Statement:** A dedicated, high-contrast block (perhaps a dark background) explicitly stating the clinic's commitment to ethical treatment plans (no unnecessary procedures).
10. **The Facility (Tour):** A visually rich section with a gallery or video tour of the premium Sector 21C location. Emphasize the calm, luxurious environment.
11. **Technology & Innovation:** Detailed breakdown of the equipment used (e.g., CBCT, intraoral scanners) and how it benefits the patient.
12. **In-House Laboratory (If applicable):** Highlighting the quality control and speed of having an in-house or dedicated premium lab partner.
13. **Global Standards & Certifications:** A structured list or grid of ISO certifications, sterilization protocols, and international benchmarks met.
14. **Community Involvement / CSR:** Briefly highlighting any community work, free camps, or educational initiatives.
15. **Media & Press:** A section detailing any features in magazines, awards won, or public recognition.
16. **Patient Philosophy Quote:** A large, impactful quote summarizing the clinic's dedication to its patients.
17. **Gallery of Excellence:** A curated selection of abstract clinical imagery (e.g., beautiful macro shots of dental tools or materials) to reinforce the "masters" theme.
18. **Career / Join the Team Teaser:** A small block linking to the careers page for interested professionals.
19. **Final CTA:** Encouraging the user to experience the difference. Link to book a consultation.
20. **Footer:** Standard global footer.
