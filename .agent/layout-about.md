# Layout: About Page Blueprint

This document defines the structural rhythm for the About Page, focusing on the story, expertise, and environment of Smile Saviors.

1. **Hero Section:** Large, warm team photo or clinic shot. Headline focusing on the journey or mission.
2. **Our Story:** Editorial, multi-column text layout or large serif drop-caps. The history of the clinic.
3. **The Philosophy:** Core values (Painless, Hygienic, Expert). Minimalist icon grid.
4. **Meet the Founders/Lead Doctors:** Deep-dive profiles. Elegant portraiture, detailed credentials, personal quotes.
5. **The Team:** Grid of hygienists, assistants, and staff. Uniform, professional photography.
6. **The Clinic Experience:** Visual tour (slider or grid) of the waiting area, operatories, and sterilization center.
7. **Community Involvement:** Highlighting local Patiala impact or charity work.
8. **Awards & Recognition:** Trust badges, certifications, and media mentions in a clean strip.
9. **Final CTA:** "Experience the difference. Book your visit."
