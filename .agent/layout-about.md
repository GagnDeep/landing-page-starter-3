# About Page Layout Architecture

## 1. Hero Section (`about-hero`)
- **Intent:** Establish a personal connection immediately.
- **Rhythm:** A full-width, warm portrait of Aman Sidhu or the team, with an elegant, understated headline overlay.

## 2. Our Story (`our-story`)
- **Intent:** Detail the origins and passion behind the studio.
- **Rhythm:** A 2-column text and image layout with generous padding, using the high-contrast serif for section headings.

## 3. The Philosophy (`philosophy`)
- **Intent:** Explain the artistic approach (e.g., timeless, candid, editorial).
- **Rhythm:** A centered, typography-heavy block with a subtle background texture or a very slow, subtle parallax effect.

## 4. The Team (`the-team`)
- **Intent:** Introduce key team members (if applicable, e.g., second shooters, videographers).
- **Rhythm:** A clean, spacious grid (2 or 3 columns) of team portraits with short, humanizing biographies.

## 5. Behind the Scenes (`behind-the-scenes`)
- **Intent:** Show the process in action, adding authenticity.
- **Rhythm:** A small, curated gallery of candid shots of the team working at events.

## 6. Values/Ethos (`values`)
- **Intent:** State what the studio stands for (e.g., integrity, art, discretion).
- **Rhythm:** A list or grid of 3-4 key values, perhaps paired with custom iconography or minimalist line art.

## 7. Press & Features (`press`)
- **Intent:** Build authority.
- **Rhythm:** A logo cloud or a list of publications where the studio's work has been featured.

## 8. Final CTA (`about-cta`)
- **Intent:** Guide the user to the next step (e.g., view portfolio or contact).
- **Rhythm:** A clear, high-contrast block prompting an inquiry.
