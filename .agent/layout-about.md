# Dentocare About Page Layout

## 1. Hero Section
- **Layout:** Elegant, large typography over a soft background.
- **Rhythm:** Calm and grounding, setting a narrative tone.

## 2. Our Story
- **Layout:** Text-heavy layout interspersed with historical or thematic images.
- **Rhythm:** Narrative-driven.

## 3. The Clinic Philosophy
- **Layout:** Deep background section highlighting core beliefs (Hygienic Luxury, Patient First).
- **Rhythm:** Authoritative and distinct.

## 4. Meet Dr. Gaurav
- **Layout:** Deep dive into the founder's background with large imagery.
- **Rhythm:** Personal and highly professional.

## 5. Our Team
- **Layout:** Grid of team members with photos and roles.
- **Rhythm:** Uniform, friendly, and approachable.

## 6. Community Involvement
- **Layout:** Highlight block showing local community events or outreach.
- **Rhythm:** Warm and civic-minded.

## 7. CTA
- **Layout:** Clean block urging the user to book a consultation.
- **Rhythm:** Conclusive.
