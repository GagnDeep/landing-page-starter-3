# About Page Layout Blueprint

## Goal
Build trust and establish Dr. Arshpreet Kaur's authority. This page should tell the story behind the clinic, detailing credentials, philosophy, and the commitment to a premium patient experience.

## Sections
1. **Hero Section:** Elegant banner with a warm, welcoming image of Dr. Arshpreet Kaur or the clinic entrance. Headline: "Meet Dr. Arshpreet Kaur".
2. **The Founder's Story:** A narrative section detailing her journey, education, and passion for blending dentistry with aesthetics.
3. **Credentials & Certifications:** A structured list or grid highlighting her degrees, specialized training, and professional affiliations.
4. **Our Philosophy:** A multi-column text section explaining the clinic's approach to holistic facial harmony and patient care.
5. **Meet the Team:** Grid of supporting staff (if applicable), showcasing a cohesive, professional team.
6. **The Clinic Tour:** High-quality imagery of the treatment rooms, waiting area, and sterilization protocols, emphasizing a luxury environment.
7. **Community Involvement / Press:** Mention of any local awards, community work, or media features.
8. **Final CTA:** "Schedule Your Consultation" leading to the contact/booking page.
