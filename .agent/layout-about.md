# Layout Blueprint: About Page

1. **Header**: Global navigation.
2. **Hero Section**: Large, welcoming image of the entire dental team outside or inside the clinic. Title: "Our Story & Our Promise."
3. **The Clinic's Origin Story**: Narrative text explaining the founding of Dr. Bansal's Dental Care, the vision for a community-first clinic in Patiala.
4. **Meet Dr. Bansal (Deep Dive)**: Detailed biography, education, philosophy on patient care, and personal interests (to humanize).
5. **Meet the Team (Grid)**: Profiles of hygienists, dental assistants, and front desk staff. Names, roles, and a short friendly quote from each.
6. **Our Core Values**: A visual list or cards (e.g., Compassion, Integrity, Excellence, Community).
7. **The Facility / Technology**: Detailed section on the state-of-the-art equipment used to ensure patient safety and comfort.
8. **Community Involvement**: Highlighting any local outreach, free clinics, or community sponsorships in Patiala.
9. **Final CTA**: "Join our patient family." Button linking to Contact/Booking.
10. **Footer**: Global footer.
