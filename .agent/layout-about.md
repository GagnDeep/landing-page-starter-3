# About Page Layout Blueprint

1. **Global Header:** Solid background navigation (Pearl White or Studio Charcoal depending on theme).
2. **Hero Section (Our Story):** Parallax image of the clinic interior, large centralized serif headline.
3. **Mission Statement:** Generous spacing, oversized quote formatting the core clinic mission.
4. **The Founder (Dr. Arshpreet Kaur):** 50/50 split. Left: High-fashion portrait. Right: Detailed biography and credentials.
5. **The Clinic Experience (Aesthetics Meets Clinical):** Full-width text block exploring the philosophy of combining bridal beauty with dental precision.
6. **Timeline (Our Journey):** Horizontal scroll or elegant vertical line detailing the clinic's history and milestones.
7. **The Team (Meet the Specialists):** Grid of team members (hygienists, aestheticians) with unified, editorial-style portraits.
8. **Team Member Spotlight (Lead Aesthetician):** Dedicated section for a key team member.
9. **Team Member Spotlight (Lead Dental Surgeon):** Dedicated section for clinical lead.
10. **The Space (Tour the Clinic):** Masonry gallery of the interior—waiting lounge, treatment rooms, lighting details.
11. **Our Values 1 (Precision):** Text and icon pairing, deeply detailed.
12. **Our Values 2 (Comfort):** Focus on the luxury, anxiety-free experience.
13. **Our Values 3 (Artistry):** Focus on the bespoke, aesthetic approach to treatments.
14. **Accreditations & Certifications:** Row of badges/logos indicating medical and aesthetic qualifications.
15. **Patient Philosophy:** A block of text detailing the patient-first approach.
16. **Community Impact/Philanthropy:** A soft, muted section on local community involvement.
17. **Behind the Scenes (Video):** A candid look at the daily preparation and care in the clinic.
18. **Careers Teaser (Join the Team):** Minimalist block inviting professionals to the Careers page.
19. **Consultation CTA:** Soft rose background, invitation to book an introductory meeting.
20. **Global Footer:** Standard footer structure.
21. **Sub-footer:** Copyright info.
