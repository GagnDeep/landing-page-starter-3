# Pricing Page Layout Blueprint

1. **Header**.
2. **Hero**: "Transparent, Accessible Care".
3. **Philosophy**: Why transparent pricing matters.
4. **Standard Packages**: Routine care pricing (Checkups, Cleanings).
5. **Specialized Care Estimates**: Starting prices for RCT, Implants.
6. **Insurance Accepted**: List of providers and how billing works.
7. **Financing Options**: EMI, payment plans.
8. **Membership/Discount Plans**: In-house plans for uninsured patients.
9. **FAQ (Billing)**.
10. **CTA**.
11. **Footer**.
