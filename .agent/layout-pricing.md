# Layout Documentation: Pricing Page

## Structural Intent
Transparent, elegant, and easy to parse. The layout avoids cluttered tables in favor of a clean, list-based approach or subtle card structures that maintain the "affordable luxury" vibe.

## Section Details

### 1. Hero Header
- **Layout:** Simple, centered typography. No large imagery required here.
- **Rhythm:** Quiet, highly legible.
- **Intent:** Direct user to the pricing information immediately.

### 2. Service Categories (Tabbed or Sticky Nav)
- **Layout:** A sticky sidebar for navigation or elegant top tabs (e.g., Hair Care, Coloring, Treatments).
- **Rhythm:** Structured, navigational.
- **Intent:** Make it easy to find specific services.

### 3. Price Lists
- **Layout:** Clean lists with service names on the left and prices on the right, connected by subtle dots or spacing.
- **Rhythm:** Tabular, uniform, readable.
- **Intent:** Clarity and transparency.

### 4. Packages / Bundles (Optional)
- **Layout:** 2 or 3 distinct cards highlighting value bundles (e.g., Bridal, Complete Overhaul).
- **Rhythm:** Slightly more designed than the price lists, drawing the eye.
- **Intent:** Upsell through packaged value.

### 5. Terms / Small Print
- **Layout:** Small, muted typography at the bottom of the lists.
- **Rhythm:** Unobtrusive.
- **Intent:** Legal and policy clarity (e.g., deposit requirements).

### 6. FAQ (Pricing Specific)
- **Layout:** Accordion list.
- **Rhythm:** Condensed.
- **Intent:** Address objections (e.g., "Why does price vary by length?").

### 7. Booking CTA
- **Layout:** Distinct bottom bar or full-width section.
