# Pricing Layout Blueprint

## Section 1: Pricing Hero
- **Intent:** "Transparent, Accessible Care." Focus on no hidden fees.
- **Rhythm:** Clean, minimalist typography. No clutter.

## Section 2: Consultation Fees
- **Intent:** Baseline cost to walk in the door.
- **Rhythm:** Single, prominent card.

## Section 3: Invisible Aligner Packages
- **Intent:** Tiered pricing for the core specialty (Mild, Moderate, Complex cases).
- **Rhythm:** 3-column pricing table (Good, Better, Best style).

## Section 4: What's Included (Aligners)
- **Intent:** Value breakdown (scans, retainers, checkups).
- **Rhythm:** Checklist under the pricing table.

## Section 5: General Dentistry Costs
- **Intent:** Standard procedures (Cleanings, Fillings, Extractions).
- **Rhythm:** Simple, elegant menu-style list.

## Section 6: Aesthetic Procedures Pricing
- **Intent:** Veneers, Whitening. Usually "starting at" prices.
- **Rhythm:** Image-backed cards with price overlays.

## Section 7: Restorative Dentistry
- **Intent:** Implants, Crowns, Root Canals.
- **Rhythm:** Accordion or list, as prices vary widely based on case.

## Section 8: Accepted Insurances
- **Intent:** List major network partners.
- **Rhythm:** Grid of insurance provider logos.

## Section 9: How Insurance Works Here
- **Intent:** Explanation of copays, claims processing.
- **Rhythm:** Step-by-step text blocks.

## Section 10: EMI & Financing Options
- **Intent:** Making high-ticket items (aligners) affordable.
- **Rhythm:** Calculator widget or simple monthly breakdown examples.

## Section 11: Payment Methods Accepted
- **Intent:** Cards, Cash, UPI, Digital Wallets.
- **Rhythm:** Small icon row.

## Section 12: The Value of Investment
- **Intent:** Why quality dentistry is worth it (longevity, health).
- **Rhythm:** Editorial block quote.

## Section 13: Special Offers / Packages
- **Intent:** Wedding smile makeovers, family bundles.
- **Rhythm:** Highlighted, colored cards.

## Section 14: Corporate Tie-ups
- **Intent:** Discounts for employees of specific companies.
- **Rhythm:** Simple text list.

## Section 15: Cancellation Policy
- **Intent:** Rules regarding missed appointments.
- **Rhythm:** Muted, small text block.

## Section 16: Guarantee/Warranty Information
- **Intent:** Terms for implant or veneer replacements.
- **Rhythm:** Badge icon with descriptive text.

## Section 17: Cost Comparison (Us vs. Average)
- **Intent:** Demonstrate competitive value without looking "cheap".
- **Rhythm:** Subtle bar chart or side-by-side table.

## Section 18: Pricing FAQ
- **Intent:** "Does it hurt my wallet?", "Are there hidden fees?"
- **Rhythm:** Accordion.

## Section 19: Get a Custom Quote CTA
- **Intent:** Encourage booking a consultation for exact pricing.
- **Rhythm:** Large, prominent button block.

## Section 20: Standard Footer
- **Intent:** Consistent navigation ending.
- **Rhythm:** Inherits global footer.
