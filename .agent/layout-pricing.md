# Pricing Layout Blueprint

## Goal
Present pricing transparently but elegantly. Avoid "cheap" framing; instead, emphasize "investment in yourself." Note: Many medical aesthetic prices vary by individual, so focus on starting prices and consultation value.

## Sections
1. **Hero Section:** "Transparent Pricing, Bespoke Care."
2. **Consultation Fees:** Clear block explaining the initial consultation cost and what it includes (e.g., 3D scanning, personalized treatment plan).
3. **Dentistry Pricing Table:** An elegant table or list showing starting prices for common procedures (e.g., Whitening, Aligners, Implants).
4. **Aesthetics Pricing Table:** Similar table for aesthetics (e.g., Fillers per syringe, Anti-wrinkle injections per area).
5. **Bridal Packages / Memberships:** Special pricing tiers for bundled services or long-term care plans.
6. **Financing Options:** Information on payment plans, insurance acceptance (if any), or third-party financing partners.
7. **FAQ (Pricing Specific):** "Why are prices 'starting at'?", "Do you take insurance?"
8. **Final CTA:** "Invest in your confidence today. Book a consultation."
