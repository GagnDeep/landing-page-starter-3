# Pricing & Packages Layout Architecture

## 1. Global Header
- Standard sticky navigation.

## 2. Hero: Transparent Luxury
- Simple, centered text hero.
- "Investment in Your Perfect Day."

## 3. Package Structure Intro
- Short text block explaining that all packages are customizable.

## 4. The Packages (Grid/Cards)
- 3 Column layout.
- Left: Engagement/Roka. Center: The Bridal Day (highlighted, slightly larger). Right: Reception/Reception.
- Clear typography, bullet points, price ranges (e.g., "Starting at ₹X,XXX").

## 5. A La Carte Services (Table/List)
- Clean, minimalist table for party makeup, trial sessions, and hair only.
- Dotted leaders connecting service name to price.

## 6. What's Included (Feature Checklist)
- Grid with checkmarks.
- E.g., Premium lashes, touch-up kit, styling assistance.

## 7. Booking Terms & Deposit Info
- Subdued grey section (`var(--muted)`).
- Clear, professional explanation of the 50% booking retainer.

## 8. FAQ Teaser (Pricing Specific)
- "Do you travel to the venue?" "Are trials paid?"

## 9. CTA: Request Custom Quote
- Large, elegant banner to contact for destination weddings or large bridal parties.

## 10. Global Footer
- Standard footer.
