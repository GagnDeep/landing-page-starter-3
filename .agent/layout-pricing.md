# Layout Blueprint: Pricing & Financing Page

## Structural Slots

1.  **Global Top Bar & Header** (Inherited)
2.  **Page Hero**
    *   *Intent:* Title: "Transparent Investment in Your Smile". Focus on value and honesty.
3.  **The Philosophy on Cost**
    *   *Intent:* Text block explaining that quality materials and expert care are an investment, not a commodity.
4.  **Pricing Tiers / Starting Costs**
    *   *Intent:* Clear, non-deceptive "Starting at..." tables for major categories (Consultations, Implants, Aligners).
5.  **What's Included in the Fee**
    *   *Intent:* Breakdown showing that aftercare, specific scans, or warranties are included.
6.  **Insurance & Corporate Tie-ups**
    *   *Intent:* Logos and information about accepted insurance plans or empanelments.
7.  **EMI / Financing Options**
    *   *Intent:* Detailed explanation of 0% EMI plans, partners (e.g., Bajaj Finserv), and how to apply.
8.  **Cost FAQ**
    *   *Intent:* Address hidden fees, tax, and insurance claims.
9.  **Consultation CTA**
    *   *Intent:* "Get a personalized quote during your consultation."
10. **Global Footer** (Inherited)
