# Layout: Pricing & Patient Information Page

## Structural Intent
To provide transparent, premium-tier pricing information and details on payment options, insurance, and international patient coordination. The tone must be reassuring, professional, and clear, avoiding a "cheap" or "discount" feel.

## Visual Rhythm
Clean, data-oriented, and highly readable. Uses tables, lists, and clear typographic hierarchy to present financial and logistical information without confusion.

## Sections

1.  **Hero Section:** Title: "Investment in Your Health." Subtitle emphasizing transparency, value, and premium care.
2.  **Philosophy on Pricing:** A brief, elegant statement explaining that prices reflect international standards, premium materials, and master-level expertise.
3.  **Consultation & Diagnostics:** Clear pricing (or starting prices) for initial consultations, CBCT scans, and comprehensive treatment planning.
4.  **Implantology Pricing Guidelines:** A structured table or list giving price ranges for single implants, All-on-4, etc. Emphasize what is included (e.g., lifetime warranty on the fixture).
5.  **Aesthetic Treatments Pricing:** Ranges for veneers, smile makeovers, and whitening.
6.  **General & Restorative Pricing:** Ranges for root canals, crowns, and fillings.
7.  **Orthodontic Pricing:** Guidelines for aligners and traditional braces.
8.  **Why Our Fees Reflect Our Quality:** A section detailing the costs of premium sterilization, advanced technology, and top-tier materials.
9.  **Payment Options & Financing:** Information on accepted payment methods (Credit Cards, Bank Transfers, EMI options if applicable).
10. **Insurance Information:** Guidance on how the clinic assists with insurance claims or reimbursement documentation (even if not direct billing).
11. **International Patients (NRI) Logistics:** A dedicated section detailing how payments and treatment phases work for patients traveling from abroad.
12. **Concierge Services (For International/Out-of-town):** Details on airport transfers, hotel tie-ups, and dedicated coordination.
13. **Treatment Timeline & Phasing:** Explaining how complex treatments can be phased over time to manage costs.
14. **Warranty & Guarantee Policy:** A clear, authoritative statement on the warranties provided for specific treatments (e.g., implants, crowns).
15. **Corporate Tie-ups:** A list of any corporate partners or embassies the clinic is empaneled with.
16. **FAQ (Financial):** 3-5 common questions related specifically to pricing, hidden costs, and payment plans.
17. **Transparency Pledge:** A final note reiterating that a detailed, written estimate is always provided before any treatment begins.
18. **Downloadable Fee Guide (Optional):** A form to download a more detailed PDF fee schedule.
19. **Final CTA:** "Book a Consultation for a Personalized Estimate."
20. **Footer:** Standard global footer.
