# Layout Blueprint: Pricing Page

1. **Header**: Global navigation.
2. **Hero Section**: Title: "Transparent Pricing. No Surprises." Subtitle: "High-quality care that fits your budget."
3. **Insurance Information**: A clear list or logos of accepted insurance providers. Details on how claims are handled.
4. **Flexible Financing Options**: Information on third-party financing (e.g., Bajaj Finserv, CareCredit equivalent in India) or in-house payment plans.
5. **New Patient Special**: Highlight the current offer (e.g., discounted exam/x-rays) to lower the barrier to entry.
6. **In-House Membership Plan (If applicable)**: Details of an annual plan for uninsured patients (preventive care included, discounts on services).
7. **Common Procedure Estimates**: A table providing a *range* for common procedures (e.g., Routine Cleaning, Basic Filling) to show transparency.
8. **Billing FAQ**: Accordion addressing common billing questions.
9. **Final CTA**: "Discuss your treatment plan and options with us."
10. **Footer**: Global footer.
