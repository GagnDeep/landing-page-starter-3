# Layout Blueprint: Pricing Page

This document outlines the structure for the Pricing page, ensuring complex information is presented cleanly and elegantly.

## 1. Hero: Transparent Pricing
- **Intent:** Establish trust regarding costs.
- **Rhythm:** Minimalist header, centered text.
- **Elements:** "An Investment in Yourself," short explanatory text about tiered pricing based on stylist experience.

## 2. Pricing Filter/Toggle
- **Intent:** Allow users to switch between Salon vs. Home Service pricing, or Junior vs. Senior Stylist.
- **Rhythm:** Centered pill-shaped toggle or dropdown.
- **Elements:** Interactive toggle button.

## 3. Hair Services Menu
- **Intent:** Detailed breakdown of hair costs.
- **Rhythm:** Clean, tabular layout or list with dotted leaders connecting service to price.
- **Elements:** Categories (Cuts, Color, Treatments). Clear starting prices (e.g., "From ₹1500").

## 4. Makeup & Bridal Packages
- **Intent:** Group complex offerings.
- **Rhythm:** Card-based layout for packages, listing inclusions.
- **Elements:** "Bronze," "Silver," "Gold" bridal packages. Bullet points of what is included.

## 5. Add-ons & Extras
- **Intent:** Detail supplementary costs cleanly.
- **Rhythm:** 2-column list, smaller typography (`text-sm`).
- **Elements:** Travel fees for home service, extra length charges, etc.

## 6. Memberships / Subscriptions
- **Intent:** Highlight recurring value.
- **Rhythm:** 3-column highlighted pricing tiers.
- **Elements:** Monthly benefits, discounted rates.

## 7. Academy Course Fees (If applicable)
- **Intent:** Separate section for educational pricing.
- **Rhythm:** Dark mode block to differentiate from salon services.
- **Elements:** Course names, duration, total investment.

## 8. Terms & Policies Brief
- **Intent:** Clarify cancellation and deposit rules.
- **Rhythm:** Accordion or small text block at the bottom of the menus.
- **Elements:** "24-hour cancellation policy," "Non-refundable deposits."

## 9. FAQ (Pricing Specific)
- **Intent:** Address objections related to cost.
- **Rhythm:** Simple 2-column accordion.
- **Elements:** "Why does price vary by stylist?" "Are travel fees included?"

## 10. CTA: Book Your Appointment
- **Intent:** Immediate action after viewing prices.
- **Rhythm:** Sticky bottom bar on mobile, large centered block on desktop.
- **Elements:** "Secure Your Slot" button linking to the booking engine.
