# Layout Blueprint: Pricing & Insurance Page
**Status:** Defined (Phase 1)
**Vibe:** Transparent, Helpful, Stress-free

## Sections
1. **Hero:** Clear messaging on affordable, transparent care.
2. **Accepted Insurances:** Logos or list of network providers.
3. **Payment Options:** Information on credit cards, payment plans (e.g., CareCredit).
4. **Membership Plan (if applicable):** Pricing tiers for uninsured patients (Cleanings, Exams, Discounts).
5. **Standard Fee Estimates:** Optional: A brief, transparent table of starting prices for common procedures.
6. **Financial FAQ:** Accordion for common billing questions.
7. **Contact Billing:** Specific CTA to call with insurance questions.
