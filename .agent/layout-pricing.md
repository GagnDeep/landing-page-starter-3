# Pricing Page Layout Intent

**Page Intent:** Provide transparent, general cost expectations and financing options without overcommitting on exact medical costs.

## Sections
1. **`PricingHero`**: Honest, straightforward messaging about the value of care.
2. **`TransparentCostTable`**: Starting prices for common procedures (e.g., Consultations, Cleanings, Whitening).
3. **`InsuranceAcceptedGrid`**: Logos of partnered insurance providers.
4. **`FinancingOptions`**: Details on EMI, payment plans, or third-party financing.
5. **`ValueProposition`**: Why quality dentistry is an investment.
6. **`PricingFAQ`**: Questions about insurance handling and estimates.
7. **`PricingCTA`**: Get a custom treatment plan.
