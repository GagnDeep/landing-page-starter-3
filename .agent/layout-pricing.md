# Layout: Pricing Page
**Goal:** Provide transparency regarding costs, insurance, and financing to reduce barriers to entry.
**Rhythm:** Clear, tabular data mixed with reassuring explanations of value.

1. **Global Header (Sticky)**: Standard navigation.
2. **Hero Section**: Title "Transparent Pricing & Easy Financing." Image of a relaxed patient talking to staff.
3. **Philosophy on Pricing**: Brief statement on providing high-quality care at fair, transparent prices.
4. **General Consultation Fees**: Clear box detailing initial visit, x-ray, and basic cleaning costs.
5. **Popular Treatments Price Guide**: A clean table or list of starting prices for common procedures (e.g., Whitening, Crowns, Fillings). *Note: Must state "Starting at" or "Estimated".*
6. **Why Prices Vary**: Explanation of factors that affect cost (materials, complexity).
7. **Accepted Insurances**: Grid of logos for accepted major health/dental insurance providers.
8. **Insurance Assistance**: Explanation of how the clinic staff helps with claims and paperwork.
9. **No Insurance? The In-House Plan**: Details of an in-house membership or discount plan for uninsured patients.
10. **In-House Plan Tiers**: Cards showing basic vs. premium membership benefits.
11. **Financing Options**: Logos and details for third-party financing (e.g., CareCredit, Bajaj Finserv).
12. **0% EMI Options**: Highlight if interest-free payment plans are available.
13. **Corporate Tie-ups**: List of companies whose employees get special rates.
14. **Payment Methods Accepted**: Icons for Credit Cards, UPI, Cash, Bank Transfer.
15. **Special Offers / Packages**: Current promotions (e.g., "Wedding Smile Package").
16. **Cost vs. Value**: Section explaining the long-term savings of quality dental work.
17. **FAQ on Billing**: Accordion addressing hidden fees, refund policies, etc.
18. **CTA: Get a Quote / Book Consultation**: Encourage users to come in for an exact estimate.
19. **Global Footer (Top)**: Standard footer links.
20. **Global Footer (Bottom)**: Legal links.
