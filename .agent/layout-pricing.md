# Pricing (Financial / Insurance) Page Layout Blueprint

1. **Global Notice Bar**: Top banner.
2. **Primary Navigation**: Sticky header.
3. **Hero Section**: "Transparent Pricing & Flexible Financing" - focus on clarity and easing financial anxiety.
4. **Our Philosophy on Cost**: Reassuring message that quality care should be accessible.
5. **Accepted Insurances**: Grid or list of major insurance providers accepted.
6. **Insurance Navigation Assistance**: How the front desk team helps patients understand and maximize their benefits.
7. **Flexible Financing Options**: Introduction to third-party financing (e.g., CareCredit equivalent) or in-house payment plans.
8. **Consultation Fees**: Clear outline of what initial visits and exams cost (or if they are complimentary for certain services).
9. **Orthodontic / Aligner Pricing Tiers**: Rough estimates or starting prices for aligners to set expectations.
10. **Pediatric Care Costs**: General info on costs for routine kids' checkups.
11. **Cosmetic Dentistry Investment**: Acknowledging cosmetic work as an investment, with starting price ranges.
12. **Membership Plan / Savings Plan**: Detail any in-house discount plan for uninsured patients (benefits, cost).
13. **What's Included in a Visit**: Breaking down the value (e.g., exam includes digital x-rays, oral cancer screening).
14. **No Hidden Fees Guarantee**: A commitment to transparent treatment plans before work begins.
15. **Payment Methods Accepted**: Cards, UPI, cash, etc.
16. **FAQ on Billing**: Answers to common billing questions.
17. **Financial Coordinator Profile**: Intro to the team member who helps with billing, putting a friendly face to the process.
18. **Downloadable Forms**: Links to financial agreement forms to read in advance.
19. **Contact Us with Questions (CTA)**: Encouraging reaching out for specific estimates.
20. **Global Footer**: Standard footer.
