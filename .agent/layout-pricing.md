# Pricing / Insurance Page Layout Architecture

## 1. Page Header
- **Visuals:** Clean, simple header.
- **Content:** "Transparent Pricing & Insurance Options".

## 2. Philosophy on Cost
- **Visuals:** Text block with soft background.
- **Content:** Explanation of value, premium care, and no hidden fees.

## 3. General Fee Guide / Estimates
- **Visuals:** Table or clean list layout.
- **Content:** Starting prices or ranges for common procedures (Consultations, X-Rays, standard cleanings). Disclaimer that complex procedures require consultation.

## 4. Accepted Insurances
- **Visuals:** Grid of insurance logos.
- **Content:** List of accepted networks.

## 5. Financing Options
- **Visuals:** Cards detailing different plans.
- **Content:** EMI options, healthcare credit partners.

## 6. FAQ (Financial)
- **Visuals:** Accordion.
- **Content:** Common billing questions.
