# Spacedent Pricing / Insurance Page Layout

Transparency builds trust. This page clarifies costs and insurance handling.

## Sections
1. **Hero Header:** "Transparent Pricing & Insurance"
2. **Philosophy on Cost:** Commitment to no hidden fees.
3. **Accepted Insurances:** Grid of accepted provider logos.
4. **Financing Options:** Information on payment plans or third-party financing.
5. **General Pricing Estimates:** Ranges for common procedures (if legally/ethically permissible, or a statement on how estimates are provided).
6. **First Visit Cost:** Special offers or standard costs for a new patient exam.
7. **FAQ (Financial):** Common questions about billing and payments.
8. **Contact Billing Team CTA:** For specific coverage questions.
