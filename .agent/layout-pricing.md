# Pricing & Insurance Layout Blueprint

**Goal:** Provide transparency while emphasizing value and affordability.

1. **Hero Section:** "Transparent Care, Clear Costs".
2. **Philosophy on Pricing:** Brief statement on balancing high-quality materials with accessible care.
3. **Common Procedures Estimator:** A table or accordion showing *starting from* prices for standard treatments (Consultation, Cleaning, Basic Filling).
4. **Insurance Partners:** Grid of accepted health insurance providers/TPAs.
5. **Financing Options:** Details on EMI plans, third-party financing (e.g., Bajaj Finserv).
6. **Membership/Loyalty Plan:** (If applicable) Annual preventative care plan details.
7. **FAQ specific to Billing:** "Do you accept card?", "How does insurance claim work?".
8. **Final CTA:** "Request a Detailed Quote" -> Contact Form.
