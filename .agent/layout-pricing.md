# Dentistree - Pricing & Insurance Page Layout Blueprint

**Goal:** Demystify dental costs, clearly explain insurance processes, and highlight flexible financing options, removing financial anxiety from the patient experience.

## Sections (20+)

1. **Hero Section:** "Transparent Pricing & Easy Financing". Reassuring text about making care accessible.
2. **Our Pricing Philosophy:** Brief statement on honesty, no hidden fees, and upfront treatment plans.
3. **Insurance Accepted (Intro):** "We work with most major providers."
4. **Insurance Providers Grid:** Logos or list of accepted insurance networks (e.g., Delta Dental, Cigna, MetLife).
5. **How Insurance Works Here:** Step-by-step process of how the clinic handles claims and estimates.
6. **No Insurance? No Problem (Header):** Introduction to alternative payment methods.
7. **In-House Membership Plan (Overview):** Details of a subscription-style plan for uninsured patients.
8. **Membership Tier 1 (Child):** Card detailing cost and included preventative services for kids.
9. **Membership Tier 2 (Adult):** Card detailing cost and included services for adults.
10. **Membership Tier 3 (Perio):** Card for patients needing specialized periodontal maintenance.
11. **Financing Options (Intro):** "Flexible ways to pay over time."
12. **CareCredit / Third-Party Financing:** Details on applying for 0% interest financing.
13. **Orthodontic Payment Plans:** Specific section on spreading out the cost of braces/aligners.
14. **Estimated Costs Guide (Disclaimer):** Note that exact costs require a consultation.
15. **Cost Range: Exams & Cleanings:** Approximate out-of-pocket range.
16. **Cost Range: Restorative Work:** Approximate range for fillings/crowns.
17. **Cost Range: Orthodontics:** Approximate range for braces/aligners.
18. **FSA / HSA Information:** Reminder that patients can use their flexible spending accounts.
19. **Financial FAQ:** 3-4 common questions about billing and payments.
20. **Global Footer:** Standard footer.
