# Layout Documentation: Pricing Page

- **Intent**: Transparent, easy-to-read list of costs.
- **Rhythm**:
  - Hero: Simple, focused header.
  - Toggle: Option to switch between individual services and packages.
  - Layout: Elegant tabular or list format. Dotted leader lines connecting service to price (classic salon menu style).
  - Note section: Small text explaining travel fees or minimum booking amounts.
