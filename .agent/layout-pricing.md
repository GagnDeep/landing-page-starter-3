# Layout Blueprint: Financial & Insurance (Pricing)

**Purpose**: To provide transparency regarding treatment costs, accepted insurance, and payment plans. The tone must be reassuring, clear, and devoid of hidden fees.

## Sections

1.  **Global Navigation Bar**: Standard sticky header.
2.  **Page Header (Hero)**: H1 ("Transparent Financial Options"). Soft background.
3.  **Financial Philosophy**: Short text block emphasizing that quality care should be accessible and pricing is transparent.
4.  **Consultation Fees**: Clear, standalone card detailing the initial consultation cost, X-ray costs, and what is included.
5.  **General Treatment Price Ranges**: A clean, readable table or list format showing *starting* prices for common procedures (Cleaning, Fillings, Simple Extractions).
6.  **Specialized Treatment Ranges**: Separate table for complex procedures (Implants, RCT, Braces). Explicitly state that final costs require clinical assessment.
7.  **Why Prices Vary**: Educational snippet explaining materials used, case complexity, and the value of premium care.
8.  **Accepted Insurance Partners**: Grid of insurance company logos (e.g., Star Health, Bajaj Allianz, CGHS if applicable).
9.  **How to Use Your Insurance**: Step-by-step guide (Bring card -> Verification -> Cashless/Reimbursement).
10. **Corporate Tie-ups**: List of local Patiala companies or institutions with corporate discount programs.
11. **Financing & EMI Options**: Details on third-party financing (e.g., Bajaj Finserv) with 0% EMI options.
12. **Payment Methods Accepted**: Icons for Credit Cards, UPI, Cash, Bank Transfer.
13. **Dental Membership/Discount Plan (If applicable)**: A "card" layout detailing an in-house preventative care subscription.
14. **Tax Benefits (Section 80D)**: Brief educational note on claiming tax deductions for preventative health checkups.
15. **Warranty/Guarantee Information**: Details on the warranty provided for implants and crowns (builds immense trust).
16. **Cancellations & Missed Appointments Policy**: Clear, polite policy statement.
17. **Financial FAQ**: Accordion for "Do you offer discounts?", "Is cosmetic dentistry covered?", etc.
18. **Contact Billing Team**: Direct phone/email for financial queries.
19. **Global Pre-Footer**: CTA to book a consultation to get an exact estimate.
20. **Global Footer**: Standard footer.
