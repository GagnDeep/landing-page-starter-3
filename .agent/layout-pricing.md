# Pricing / Insurance Page Layout Blueprint

1. **Hero Header:** Page title "Investment in Your Smile" or "Pricing & Insurance".
2. **Philosophy on Pricing:** A brief explanation that premium care is an investment, emphasizing transparency and value.
3. **Insurance Accepted:** A clear list or grid of accepted insurance providers.
4. **Financing Options:** Details on payment plans, partnerships (e.g., CareCredit equivalent), and flexible payment methods.
5. **Consultation Fees:** Transparent information regarding initial consultation costs.
6. **General Price Guide (Optional):** A high-level overview or starting prices for common procedures, if appropriate for the clinic's strategy.
7. **FAQ for Billing:** Common questions about payments, insurance claims, and estimates.
8. **Final CTA:** "Contact our billing coordinator for questions."
