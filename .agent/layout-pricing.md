# Pricing / Investment Page Layout Blueprint

## Visual Rhythm & Layout Intent
Transparent, trustworthy, and clear. Avoid overwhelming the user with raw numbers; instead, frame it as an "investment in your health."

## Sections
1. **Hero:** "Transparent Pricing" / "Investment in Your Smile".
2. **Philosophy on Pricing:** Brief text on value, quality of materials, and ethical practice.
3. **Consultation & Diagnostics:** Base fees for initial visits, X-rays, scans.
4. **Core Services Breakdown:** High-level starting prices or ranges for Implants, Aligners, General treatments.
5. **Payment Plans & Insurance:** Information on accepted payment methods, EMI options, and insurance processing.
6. **Why Quality Matters:** Educational section on why premium materials (e.g., specific implant brands) cost what they do.
7. **FAQ (Financial):** Common questions about costs and payments.
8. **Final CTA:** Book a consultation to get a precise quote.
