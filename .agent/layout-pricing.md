# Layout: Pricing & Insurance Page Blueprint

Focuses on transparency, value, and financial accessibility without diminishing the premium brand.

1. **Hero Section:** "Transparent Pricing, Premium Care."
2. **Pricing Philosophy:** Short editorial text on why quality dentistry is an investment.
3. **General Price Guide:** A clean, easy-to-read table or list of starting prices for common procedures. (Avoid cluttered spreadsheets; use spacious rows).
4. **Insurance Partners:** Grid of accepted insurance providers (logos).
5. **Financing Options:** Information on payment plans, EMIs, or third-party financing.
6. **Membership/Loyalty Plan (Optional):** Details of any in-house dental plans.
7. **FAQ (Financial):** Common questions about billing, insurance claims, and hidden costs.
8. **Contact Billing CTA:** "Have questions about your coverage? Speak with our financial coordinator."
