# Pricing Page Layout Architecture

## Section 1: Header (`Pricing Title`)
- **Intent:** Clear, transparent pricing introduction.
- **Rhythm:** Centered title with a brief explanation of the pricing philosophy.

## Section 2: Category Tabs (`Pricing Navigation`)
- **Intent:** Allow easy filtering of services.
- **Rhythm:** Horizontal sticky tabs (Nails, Hair, Spa, Bridal).

## Section 3: Price Menu (`The Menu`)
- **Intent:** Display prices cleanly.
- **Rhythm:** Classic menu layout: Service name left, dotted leader, price right. Grouped by category.

## Section 4: Memberships (`Packages`)
- **Intent:** Sell recurring packages.
- **Rhythm:** 2 or 3 distinct pricing cards highlighting value.

## Section 5: FAQ (`Pricing FAQ`)
- **Intent:** Address common questions regarding costs and deposits.
- **Rhythm:** Accordion style list.
