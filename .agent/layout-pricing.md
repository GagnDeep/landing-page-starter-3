# Pricing Page Layout Architecture

## 1. Hero Section (`pricing-hero`)
- **Intent:** Set a tone of value and investment rather than just cost.
- **Rhythm:** A beautiful, calming image with a headline focused on "Investment" or "Legacy".

## 2. Investment Philosophy (`investment-philosophy`)
- **Intent:** Explain *why* the pricing is structured as it is.
- **Rhythm:** A short, centered paragraph emphasizing quality and timelessness.

## 3. The Signature Package (`package-signature`)
- **Intent:** Highlight the most popular or comprehensive offering.
- **Rhythm:** A prominent, styled card or a full-width section detailing what's included in the flagship package.

## 4. A La Carte Options (`a-la-carte`)
- **Intent:** Offer flexibility (e.g., extra hours, engagement shoots).
- **Rhythm:** A clean, easy-to-read list or grid of additional services.

## 5. Fine Art Albums (`albums-pricing`)
- **Intent:** Detail the costs and options for physical albums.
- **Rhythm:** Images of the albums alongside clear pricing tiers or starting prices.

## 6. Destination Wedding Pricing (`destination-pricing`)
- **Intent:** Clarify how travel is handled.
- **Rhythm:** A small, distinct section explaining custom quotes for travel.

## 7. FAQ (Pricing Specific) (`pricing-faq`)
- **Intent:** Address immediate financial or logistical questions (e.g., payment plans, deposit).
- **Rhythm:** An accordion layout.

## 8. Final CTA (`pricing-cta`)
- **Intent:** The ultimate push to contact.
- **Rhythm:** Standard high-contrast CTA block, perhaps reading "Request a Custom Quote".
