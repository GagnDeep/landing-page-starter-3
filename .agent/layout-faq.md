# Layout: FAQ Page

## Structural Intent
To address patient concerns preemptively, clearly, and authoritatively. This page builds trust by demonstrating transparency and expertise.

## Visual Rhythm
Highly structured, text-focused, and utilitarian but refined. Uses accordions or categorized lists to make finding answers easy without overwhelming the user with text.

## Sections

1.  **Hero Section:** Title: "Frequently Asked Questions." Subtitle: "Clear answers to help you make informed decisions." Clean, minimal.
2.  **Search Bar:** A prominent, fast search input to quickly find specific questions.
3.  **Category Navigation (Sticky):** A sidebar or sticky top menu to jump between categories (e.g., General, Implants, Financial, International).
4.  **Category 1: General Clinic Information:** Accordion list (Hours, Location, Parking, Appointment protocols).
5.  **Category 2: First Visit & Consultation:** Accordion list (What to expect, What to bring, CBCT scan details).
6.  **Category 3: Dental Implants (Deep Dive):** Extensive accordion list (Pain, Timeline, Lifespan, Materials, Failure rates).
7.  **Category 4: Aesthetic Treatments:** Accordion list (Veneers vs. Crowns, Whitening sensitivity, Durability).
8.  **Category 5: Orthodontics & Aligners:** Accordion list (Age limits, Timeline, Discomfort, Retainers).
9.  **Category 6: Financial & Insurance:** Accordion list (Payment methods, EMI, Insurance claims, Hidden costs).
10. **Category 7: International Patients (NRI):** Accordion list (Treatment staging, Travel coordination, Warranty for travelers).
11. **Category 8: Hygiene & Sterilization:** Accordion list detailing the strict protocols, addressing post-COVID concerns or general safety.
12. **Category 9: Aftercare & Maintenance:** Accordion list (Post-surgery instructions, Regular checkups, Guarantee terms).
13. **Ask a Question Form:** A clean, simple form for users whose questions were not answered in the FAQ.
14. **Video FAQ Teaser:** A section linking to video answers from the master clinicians (adds a personal touch).
15. **Downloadable Care Guides:** Links to PDF documents for specific post-operative care instructions.
16. **Emergency Contact Info:** A highlighted block detailing what to do in a dental emergency.
17. **Glossary Link:** A link to a page defining clinical terms used in the answers.
18. **Patient Support Contact:** Direct contact details for the patient coordination team.
19. **Final CTA:** "Ready to begin your journey? Book a consultation."
20. **Footer:** Standard global footer.
