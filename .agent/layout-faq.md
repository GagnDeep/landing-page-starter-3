# Layout Documentation: FAQ Page

## Structural Intent
Utilitarian but refined. The focus is purely on typography and ease of use, ensuring answers are accessible without breaking the luxury aesthetic.

## Section Details

### 1. Header
- **Layout:** Centered, large typography.
- **Rhythm:** Immediate focus on the title.

### 2. Category Navigation (Sticky)
- **Layout:** Sidebar or sticky top menu mapping to question categories (e.g., Booking, Treatments, Academy).
- **Rhythm:** Functional, anchoring the long-scrolling content.

### 3. Accordion Lists
- **Layout:** Clean, borderless or single-line bordered accordions. Large, legible text.
- **Rhythm:** Dense when closed, spacious when open.
- **Intent:** Answer questions efficiently.

### 4. Still Have Questions? (Contact CTA)
- **Layout:** A subtle box or section at the end directing users to the contact page.
- **Rhythm:** Friendly, concluding.
