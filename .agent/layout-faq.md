# FAQ Page Layout Blueprint

1. **Global Notice Bar**: Top banner.
2. **Primary Navigation**: Sticky header.
3. **Hero Section**: "Frequently Asked Questions" - finding answers easily.
4. **Search FAQs**: Prominent search bar for quick finding.
5. **Category Jump Links**: Links to jump to sections (General, Pediatric, Orthodontics, Billing).
6. **General Clinic Questions (Section Header)**: e.g., Hours, Location, What to bring.
7. **General Accordion Items**: The questions and answers.
8. **Pediatric Care FAQs (Section Header)**: First visit, thumb sucking, sealants.
9. **Pediatric Accordion Items**: The questions and answers.
10. **Orthodontics & Aligners FAQs (Section Header)**: Duration, pain, cleaning.
11. **Orthodontics Accordion Items**: The questions and answers.
12. **Billing & Insurance FAQs (Section Header)**: Accepted plans, payment methods.
13. **Billing Accordion Items**: The questions and answers.
14. **Dental Anxiety / Comfort FAQs (Section Header)**: Sedation, pain management.
15. **Anxiety Accordion Items**: The questions and answers.
16. **Post-Op Instructions (Link/Section)**: Quick links to care instructions after procedures.
17. **"Still have questions?" Box**: Friendly prompt with a direct phone number.
18. **Chat Widget Integration**: Indication of where the live chat (if applicable) is located.
19. **Contact Us (CTA)**: Button linking to the full contact page.
20. **Global Footer**: Standard footer.
