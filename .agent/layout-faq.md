# Layout Blueprint: FAQ Page

1. **Header**: Global navigation.
2. **Hero Section**: Title: "Frequently Asked Questions." Subtitle: "Everything you need to know about your visit."
3. **Search Bar**: A prominent search input to filter questions quickly.
4. **Category Navigation**: Links to jump to "First Visit", "Treatments", "Billing", "Emergencies".
5. **General / First Visit FAQs (Accordion)**: Questions about what to bring, how long it takes, etc.
6. **Treatments & Pain Management FAQs (Accordion)**: Addressing anxiety, anesthesia, specific procedure recoveries.
7. **Insurance & Payment FAQs (Accordion)**: Details on coverage, payment methods.
8. **Still Have Questions? (CTA)**: "Can't find the answer? Contact our friendly team." Link to contact page or direct phone number.
9. **Footer**: Global footer.
