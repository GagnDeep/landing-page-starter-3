# Layout Blueprint: FAQ

**Purpose**: To answer common patient questions quickly, reduce admin phone calls, and alleviate dental anxiety.

## Sections

1.  **Global Navigation Bar**: Standard sticky header.
2.  **Page Header (Hero)**: H1 ("Frequently Asked Questions"). Clean, minimal design.
3.  **Search Bar**: Prominent search input to instantly filter questions.
4.  **Quick Jump Links**: Anchor links to specific categories (General, Implants, Pain, Financial).
5.  **Category 1: General & Appointments**: Accordion list (Working hours, how to book, parking availability).
6.  **Category 2: Dental Implants**: Accordion list (Pain, duration, success rate, cost).
7.  **Category 3: Pain Management & Anxiety**: Accordion list (Is it going to hurt? Sedation options).
8.  **Category 4: Cosmetic Procedures**: Accordion list (Whitening longevity, Veneer process).
9.  **Category 5: Orthodontics**: Accordion list (Age limits, Invisalign vs. traditional).
10. **Category 6: Financial & Insurance**: Accordion list (EMI, Accepted providers).
11. **Category 7: Pediatric Dentistry**: Accordion list (First visit age, milk teeth importance).
12. **Category 8: Emergencies**: Accordion list (What to do if a tooth falls out, severe pain).
13. **Category 9: Post-Treatment Care**: Accordion list (Eating after extraction, brushing with braces).
14. **"Still have questions?" Banner**: Highlighted box.
15. **Direct WhatsApp Link**: Large button to chat directly with reception.
16. **Video FAQ Teaser**: Thumbnails linking to video answers from Dr. Anshum.
17. **Downloadable Care Guides**: Links to PDF instructions.
18. **Contact Form**: Simple form for unaddressed queries.
19. **Global Pre-Footer**: Booking CTA.
20. **Global Footer**: Standard footer.
