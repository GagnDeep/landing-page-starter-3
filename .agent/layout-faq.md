# Layout: FAQ Page
**Goal:** Answer common patient questions to reduce anxiety and administrative overhead.
**Rhythm:** Highly structured, scannable accordion lists categorized logically.

1. **Global Header (Sticky)**: Standard navigation.
2. **Hero Section**: Title "Frequently Asked Questions." Subtitle "Find answers to help you prepare for your visit."
3. **Search Bar**: Large, prominent search input specifically for finding FAQs.
4. **Category Navigation**: Quick links to jump to specific FAQ sections (e.g., General, Booking, Procedures).
5. **Section 1: General Information**: Accordions for hours, location, parking, walk-ins.
6. **Section 2: Booking & Appointments**: Accordions for how to book, cancellation policy, running late.
7. **Section 3: Emergency Services**: Accordions defining what counts as an emergency, what to do while waiting.
8. **Section 4: Payments & Insurance**: Accordions detailing accepted payments, insurance claims, EMI options.
9. **Section 5: Treatments & Procedures**: Accordions for pain management, recovery times, specific treatments (e.g., implants).
10. **Section 6: Pediatric Dentistry**: Accordions specific to children's care (first visit, braces).
11. **Section 7: Safety & Hygiene**: Accordions explaining sterilization protocols and COVID/infection safety.
12. **Ask a Question Form**: "Didn't find what you were looking for?" Contact form for unaddressed queries.
13. **Live Chat Teaser**: "Chat with our receptionist now" (if applicable) or WhatsApp link.
14. **Helpful Resources**: Links to blog posts or downloadable pre/post-op instructions.
15. **Patient Testimonial**: A reassuring quote about how well the clinic explains procedures.
16. **Emergency Banner**: Reminder of the 24/7 emergency number.
17. **Map & Directions**: Reiteration of how to find the clinic.
18. **CTA: Book an Appointment**: Standard booking push.
19. **Global Footer (Top)**: Standard footer links.
20. **Global Footer (Bottom)**: Legal links.
