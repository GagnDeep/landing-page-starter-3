# FAQ Layout Blueprint

## Section 1: FAQ Hero
- **Intent:** "We have answers." Searchable knowledge base.
- **Rhythm:** Centered title with a prominent, large search bar.

## Section 2: Quick Links/Categories
- **Intent:** Jump to Specific sections (Aligners, Pain, Costs, Appointments).
- **Rhythm:** Row of icon-based buttons.

## Section 3: Top 5 Most Asked Questions
- **Intent:** Immediately address the most common concerns.
- **Rhythm:** Prominent accordion list, open by default or visually distinct.

## Section 4: Category 1: Invisible Aligners
- **Intent:** Deep dive into aligner specific questions (duration, eating, cleaning).
- **Rhythm:** Section header followed by accordion.

## Section 5: Category 2: Pain & Anxiety (Painless Dentistry)
- **Intent:** Reassure nervous patients.
- **Rhythm:** Soft background, empathetic language in answers.

## Section 6: Category 3: General Dentistry
- **Intent:** Questions about cleanings, fillings, frequency of visits.
- **Rhythm:** Standard accordion.

## Section 7: Category 4: Cosmetic Procedures
- **Intent:** Veneers, Whitening longevity and process.
- **Rhythm:** Standard accordion.

## Section 8: Category 5: Costs & Insurance
- **Intent:** Clarify financial questions.
- **Rhythm:** Accordion, linking to the pricing page where necessary.

## Section 9: Category 6: Appointments & Logistics
- **Intent:** How to book, cancel, find the clinic.
- **Rhythm:** Standard accordion.

## Section 10: Category 7: Emergencies
- **Intent:** What constitutes an emergency, what to do.
- **Rhythm:** Highlighted section, perhaps a red or accent border.

## Section 11: Video Answers
- **Intent:** Dr. Sumeet answering common questions directly on camera.
- **Rhythm:** Grid of video thumbnails.

## Section 12: Downloadable Guides
- **Intent:** "New Patient Guide", "Aligner Care PDF".
- **Rhythm:** Card layout with download icons.

## Section 13: "Still have questions?" Banner
- **Intent:** Transition to contact.
- **Rhythm:** Solid color block to break up the text heavy page.

## Section 14: Direct Contact Form
- **Intent:** Allow users to ask a specific question not covered.
- **Rhythm:** Simple, inline form (Name, Email, Question).

## Section 15: Chatbot / Live Chat Prompt
- **Intent:** Immediate assistance.
- **Rhythm:** Floating widget or dedicated section pointing to it.

## Section 16: Clinic Policies
- **Intent:** Strict rules (late arrivals, cancellations).
- **Rhythm:** Muted, smaller text block.

## Section 17: Glossary of Terms
- **Intent:** Explain dental jargon (e.g., Malocclusion, Prophylaxis).
- **Rhythm:** Alphabetical list or simple definition list.

## Section 18: Feedback Request
- **Intent:** "Was this page helpful?"
- **Rhythm:** Tiny thumbs up/down widget.

## Section 19: Final CTA
- **Intent:** "Ready to book your consultation?"
- **Rhythm:** Centered button.

## Section 20: Standard Footer
- **Intent:** Consistent navigation ending.
- **Rhythm:** Inherits global footer.
