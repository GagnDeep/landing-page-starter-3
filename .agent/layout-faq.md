# Layout Blueprint: FAQ Page

This document outlines the structure for the Frequently Asked Questions page, focusing on clarity and ease of navigation.

## 1. Hero: How Can We Help?
- **Intent:** Welcoming and helpful tone.
- **Rhythm:** Minimal header, large search bar if applicable.
- **Elements:** "Frequently Asked Questions," search input (optional).

## 2. Category Jump Links
- **Intent:** Quick navigation for long pages.
- **Rhythm:** Sticky horizontal list or sidebar.
- **Elements:** Links to "Home Service," "Salon Visit," "Pricing," "Academy."

## 3. General Queries Accordion
- **Intent:** Answer the most common questions first.
- **Rhythm:** Standard accordion list, `space-4` between items.
- **Elements:** Questions about booking processes, operating hours.

## 4. Home Service Logistics
- **Intent:** Specific details about the at-home experience.
- **Rhythm:** Accordion list under a clear section heading.
- **Elements:** "Do I need to provide anything?" "Travel radius?"

## 5. Salon / Studio Logistics
- **Intent:** Details for physical visits.
- **Rhythm:** Accordion list.
- **Elements:** Parking, location details, COVID/hygiene protocols.

## 6. Payment & Policies
- **Intent:** Clear rules regarding transactions.
- **Rhythm:** Accordion list.
- **Elements:** Accepted payment methods, cancellation policy, refunds.

## 7. Academy FAQ
- **Intent:** Questions related to courses.
- **Rhythm:** Distinct visual section (perhaps dark mode).
- **Elements:** Prerequisites, certification, installment plans.

## 8. Still Need Help? (Contact Block)
- **Intent:** Catch-all for unanswered questions.
- **Rhythm:** Highlighted box, soft background.
- **Elements:** "Didn't find your answer? Reach out to us." Links to email/phone.

## 9. Live Chat Prompt
- **Intent:** Immediate assistance.
- **Rhythm:** Floating action button (FAB) or bottom right corner block.
- **Elements:** Icon linking to WhatsApp or integrated chat.

## 10. Footer
- **Intent:** Standard navigation ending.
- **Rhythm:** Standard global footer.
- **Elements:** Global links.
