# Dentistree - FAQ Page Layout Blueprint

**Goal:** Answer common patient questions quickly and clearly, reducing administrative phone calls and easing patient anxiety.

1. **Hero Section:** "Frequently Asked Questions". Subtext: "Everything you need to know before your visit."
2. **Search Bar:** Input to quickly search FAQs.
3. **Category Jump Links:** "General", "Pediatric", "Orthodontics", "Billing".
4. **Category: General Information (Header):**
5. **FAQ Item:** Are you accepting new patients?
6. **FAQ Item:** What should I bring to my first appointment?
7. **FAQ Item:** How often should I see the dentist?
8. **Category: Pediatric Care (Header):**
9. **FAQ Item:** When should my child first see a dentist?
10. **FAQ Item:** How do you handle dental anxiety in children?
11. **FAQ Item:** Are dental X-rays safe for kids?
12. **Category: Orthodontics (Header):**
13. **FAQ Item:** What is the best age for braces?
14. **FAQ Item:** Invisalign vs. Traditional Braces: Which is better?
15. **FAQ Item:** How long does orthodontic treatment usually take?
16. **Category: Billing & Insurance (Header):**
17. **FAQ Item:** Do you accept my insurance?
18. **FAQ Item:** What payment methods do you accept?
19. **Still Have Questions? (CTA Section):** Contact info and a button to the contact page.
20. **Global Footer:** Standard footer.
