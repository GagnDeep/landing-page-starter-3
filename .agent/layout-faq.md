# FAQ Page Layout Blueprint

**Goal:** Resolve anxieties and operational questions efficiently.

1. **Hero Section:** "How Can We Help You?" with a prominent search bar.
2. **Quick Links / Categories:** Jump links to General, Treatments, Billing, Emergencies.
3. **General FAQs:** Clinic hours, how to book, parking availability.
4. **Treatment Specific FAQs:** Grouped accordions (e.g., one for Implants, one for Braces).
5. **Pain & Anxiety FAQs:** Specifically addressing dental fear and sedation options.
6. **Billing & Insurance FAQs:** Reiteration of payment queries.
7. **Still Have Questions?:** A contact form embedded directly on the page.
8. **Final CTA:** "Ready to schedule?" -> Booking Link.
