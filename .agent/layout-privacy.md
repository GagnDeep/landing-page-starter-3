# Layout: Privacy Policy Page
**Goal:** Inform users how their personal and medical data is collected, used, and protected.
**Rhythm:** Standard legal format, highly readable text.

1. **Global Header (Sticky)**: Standard navigation.
2. **Hero Section**: Simple title "Privacy Policy". Date of last update.
3. **Table of Contents**: Sticky sidebar or top list of links to jump to sections.
4. **1. Introduction**: Statement of commitment to privacy.
5. **2. Information We Collect**: Personal data (name, contact) and Health data (medical history).
6. **3. How We Collect Information**: Website forms, cookies, in-person registration.
7. **4. Use of Your Information**: To provide treatment, process billing, send reminders.
8. **5. Sharing of Information**: With specialists, insurance, or legal requirements. (Explicit statement: we do not sell data).
9. **6. Data Security**: How patient records are kept safe (encryption, physical security).
10. **7. Patient Rights**: Right to access, modify, or delete records.
11. **8. Cookie Policy**: Explanation of website tracking and analytics.
12. **9. Third-Party Services**: Use of third-party software for booking or payments.
13. **10. Children's Privacy**: Special notes on collecting data for minors.
14. **11. Changes to This Policy**: Statement that the policy may update.
15. **12. Contact for Privacy Concerns**: Designation of a privacy officer or contact point.
16. **Print Button**: Easy way to print the document.
17. **Back to Top Button**: Floating action button.
18. **Newsletter Signup**: Standard footer signup.
19. **Global Footer (Top)**: Standard footer links.
20. **Global Footer (Bottom)**: Legal links.
