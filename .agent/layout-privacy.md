# Privacy Policy Page Layout Blueprint

1. **Global Notice Bar**: Top banner.
2. **Primary Navigation**: Sticky header.
3. **Hero Section**: Clean, simple "Privacy Policy".
4. **Last Updated Date**: Prominent text indicating the document version.
5. **Introduction**: Commitment to protecting patient and visitor privacy.
6. **Information We Collect**: Breakdown of personal data (name, email) vs. technical data (cookies, IP).
7. **Health Information Privacy (HIPAA/Local equivalent)**: Specific section on how sensitive medical data is handled securely.
8. **How We Use Your Information**: Purposes like scheduling, communication, improving the website.
9. **Cookies and Tracking Technologies**: Explanation of analytics, cookies, and how to opt-out.
10. **Information Sharing and Disclosure**: Stating that data is not sold; outlining when it might be shared (e.g., with service providers).
11. **Data Security**: Measures taken to protect data (encryption, secure servers).
12. **Data Retention**: How long information is kept.
13. **Your Rights**: User rights regarding their data (access, correction, deletion).
14. **Children's Privacy**: Specific clause regarding collecting data from minors (crucial for a pediatric clinic).
15. **Third-Party Services**: Privacy practices of embedded tools (e.g., booking widgets, maps).
16. **Changes to This Privacy Policy**: How updates will be communicated.
17. **Opt-Out/Unsubscribe Info**: How to stop receiving marketing emails or communications.
18. **Contact Us About Privacy**: Specific contact detail for the Privacy Officer or management.
19. **Regulatory Compliance**: Mention of adherence to relevant Indian data protection laws.
20. **Global Footer**: Standard footer.
