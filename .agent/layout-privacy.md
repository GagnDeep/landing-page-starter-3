# Layout Blueprint: Privacy Policy

This document outlines the structure for the Privacy Policy page, ensuring transparency regarding user data.

## 1. Header
- **Intent:** Clear identification.
- **Rhythm:** Minimal, typographic header.
- **Elements:** "Privacy Policy," "Last Updated: [Date]."

## 2. Introduction & Scope
- **Intent:** Explain what the policy covers.
- **Rhythm:** Standard text block.
- **Elements:** Commitment to protecting client data.

## 3. Information We Collect
- **Intent:** Detail the types of data gathered.
- **Rhythm:** Categorized lists.
- **Elements:** Personal info (Name, Email, Phone), Booking history, Payment info (handled securely), Usage data (cookies).

## 4. How We Use Your Information
- **Intent:** Explain the purpose of data collection.
- **Rhythm:** Bulleted list.
- **Elements:** To provide services, process payments, send appointment reminders, marketing (with consent).

## 5. Sharing of Information
- **Intent:** Transparency about third parties.
- **Rhythm:** Text section.
- **Elements:** Service providers (e.g., booking software, payment gateways), legal requirements. Assurance that data is not sold.

## 6. Data Security
- **Intent:** Reassure users their data is safe.
- **Rhythm:** Standard text block.
- **Elements:** Measures taken to protect data (encryption, access controls).

## 7. Your Rights & Choices
- **Intent:** Inform users of their control over their data.
- **Rhythm:** Bulleted list.
- **Elements:** Opting out of marketing, requesting data deletion or access.

## 8. Cookies and Tracking Technologies
- **Intent:** Explain digital tracking.
- **Rhythm:** Text section.
- **Elements:** Types of cookies used, how to manage preferences in browsers.

## 9. Changes to This Policy
- **Intent:** Note that the policy may evolve.
- **Rhythm:** Short text block.
- **Elements:** How users will be notified of significant changes.

## 10. Contact Us Regarding Privacy
- **Intent:** Provide a specific channel for privacy concerns.
- **Rhythm:** Contact block.
- **Elements:** Email address designated for privacy inquiries (e.g., privacy@yourhomesalon.in).
