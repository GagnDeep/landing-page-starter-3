# Privacy Page Layout Architecture

## 1. Header (`privacy-header`)
- **Intent:** Clear, legal indication.
- **Rhythm:** Simple, text-only header ("Privacy Policy").

## 2. Last Updated Date (`privacy-date`)
- **Intent:** Legal clarity.
- **Rhythm:** Small, muted text at the top.

## 3. Content Sections (`privacy-content`)
- **Intent:** Present legal text legibly regarding data usage.
- **Rhythm:** Long-form text layout with clear headings (h2, h3).

## 4. Contact for Privacy Inquiries (`privacy-contact`)
- **Intent:** Provide a point of contact.
- **Rhythm:** A short sentence at the end with an email address.
