# Dentistree - Privacy Policy Layout Blueprint

**Goal:** Clearly explain how patient data and website visitor data is collected, used, and protected, emphasizing HIPAA/local healthcare compliance.

## Sections (20+)

1. **Hero Section:** "Privacy Policy".
2. **Last Updated Date:** Text indicating the effective date.
3. **1. Introduction:** Commitment to protecting patient privacy.
4. **2. Information We Collect (Header):**
5. **Personal Information:** Name, email, phone, DOB.
6. **Health Information:** Details collected via forms/appointments.
7. **Technical Information:** Cookies, IP addresses, analytics.
8. **3. How We Use Your Information (Header):**
9. **Use Case:** Providing dental services and appointments.
10. **Use Case:** Processing payments and insurance.
11. **Use Case:** Communication (reminders, newsletters).
12. **Use Case:** Website improvement and analytics.
13. **4. How We Share Your Information:** Details on third parties (e.g., insurance companies, software providers).
14. **5. Data Security:** Explanation of security measures (encryption, secure servers).
15. **6. Your Privacy Rights:** Patient rights to access, correct, or delete data.
16. **7. Children's Privacy:** Specific clause given the pediatric focus (COPPA/local equivalent).
17. **8. Cookies Policy:** Explanation of website tracking.
18. **9. Changes to this Policy:** Right to update.
19. **10. Contact Us:** Specific contact info for privacy concerns.
20. **Global Footer:** Standard footer.
