# Layout Blueprint: Privacy Policy

**Purpose**: Detail data handling and patient privacy practices in compliance with healthcare and web standards. Must be highly readable.

## Sections

1.  **Global Navigation Bar**: Standard sticky header.
2.  **Page Header (Hero)**: Minimalist H1 ("Privacy Policy"). Last updated date.
3.  **Introduction**: Commitment to patient privacy and data security.
4.  **Content Container**: Narrow max-width (`max-w-3xl`) for readability.
5.  **Section: Information We Collect**: Details on personal data (name, email) and protected health information (PHI).
6.  **Section: How We Collect Information**: Website forms, cookies, in-clinic intake.
7.  **Section: Use of Your Information**: For scheduling, billing, and treatment purposes.
8.  **Section: Sharing of Information**: Situations where data is shared (specialist referrals, insurance processing).
9.  **Section: Data Security**: Explanation of measures taken to protect digital and physical records.
10. **Section: Patient Rights**: Rights regarding accessing, amending, or requesting deletion of records.
11. **Section: Cookie Policy**: Specific details on web tracking, analytics, and how to opt out.
12. **Section: Third-Party Services**: Mentions of Google Analytics, scheduling software, etc.
13. **Section: Children's Privacy**: Compliance statement regarding minors' data.
14. **Section: Communications**: Policy on marketing emails/SMS and opt-out procedures.
15. **Section: Data Retention**: How long medical and web records are kept.
16. **Section: Changes to Policy**: Right to update the privacy practices.
17. **Section: Governing Law**: Jurisdiction for privacy disputes.
18. **Contact the Privacy Officer**: Direct contact details for data concerns.
19. **Print Button**: Utility link to print the page.
20. **Global Footer**: Standard footer.
