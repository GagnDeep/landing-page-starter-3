# Layout Blueprint: Privacy Page

1. **Header**: Global navigation.
2. **Hero Section**: Title: "Privacy Policy." Subtitle: "How we protect your data." (Minimalist styling).
3. **Last Updated Date**: Clear timestamp.
4. **Information We Collect**: Breakdown of personal data (forms, cookies) and medical data (if submitted online).
5. **How We Use Your Information**: Explanation of data usage for appointments, communication, and site analytics.
6. **Patient Confidentiality (HIPAA/Local equivalent)**: Strong statement on protecting medical records.
7. **Data Sharing**: Clarification that data is not sold; only shared with necessary partners (e.g., payment processors).
8. **Cookies Policy**: Explanation of tracking technologies used on the site.
9. **User Rights**: How a user can request to view, amend, or delete their non-medical data.
10. **Contact for Privacy Concerns**: Email or address for the Data Protection Officer / Clinic Manager.
11. **Footer**: Global footer.
