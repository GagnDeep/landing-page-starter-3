# Privacy Policy Layout Blueprint

## Section 1: Privacy Header
- **Intent:** Clear, serious title. Last updated date.
- **Rhythm:** Minimalist, centered, no imagery.

## Section 2: Table of Contents
- **Intent:** Easy navigation.
- **Rhythm:** Sticky sidebar on desktop, inline list on mobile.

## Section 3: Introduction & Commitment
- **Intent:** Reassure patients that their medical data is safe.
- **Rhythm:** Large introductory paragraph.

## Section 4: Information We Collect
- **Intent:** Differentiate between website data (cookies) and medical data (forms).
- **Rhythm:** Bulleted list.

## Section 5: How We Collect Information
- **Intent:** Direct forms, cookies, third-party analytics.
- **Rhythm:** Standard paragraph text.

## Section 6: Use of Information
- **Intent:** Why we need it (to book appointments, improve site).
- **Rhythm:** Standard paragraph text.

## Section 7: Medical Information Privacy (HIPAA/Local equivalent)
- **Intent:** Specific section regarding patient health data.
- **Rhythm:** Highlighted box, serious tone.

## Section 8: Sharing of Information
- **Intent:** Who gets the data (Insurance, Aligner labs). Assure we don't sell data.
- **Rhythm:** Standard paragraph text.

## Section 9: Third-Party Service Providers
- **Intent:** Mention hosting, analytics, booking software.
- **Rhythm:** List format.

## Section 10: Data Security Measures
- **Intent:** Explain encryption, secure servers.
- **Rhythm:** Icon + text list for readability.

## Section 11: Data Retention Policy
- **Intent:** How long data is kept.
- **Rhythm:** Standard paragraph text.

## Section 12: User Rights regarding Data
- **Intent:** Right to access, delete, or correct information.
- **Rhythm:** Bulleted list of rights.

## Section 13: Cookies & Tracking Technologies
- **Intent:** Detail the use of cookies.
- **Rhythm:** Standard paragraph text.

## Section 14: Managing Cookies
- **Intent:** Instructions on how users can opt-out.
- **Rhythm:** Step-by-step or link to cookie settings.

## Section 15: Children's Privacy
- **Intent:** Policy regarding collecting data from minors.
- **Rhythm:** Standard paragraph text.

## Section 16: International Data Transfers
- **Intent:** Relevant if servers are hosted abroad.
- **Rhythm:** Standard paragraph text.

## Section 17: Changes to this Policy
- **Intent:** Right to update.
- **Rhythm:** Standard paragraph text.

## Section 18: Contact the Privacy Officer
- **Intent:** Direct contact for data concerns.
- **Rhythm:** Contact block format.

## Section 19: Cookie Consent Re-prompt Link
- **Intent:** Allow users to change their preferences.
- **Rhythm:** Small text button.

## Section 20: Standard Footer
- **Intent:** Consistent navigation ending.
- **Rhythm:** Inherits global footer.
