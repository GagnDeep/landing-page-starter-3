# Layout: Privacy Policy Page

## Structural Intent
To transparently and reassuringly explain how patient data (both medical and digital) is collected, stored, and protected. Crucial for building trust with international patients accustomed to strict privacy laws (like GDPR).

## Visual Rhythm
Similar to the Terms page: minimalist, clean, single-column reading experience. High legibility.

## Sections

1.  **Hero Section:** Title: "Privacy Policy." Subtitle: "Last updated: [Date]." Minimal styling.
2.  **Our Commitment to Privacy:** A reassuring opening statement about the strict confidentiality of medical records and personal data.
3.  **Data Collection:** What information is collected (e.g., contact details, medical history, CBCT scans, website cookies).
4.  **Data Usage:** How the information is used (e.g., treatment planning, appointment reminders, analytics).
5.  **Data Storage & Security:** Explaining the secure digital infrastructure used to protect patient files.
6.  **Third-Party Sharing:** A strict clause stating under what circumstances data might be shared (e.g., with specialized dental labs, insurance companies with patient consent).
7.  **Cookies Policy:** Standard website cookie usage statement.
8.  **Patient Rights:** The user's right to access, correct, or request deletion of their data.
9.  **International Patient Data:** How data for NRI/International patients is handled across borders.
10. **Policy Updates:** Notification that the policy may change.
11. **Privacy Officer Contact:** Direct email for privacy-related concerns.
12. **Footer:** Standard global footer.
