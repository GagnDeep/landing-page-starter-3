# Privacy Policy Layout Blueprint

**Goal:** Patient data protection assurance (HIPAA/local equivalent compliance).

1. **Header:** Simple text header "Privacy Policy".
2. **Last Updated Date:** Prominent display.
3. **Information Collection:** What data is collected (forms, cookies, medical history).
4. **Use of Information:** How data is used (treatment, billing, communication).
5. **Data Protection:** How patient records are secured.
6. **Third-Party Sharing:** Disclosure policies (e.g., sharing with labs or insurance).
7. **User Rights:** How to request access or deletion of personal data.
8. **Contact Info:** Privacy officer contact details.
