# Privacy Page Layout Blueprint

1. **Global Header:** Standard navigation.
2. **Hero Section:** Very simple, text-only. "Privacy Policy".
3. **Last Updated Date:** Clear timestamp.
4. **Introduction:** Commitment to patient privacy.
5. **Table of Contents:** Sticky sidebar for easy navigation.
6. **Section 1: Information We Collect:** Personal, medical, and technical data.
7. **Section 2: How We Collect Information:** Forms, cookies, in-person consultations.
8. **Section 3: Use of Information:** How data is used (treatment plans, marketing, communication).
9. **Section 4: Medical Data Protection:** Specifics on how health records are secured (HIPAA equivalent/local laws).
10. **Section 5: Sharing of Information:** Third-party partners, legal requirements.
11. **Section 6: Cookie Policy:** Details on website tracking.
12. **Section 7: Data Retention:** How long records are kept.
13. **Section 8: Your Rights:** Patient rights to access, modify, or delete data.
14. **Section 9: Security Measures:** Technical and physical safeguards in the clinic.
15. **Section 10: Children's Privacy:** Policy regarding minors.
16. **Section 11: Changes to Privacy Policy:** Update notification process.
17. **Section 12: Contact the Privacy Officer:** Specific contact info for data concerns.
18. **Print Version:** Button to format for printing.
19. **Link to Terms of Service:** Clear cross-linking.
20. **Global Footer:** Standard footer.
21. **Sub-footer:** Copyright.
